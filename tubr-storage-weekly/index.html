Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Tubr Storage closed Week 36 with $3,471 in total sales and 149 units sold, driven by a strong Sunday August 30 peak day that anchored the week's performance. PPC contributed $2,971 in revenue, demonstrating that sponsored campaigns continue to generate meaningful customer acquisition and sales volume. A CVR of 3.01% reflects solid conversion momentum from shoppers who are clicking through to the product.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 89.2% and TACoS at 76.4% are areas we are actively working to bring down through bid refinements and keyword-level pruning across campaigns. Ad CTR of 0.95% has room to grow, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations and headline copy within the ad platform to improve click engagement from targeted audiences. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both efficiency metrics and ad CTR to trend in a more favorable direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,650 in ad spend against $3,471 in total sales, we are in a phase of active campaign calibration to improve our ROAS beyond the current 1.12x. We are proactively identifying underperforming keywords and shifting budget toward placements and match types that are delivering stronger returns, while maintaining the visibility needed to protect our category position. These adjustments are designed to reduce wasted spend and drive more efficient revenue generation in the weeks ahead.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS across all active campaigns, pausing or reducing bids on terms exceeding target thresholds to concentrate spend on higher-converting placements. We will also launch A/B tests on Sponsored Product ad creative, including headline variations and ad copy, to work toward improving ad CTR above the current 0.95%. Finally, we will review dayparting data to determine whether budget allocation can be better aligned to high-conversion windows, building on the strong Sunday performance seen this week.
