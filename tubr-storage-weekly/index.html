Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords with high spend and low conversion to bring ACOS down meaningfully from 121.5%. Budget reallocation toward top-converting campaigns will be prioritized to push ROAS above 1.0x and improve TACoS by end of Week 34.
