Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Tubr Storage closed Week 34 with $4,911 in total sales and 246 units sold, maintaining strong volume heading into the back half of August. PPC drove $3,522 of that revenue, and a CVR of 4.08% confirms that shoppers are converting well once they reach the listing. Thursday August 20 was the peak day of the week, showing healthy mid-week demand.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 66.4% and TACoS at 47.6% reflect an aggressive ad investment that is actively being refined as we work to improve return efficiency. ROAS of 1.51x gives us a clear baseline to build from, and as campaign optimizations take hold we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend downward in the coming week. Ad CTR of 1.09% is an area of active focus, and testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations within the ad platform is underway to drive stronger click volume.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,340 in ad spend against $3,522 in PPC revenue, we are in a phase of deliberate investment to defend category positioning and sustain sales velocity into a competitive period. We are actively adjusting bids on underperforming keywords, shifting budget toward top-converting targets, and reducing spend on placements that are not contributing efficiently to revenue. These moves are designed to bring ROAS upward while protecting the unit volume and ranking momentum we have built.
►
Actions This Week — Conduct a keyword-level bid review to pause or reduce spend on targets with high spend and low conversion, reallocating that budget to proven performers. Test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations, including headline copy and ad imagery, within the ad platform to improve ad CTR above the current 1.09% benchmark. Monitor daily performance against the Thursday peak pattern to ensure budget pacing supports strong mid-week coverage through the remainder of August.
