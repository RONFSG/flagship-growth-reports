Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Tubr Storage moved 173 units and generated $4,018 in total sales during Week 35, with PPC driving $3,374 of that revenue — a strong indicator that paid traffic is converting. Thursday August 27 stood out as the peak sales day, showing that mid-week demand is real and capturable with the right bid strategy in place.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 68.8% and TACoS at 57.8% reflect the current investment level in paid visibility, and both metrics are actively being addressed through campaign-level optimizations this week. CVR is holding at a solid 3.00%, which confirms that shoppers who click are buying — meaning efficiency gains will come from smarter targeting and spend allocation rather than conversion fixes. As we tighten keyword bids and reduce spend on underperforming targets, we expect ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,322 in ad spend against $3,374 in PPC revenue, ROAS came in at 1.45x, and we are actively working to improve that ratio by reallocating budget toward the highest-converting keywords and ad placements. Ad CTR of 0.99% gives us a clear target to build on, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad-level imagery — to improve click quality and bring in more purchase-ready traffic. These adjustments are designed to strengthen efficiency while maintaining the competitive visibility that supports Tubr's category positioning.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit all active campaigns to pause or reduce bids on keywords with high spend and low conversion, redirecting that budget toward proven performers to lift ROAS above 1.45x. Ad creative testing will continue within the ad platform — specifically Sponsored Product headline and creative variations — to push ad CTR meaningfully past the current 0.99% baseline. We will also monitor Thursday performance closely given last week's peak on Aug 27 and ensure bids are positioned to capitalize on any recurring mid-week demand patterns.
