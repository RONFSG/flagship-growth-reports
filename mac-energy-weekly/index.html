►
Weekly Highlights — MAC Energy delivered $53,772 in total sales across Week 34, with PPC revenue of $25,462 driving a strong 3.03x ROAS and an impressive CVR of 20.92% — reflecting highly qualified traffic converting at an exceptional rate. Saturday August 22 was the peak sales day of the week, and TACoS held at 15.6%, indicating healthy overall revenue relative to ad investment.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 33.0% and a 0.45% ad CTR are both active areas of optimization within our campaigns, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations and headline copy to improve click-through performance on paid placements. The -3.2% WoW dip in total sales is a normal week-over-week fluctuation and does not signal a structural shift given the strong underlying CVR of 20.92%. As campaign refinements take effect, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to trend in a more favorable direction this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $8,405 in ad spend against $25,462 in PPC revenue, the 3.03x ROAS reflects a solid foundation, and we are actively adjusting bids and reallocating budget from lower-performing keywords to maximize return on every dollar spent. The 33.0% ACOS presents a clear opportunity to improve efficiency by tightening targeting on broad match terms and concentrating spend on proven converting keywords. These proactive steps are designed to protect our competitive market position while improving margin contribution over the coming weeks.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS data to identify and reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds, redirecting that budget toward high-converting placements that are already delivering strong CVR results. We will also test new Sponsored Product ad creative — including updated ad copy and imagery within the ad platform — to improve the 0.45% ad CTR and drive more qualified clicks into the funnel. Daily monitoring of spend pacing and conversion trends will ensure we respond quickly to any shifts in performance and maintain momentum heading into the back half of August.
