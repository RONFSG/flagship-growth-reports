Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 38 delivered $135,956 in total sales across 4,597 units sold, representing a strong +53.4% week-over-week increase that signals powerful momentum heading into fall. Tuesday, September 15 stood out as the peak day, anchoring a consistently strong week across the board.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 91.2% and TACoS at 37.7% reflect the elevated ad investment fueling this week's significant volume surge, and we are actively working to improve efficiency as spend scales. The 0.27% ad CTR presents a clear opportunity to drive more qualified clicks at a lower cost-per-click, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations and headline copy within the ad platform to improve engagement. As campaign optimizations take hold, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $51,298 in ad spend against a 1.10x ROAS, we are making deliberate adjustments to shift budget toward top-performing campaigns and reduce investment in lower-efficiency placements. This proactive reallocation is designed to protect market position and ranking gains achieved during this high-velocity week while improving return on every dollar spent. We are confident that tightening bid strategies and prioritizing proven converting keywords will strengthen ROAS week over week.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level performance to pause or reduce bids on terms driving spend without proportionate returns, while scaling budgets on converting campaigns to sustain the sales momentum built in Week 38. Sponsored Product ad creative will be tested with refreshed copy and imagery variations within the ad platform to improve ad CTR above the current 0.27% benchmark. We will also set dayparting adjustments around Tuesday performance patterns to concentrate spend during the highest-converting windows.
