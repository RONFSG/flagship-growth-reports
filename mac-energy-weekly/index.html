Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — MAC Energy delivered $68,857 in total sales across 2,301 units in Week 31, with PPC driving $34,009 — nearly half of total revenue. A standout CVR of 24.39% reflects strong purchase intent and highly qualified traffic, and Saturday August 1 emerged as the peak sales day of the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 19.3% and TACoS at 9.5% are areas we are actively optimizing as we refine targeting and shift budget toward the highest-converting ad groups. The 20.4% WoW dip in total sales is being monitored in the context of broader demand patterns and is expected to stabilize as campaign adjustments take effect this week. With CVR holding strong at 24.39%, the fundamentals of the funnel remain healthy and provide a solid foundation for recovery.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $6,565 in ad spend against a 5.18x ROAS, the account is generating meaningful returns, and we are focused on pushing that efficiency even further by reducing spend on lower-performing keywords and reallocating to proven converters. Ad CTR currently sits at 1.42%, and we are testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to improve click quality and volume. These optimizations are designed to protect our market position while improving the ratio of revenue generated per ad dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit current keyword bids to reduce waste on high-spend, low-conversion terms and redirect that budget toward the ad groups contributing most to the $34,009 in PPC revenue. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be prioritized to improve the 1.42% ad CTR and drive more efficient top-of-funnel traffic. We will also monitor daily pacing closely following the Saturday peak to identify any demand trends worth capitalizing on heading into Week 32.
