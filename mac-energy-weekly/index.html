Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — MAC Energy closed Week 44 with $91,137 in total sales and 3,631 units sold, with Friday October 30 standing out as the peak sales day of the week. PPC drove $47,877 in revenue, contributing meaningfully to overall volume and demonstrating continued shopper demand for the brand. CVR held strong at 19.36%, reflecting that customers who engaged with MAC Energy ads converted at a healthy and competitive rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 124.5% and TACoS at 65.4% remain elevated, and we are actively working through campaign-level optimizations to bring these ratios into a more efficient range. The 24.4% WoW sales dip is being reviewed in the context of prior-week performance patterns, and we are adjusting bids and budget allocation accordingly. As spend efficiency improves through these refinements, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $59,600 deployed against $47,877 in PPC revenue, we are actively analyzing campaign performance at the keyword and ad group level to identify where spend can be reallocated for stronger returns. ROAS at 0.80x reflects a period of heavy investment, and we are making targeted adjustments to shift budget toward top-performing segments while pulling back on underperforming placements. These moves are designed to protect MAC Energy's market position while progressively improving the return on every dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a full bid optimization pass across Sponsored Product campaigns, reducing CPCs on keywords with high spend and low conversion contribution while protecting top-converting terms. Ad creative testing within the ad platform — including headline variations and ad copy for Sponsored Brand campaigns — will be prioritized to improve ad CTR above the current 0.20% rate. Budget will be redistributed toward the highest-ROAS ad groups to begin closing the gap between ad spend and PPC revenue week over week.
