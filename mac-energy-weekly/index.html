►
Weekly Highlights — Week 37 delivered $88,620 in total sales across 2,972 units sold, marking a strong +69.2% week-over-week surge with Saturday September 12 emerging as the peak sales day. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 126.2% and TACoS at 67.9% reflect the aggressive spend investment made to fuel this week's significant volume growth, and both metrics are active areas of optimization as we refine campaign targeting and bidding efficiency. ROAS of 0.79x is expected to improve as we shift budget toward higher-converting keywords and reduce spend on terms with lower return. The strong CVR of 18.29% confirms that shopper quality is high, giving us a solid foundation to optimize toward profitability as spend efficiency improves.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $60,130 supported a substantial +69.2% WoW growth sprint, and we are now actively reallocating budget away from underperforming placements to improve overall return. Ad CTR of 0.26% presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline alternatives — to improve click-through rates and lower effective cost per click. These targeted adjustments are designed to strengthen ROAS while maintaining the momentum and market presence built this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a targeted keyword-level bid review, pausing or reducing spend on high-spend, low-conversion terms to bring ACOS and TACoS directionally closer to target thresholds. Ad creative testing across Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand formats will be prioritized to address the 0.26% ad CTR and improve click efficiency without sacrificing impression share. We will also monitor Saturday performance closely, given its status as this week's peak day, to identify and double down on any placement or keyword patterns that drove that conversion spike.
