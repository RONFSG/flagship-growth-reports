Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 40 delivered $149,794 in total sales across 5,084 units sold, representing a strong +44.2% week-over-week increase that signals meaningful momentum for the MAC Energy brand. Thursday, October 1 stood out as the peak sales day, anchoring a week of broad category engagement. CVR of 15.38% reflects a highly qualified audience converting at an impressive rate once they reach the product page.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 148.2% and TACoS at 62.4% reflect the current investment phase of our ad strategy, and we are actively working campaign-by-campaign to improve efficiency as scale builds. ROAS of 0.67x is an area of focused optimization — our team is pulling back on underperforming targets and reallocating toward placements with stronger conversion history. As these adjustments compound over the coming days, we expect measurable improvement in both ROAS and ACOS heading into Week 41.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $93,540 in ad spend driving $63,114 in PPC revenue, we are in an active optimization phase where the priority is improving return per dollar spent while protecting our category position and sales velocity. We are refining keyword bids, pausing low-efficiency targets, and concentrating spend on our highest-converting ad placements to drive stronger ROAS. These proactive adjustments are designed to progressively close the gap between ad spend and PPC revenue while sustaining the growth trajectory demonstrated this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations aimed at improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.23%, including headline copy and ad imagery updates within the ad platform. Bid adjustments will be applied across campaigns to reduce investment in high-spend, low-return keywords while scaling proven performers. We will also set daily spend pacing guardrails to ensure ad budget is being deployed during peak conversion windows identified from the Week 40 day-parting data.
