Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — MAC Energy nearly tripled total sales to $272,432, up +196.6% WoW from $91,845, on 13261 units, with organic sales up +269.5% to $169,093. Thursday, Nov 19 was the peak day at $81,811, followed by $74,424 on Friday. Every core flavor grew: Proper Punch rose to $53,222 (+467.5%), Forbidden Green Apple to $57,300 (+339.4%) with CVR up +49.9% to 22.8%, Orange Creamsicle to $42,807 (+311.1%) at a 35.9% CVR, and the 4-in-1 Variety Pack led at $90,603 (+101.7%).
►
Metrics to Watch — Proper Punch's sessions jumped +1315.2% to 21242, which brought its CVR to 13.4% (-41.5%) as much broader traffic arrived, and the Variety Pack's CVR eased to 26.2% (-12.9%) on sessions up +190.6%. Even so, both SKUs more than doubled sales, and organic now makes up $169,093 of the $272,432 total. We are refining traffic quality on Proper Punch to lift its conversion back up as volume scales.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — TACoS improved to 58.7% from 81.91%, so advertising is now a much smaller share of total sales, and ROAS edged up to 0.65x from 0.61x. Ad spend rose to $160,025 (prior $75,232) and drove $103,338 in PPC revenue (+124.2%) with a strong 22.85% ad conversion rate; ACOS at 154.9% and the 0.21% ad CTR are where we are tightening next, cutting non-converting terms and rebalancing budget toward the best converters.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Shift budget toward Orange Creamsicle (35.9% CVR) and Forbidden Green Apple (22.8% CVR), the strongest converters. (2) Tighten Proper Punch targeting to raise CVR back above 13.4% on its 21242 sessions. (3) Pause non-converting search terms and test new ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.21% and bring ACOS down from 154.9%.
