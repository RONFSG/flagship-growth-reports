Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — MAC Energy posted $56,831 in total sales across 1,900 units sold in Week 33, with PPC driving $31,100 of that revenue at a strong 3.73x ROAS. Conversion rate held exceptionally well at 19.20%, reflecting strong shopper intent and solid product-market fit. Sunday August 9 was the peak sales day, giving the week a strong opening momentum.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 26.8% and TACoS at 14.7% are areas we are actively working to improve as campaigns are refined and spend is allocated more efficiently toward top-performing keywords. The 0.46% ad CTR is an opportunity we are addressing through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing — rotating headline variations and ad copy to improve click engagement. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to trend in a more favorable direction this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $8,334 in ad spend against $31,100 in PPC revenue, the 3.73x ROAS reflects a solid return, and we are focused on pushing that efficiency higher by pulling back on underperforming targets and reallocating budget to proven converters. The competitive energy drink category means maintaining visibility on branded and category terms remains a priority, and we are balancing that market presence with tighter bid discipline. Proactive campaign adjustments this week are designed to protect our ranking position while improving the overall margin contribution from paid traffic.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS to identify and reduce spend on terms exceeding target thresholds, redirecting that budget toward high-converting, lower-cost placements. Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations will be tested to improve the 0.46% ad CTR and drive stronger click volume from existing impressions. We will also monitor daily pacing closely following the Week 33 WoW decline to ensure spend efficiency and sales velocity stabilize and build through the back half of August.
