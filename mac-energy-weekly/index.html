Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — MAC Energy delivered $57,701 in total sales with 1,929 units sold during Week 33, supported by strong PPC revenue of $27,981 and an efficient ROAS of 3.36x. CVR came in at an impressive 17.29%, reflecting strong purchase intent from shoppers reaching our listings. Sunday August 9 was the peak sales day, anchoring a solid week of performance.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 29.8% and TACoS at 14.4% are areas we are actively optimizing as we refine bid strategies and shift budget toward higher-converting placements. The 26.1% WoW decline is expected given the sales cadence shift from the prior week, and our baseline unit velocity and CVR remain healthy. As campaign adjustments take effect, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $8,327 in ad spend driving $27,981 in PPC revenue, the 3.36x ROAS reflects a solid foundation that we are actively building on through ongoing bid and placement optimizations. Ad CTR at 0.45% presents a clear opportunity, and we are initiating Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing — including headline variations and ad imagery — to improve click-through rates directly within the ad platform. These adjustments are designed to increase traffic quality and volume while keeping spend efficient and protecting our category position.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations including headline copy and ad imagery to improve ad CTR from its current 0.45% baseline. Bid adjustments will continue across underperforming targets to bring ACOS closer to our efficiency goals while sustaining the strong 17.29% CVR we have established. We will also monitor daily pacing on ad spend to ensure budget is concentrated on peak conversion windows and highest-performing placements.
