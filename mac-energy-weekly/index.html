Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — MAC Energy delivered $100,734 in total sales for Week 46, up 6.1% from $94,929 the prior week, on 3439 units, with organic sales growing 6.6% to $56,655. Forbidden Green Apple led SKU growth at $14,221 (+15.9%) on units up 17.4%, Orange Creamsicle rose to $11,775 (+12.6%) with conversion up to 29.9%, and the 4-in-1 Variety Pack grew to $48,437 (+5.2%) with conversion at 37.6%. Friday, Nov 13 was the peak day at $16,302.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad spend rose to $75,329 from $37,413 while PPC revenue grew 5.5% to $44,078, so ROAS fell to 0.59x from 1.12x and TACoS climbed to 74.8% from 39.41%. Sessions eased on the Variety Pack (4402, -21.0%), Macberry Lychee (2515, -25.2%) and Orange Creamsicle (1402, -22.9%), but conversion improved on all three (37.6%, 22.1% and 29.9%), keeping Macberry Lychee steady at $15,706 (-0.1%). This week's priority is bringing spend back in line with returns while protecting the 6.6% organic growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $75,329 returned $44,078 in PPC revenue at a 0.59x ROAS and 170.9% ACOS. Ad conversion held healthy at 17.19% of clicks, so the clicks we win are converting; the 0.18% ad CTR shows we are paying for many impressions that do not earn clicks, which is the main efficiency lever to pull. We are tightening bids and budgets to move spend back toward the prior week's $37,413 level, where ROAS was 1.12x.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Scale back bids and budgets on the highest-ACOS campaigns to bring weekly spend toward $37,413 and ROAS back toward 1.12x. (2) Pause non-converting search terms and redirect budget to the Variety Pack and Orange Creamsicle, which are converting at 37.6% and 29.9%. (3) Test new Sponsored Brand and video ad creative to lift the 0.18% ad CTR heading into the holiday season.
