►

These adjustments are designed to strengthen efficiency while maintaining the brand's competitive presence in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will reduce bids on high-spend, low-return keywords and shift budget toward proven converters to improve ROAS and bring ACOS down meaningfully. We will also monitor daily performance closely following Friday's peak to identify momentum and capitalize on any demand carryover into Week 40.
