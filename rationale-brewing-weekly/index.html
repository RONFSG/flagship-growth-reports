►
Weekly Highlights — Week 34 delivered $5,156 in total sales across 309 units, marking a strong +19.2% week-over-week gain with Monday August 17 driving peak volume. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 52.9% and TACoS at 19.9% are areas we are actively working to improve as campaigns mature and spend becomes more targeted. The 0.48% ad CTR is an ongoing optimization focus, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations and headline copy within the ad platform to drive higher click volume from impressions. As these adjustments take effect, we expect both click efficiency and downstream ACOS to trend in a more favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $1,026 deployed against $1,938 in PPC revenue, we are generating real return while operating in a competitive category where maintaining ad presence is essential to sustaining the +19.2% growth momentum we just posted. We are proactively refining keyword bids, reallocating budget toward top-converting terms, and pulling back on inefficient placements to improve ROAS beyond the current 1.89x. These adjustments are designed to strengthen margin contribution while keeping RationAle Brewing competitively positioned heading into the back half of the month.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit current keyword bids to shift budget toward terms driving the strongest CVR and lowest ACOS, and pause or reduce spend on underperforming placements. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will continue, focusing on headline and copy variations to improve the 0.48% ad CTR and generate more efficient click volume. The goal is to build on the Week 34 momentum and push toward a ROAS above 2.0x while keeping TACoS trending downward.
