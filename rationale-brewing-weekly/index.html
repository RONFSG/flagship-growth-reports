Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — RationAle Brewing brought in $4,317 in total sales across 232 units during Week 42, with PPC contributing $1,242 to that revenue. Thursday October 15 stood out as the peak sales day, reflecting solid mid-week momentum. CVR of 11.85% is a strong signal that shoppers who engaged with the brand converted at a healthy rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 89.4% and TACoS at 25.7% reflect the current phase of spend pullback following the post-Prime Day slowdown, and both are being actively monitored as campaigns are recalibrated. Ad CTR of 0.52% presents an opportunity to improve ad creative engagement, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations within the ad platform to lift click volume. As inefficient spend continues to be trimmed and ad creative is refined, we expect both ACOS and CTR to improve in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $1,110 in ad spend generating a 1.12x ROAS, we are in an intentional phase of pulling back on lower-performing campaigns to sharpen efficiency across the board. This approach is consistent with the broader post-slowdown strategy and positions RationAle to re-enter growth mode with a leaner, better-optimized campaign structure. Adjustments are being made to protect market position while improving the ratio of revenue generated per dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue reducing spend on underperforming ad placements while preserving coverage on top-converting keywords that support the 11.85% CVR. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products, including headline and copy variations, will be prioritized to improve ad CTR above the current 0.52% benchmark. Campaign performance will be reviewed mid-week with Thursday as a key checkpoint, given its established pattern as the peak sales day.
