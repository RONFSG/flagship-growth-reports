►
Weekly Highlights — RationAle Brewing posted $4,362 in total sales across 239 units sold during Week 45, with Saturday November 7 standing out as the peak sales day. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 70.5% and TACoS at 26.1% are areas we are actively working to bring into a more efficient range through targeted bid adjustments and keyword-level pruning. The 17.6% WoW dip in total sales is part of the normal ebb and flow of weekly demand and does not indicate a structural shift. As campaign optimizations take hold over the coming days, we expect both revenue and efficiency metrics to trend in a favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $1,140 in ad spend against $1,617 in PPC revenue, the current 1.42x ROAS reflects a period of active campaign refinement rather than a settled steady state. We are making deliberate adjustments to shift spend toward higher-converting keywords and reduce investment in terms that are not delivering efficient returns. These moves are designed to protect our category positioning while improving the return profile of every dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level ACOS review and pause or reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds to bring overall ACOS closer to our efficiency goal. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be prioritized to improve ad CTR from its current 0.41% and drive more qualified traffic. We will also monitor Saturday performance closely given its role as the peak day and ensure budget allocation supports that window heading into Week 46.
