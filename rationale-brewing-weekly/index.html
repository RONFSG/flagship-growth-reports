Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — RationAle grew Total Sales to $4,880, up 13.1% WoW from $4,317, on 289 units. Organic sales led the way at $3,271 (+21.7% WoW), and the Mexican Cerveza 6-Pack was the standout SKU at $1,110 (+171.5%), with units up 180.6% to 87, sessions up 114.5% to 236 and conversion rising to 36.9%. The Hazy IPA ($547, +20.4%) and Kolsch ($403, +22.3%) also grew, and Sunday, Nov 8 was the peak day at $808.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Variety Pack eased to $2,000 (-6.8%) on slightly fewer sessions (313, -1.6%) and a softer 22.4% conversion rate (-3.9%), while the West Coast IPA dipped to $445 (-20.2%) as conversion fell to 31.0% (-21.7%) even though sessions rose 4.5% to 116. PPC revenue held close to flat at $1,609 (-1.2%) as ad spend rose to $1,310 from $1,136. What held up: organic sales climbed 21.7% to $3,271 and carried total sales up 13.1%. We are focused on restoring conversion on the West Coast IPA and Variety Pack to bring both back to growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — TACoS stayed close to last week at 26.9% (prior 26.33%) because total sales grew alongside ad spend, and ad conversion held solid at 17.45% on a 0.38% ad CTR. ROAS eased to 1.23x from 1.43x, with $1,310 of spend returning $1,609 in PPC revenue at an 81.4% ACOS. We are correcting this by trimming bids on high-ACOS search terms and moving that budget toward the best-converting SKUs, aiming to hold TACoS near 26.9% while lifting ROAS back toward 1.43x.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Shift more ad budget to the Mexican Cerveza 6-Pack, which converted at 36.9%, to build on its +171.5% week. (2) Audit search terms and lower bids on high-ACOS terms to bring ACOS down from 81.4% and ROAS back toward 1.43x. (3) Review West Coast IPA ad targeting to recover its 31.0% conversion rate, and test new Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.38% heading into the holiday season.
