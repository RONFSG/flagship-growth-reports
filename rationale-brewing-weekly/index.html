Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — A breakout week: total sales more than doubled to $10,891 (+124.5% WoW from $4,851) on 919 units, led by organic revenue surging +186.1% to $8,455. The Variety Pack 12 Pack climbed to $4,376 (+122.0%) on 225 units (+230.9%), while the Hazy IPA 6 Pack ($1,633, +198.1%), West Coast IPA 6 Pack ($1,259, +182.3%) and Cherry Blossom Japanese Style 6 Pack ($1,078, +189.1%) all nearly tripled. Thursday, Nov 19 was the peak day at $3,349.
►
Metrics to Watch — With every top SKU growing, the main thing to watch is keeping paid efficiency moving in the right direction: ACOS sits at 48.6% and ad CTR at 0.58%, so there is room to get more out of each ad dollar. Conversion is exceptionally strong, with the Hazy IPA 6 Pack at 85.6% and the West Coast IPA 6 Pack at 85.2%, which means traffic is the main lever for more volume. We are focused on driving more sessions to these high-converting SKUs to build on this momentum.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — ROAS jumped to 2.06x from 1.46x and TACoS dropped sharply to 10.9% from 26.78%, as ad spend eased to $1,185 (prior $1,299) while PPC revenue grew to $2,436 (+28.5%). Ads converted at a strong 35.80% with a 0.58% ad CTR and ACOS of 48.6%. Spend is now working much harder, and we will keep reinvesting into the best-converting campaigns.
►
Actions This Week — Increase bids and budget on the Hazy IPA 6 Pack and West Coast IPA 6 Pack, whose 85.6% and 85.2% conversion rates make them the most efficient places to add traffic. Scale campaigns for the Variety Pack 12 Pack to grow its 410 sessions while conversion holds at 54.9%. Test new ad creative to lift the 0.58% ad CTR and trim search terms running above the 48.6% ACOS to push ROAS beyond 2.06x.
