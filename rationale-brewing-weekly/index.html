Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — RationAle Brewing delivered $4,126 in total sales for Week 45 on 239 units, versus $5,000 the prior week (-17.5% WoW). ►
Metrics to Watch — The Variety Pack 12-Pack remains our top seller at $2,046 but eased 21.6% as sessions dipped 5.9% to 318, while the Hazy IPA 6-Pack slipped to $437 (-33.4%) on 19.3% fewer sessions. Conversion is holding up on both (23.6% and 24.5%), so we are focused on rebuilding traffic to these listings to bring organic sales of $2,535 (-14.7% WoW) back up.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend held steady at $1,140 (prior $1,162) and generated $1,591 in PPC revenue for a 1.40x ROAS and 71.6% ACOS, with TACoS at 27.6%. Ad conversion is healthy at 16.74% on a 0.41% ad CTR, so the opportunity is in tightening targeting and bids to lift ROAS back toward the prior week's 1.74x.
►
Actions This Week — We will trim bids on low-converting search terms and shift budget toward the West Coast IPA and Mexican Cerveza 6-Packs, which converted at 39.6% and 30.0%. We will also increase visibility for the Variety Pack and Hazy IPA to recover sessions, and test new Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.41%.
