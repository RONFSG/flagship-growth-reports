Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Addasound recorded $186 in total sales for Week 45 on 8 units, compared with $225 the prior week (-17.6% WoW). PPC revenue grew 277.0% to $194, and the Crystal 2732 Dual-Ear headset rose 20.5% to $59 as its sessions climbed 277.8%. Tuesday, Nov 3 was the peak day at $74.

Metrics to Watch — Sales were concentrated from Mon Nov 2 through Wed Nov 4, with $0 recorded Thu Nov 5 through Sat Nov 7. Newer models like the Crystal SR2731 drew 45 sessions (+95.7%) without a sale yet, so we are focused on turning that rising traffic into orders.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $323 (prior $110) produced $194 in PPC revenue for a 0.60x ROAS, up from 0.47x, at 166.4% ACOS and 173.8% TACoS. Ad CTR held at 0.42% with a 1.36% ad conversion rate, so we will tighten targeting to make every click work harder.
►
Actions This Week — We will cut bids on non-converting search terms and shift budget toward the Epic 512, Crystal 2732 and Epic 302, which each sold this week. We will also test new Sponsored Product ad creative to build on the 0.42% ad CTR and keep the Crystal SR2731 visible as its sessions grow.
