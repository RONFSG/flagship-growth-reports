►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada posted $2,374 in total sales across 80 units sold during Week 45, with PPC driving $1,116 of that revenue. CVR held at a healthy 6.47%, confirming that shoppers landing on the product are converting at a solid rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 67.5% and TACoS at 31.7% remain elevated, and we are actively working through campaign-level optimizations to bring those figures down over the coming weeks. The WoW dip of 14.2% is being monitored in context, and current bid and targeting adjustments are designed to stabilize spend efficiency while protecting sales volume. As these refinements take hold, we expect to see ACOS and TACoS trending in a more favorable direction heading into Week 46.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $753 in ad spend generating a 1.48x ROAS, we are focused on improving return by reallocating budget toward the highest-performing keywords and pausing underperforming targets. Ad CTR at 0.25% presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to improve click engagement and drive more efficient cost-per-click. These proactive adjustments are aimed at strengthening ROAS while maintaining competitive visibility in the Canadian market.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level review to identify and reduce spend on terms contributing to inflated ACOS, redirecting that budget toward proven converters. Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing will be prioritized to lift ad CTR above the current 0.25% baseline. We will also set a daily spend pace that keeps TACoS on a downward trajectory while sustaining the 6.47% CVR that is already working in our favor.
