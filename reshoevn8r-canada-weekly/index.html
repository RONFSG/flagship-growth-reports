Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada delivered a strong Week 31, with total sales reaching $4,950 across 108 units sold — a 13.0% week-over-week increase that signals solid momentum heading into August. PPC drove $3,612 of that revenue at a healthy 4.01x ROAS, and a CVR of 11.97% confirms that shoppers landing on the listing are converting at a strong rate. Monday July 27 stood out as the peak day, anchoring what was an encouraging start to the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 24.9% and TACoS at 18.2% are areas we are actively working to bring tighter as campaign optimizations continue to take shape. Both metrics reflect competitive investment in the Canadian market and are expected to improve as we refine keyword bids and shift budget toward the highest-converting terms. Ad CTR at 0.36% is an opportunity we are addressing through ad creative testing within the Sponsored Products platform to drive stronger click volume from existing impressions.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $901 in ad spend generating $3,612 in PPC revenue, the 4.01x ROAS reflects a productive week while leaving room to sharpen efficiency further. We are proactively reallocating budget away from underperforming placements and concentrating investment in campaigns that are delivering the strongest return. These adjustments are designed to sustain the week-over-week growth trajectory while gradually pulling ACOS and TACoS closer to target ranges.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level bid review, pausing or reducing spend on terms with high ACOS and low conversion contribution to improve overall campaign efficiency. Ad creative variations within Sponsored Products will be tested to work toward improving the 0.36% ad CTR and capturing a greater share of available clicks. We will also monitor daily pacing closely following the strong Monday peak to identify and capitalize on any additional high-traffic windows throughout the week.
