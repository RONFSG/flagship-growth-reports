Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada delivered a strong Week 40, generating $4,306 in total sales across 93 units sold — a healthy 11.5% week-over-week increase that signals continued momentum heading into Q4. Sunday September 27 was the peak sales day, and PPC drove $2,272 of total revenue at a solid 3.35x ROAS, confirming that paid campaigns are actively contributing to growth.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 29.8% and TACoS at 15.7% are areas we are actively working to improve as campaigns mature and spend becomes more targeted. CVR of 8.73% is a strong signal that customers who engage are converting at a healthy rate, which gives us a good foundation to work from as we refine keyword targeting and bid strategies. As we continue shifting budget toward top-performing placements, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more efficiently in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $677 in ad spend generating $2,272 in PPC revenue, the 3.35x ROAS reflects a competitive and productive use of budget in the Canadian market. Ad CTR at 0.19% presents a clear opportunity to drive more efficient top-of-funnel volume, and we are testing ad creative variations within the Sponsored Product ad platform — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve click engagement. Strengthening CTR will lower effective CPCs over time and give us more room to scale spend profitably.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine keyword bids on top-converting search terms to push ACOS closer to our target threshold while protecting revenue share on high-priority placements. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will continue this week with a focus on improving the 0.19% ad CTR through headline and imagery variations. We will also review Sunday performance data from the Week 40 peak to identify audience and placement patterns worth amplifying heading into the October stretch.
