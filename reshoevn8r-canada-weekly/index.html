Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada delivered $3,569 in total sales across 81 units sold during Week 38, with PPC driving $2,760 of that revenue at a solid 3.21x ROAS. Saturday September 19 stood out as the peak sales day, reflecting strong weekend demand for the brand. CVR held at a healthy 9.72%, confirming that shoppers landing on the listing are converting at a competitive rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 31.2% and TACoS at 24.1% are areas we are actively working to bring down through tighter keyword targeting and bid adjustments on underperforming placements. The WoW decline of 15.3% is being monitored in the context of normal weekly demand fluctuation, and our campaign structure is being refined to support a stronger recovery in Week 39. As optimizations take hold, we expect both efficiency metrics to trend in a more favorable direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $860 in ad spend generating $2,760 in PPC revenue, the 3.21x ROAS reflects a functional paid media engine that we are continuing to sharpen. Ad CTR of 0.38% presents a clear opportunity to improve click volume and cost efficiency, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to improve engagement at the impression level. These adjustments are designed to lower effective CPC and improve return on every ad dollar invested while protecting Reshoevn8r's category position in the Canadian marketplace.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords contributing to elevated ACOS while reallocating budget toward top-converting terms that are driving the strongest return. Sponsored Product ad creative testing will be prioritized this week to lift ad CTR above the current 0.38% baseline. We will also analyze Saturday's peak-day performance data to identify which campaigns and keywords drove the strongest conversions and weight budget toward those ahead of the upcoming weekend.
