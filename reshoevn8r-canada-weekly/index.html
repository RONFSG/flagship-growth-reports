Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada delivered $2,283 in total sales for Week 45, up 9.1% from $2,092 the prior week, on 80 units. Organic revenue grew 71.5% to $1,166, with the Refresh Kit up 542.0% on 12 units and the Signature Kit up 134.3% on 9 units. Friday, Nov 6 was the peak day at $575.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Essential Kit eased 25.0% on 15 units with sessions down 14.4%, and the 8oz Cleaning Solution dipped 56.4% even as its sessions grew 46.1%. We are reviewing targeting and ad placements on both so the added traffic converts, and we expect the Essential Kit to regain momentum as we refocus spend behind it.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $753 (prior $759) generated $1,116 in PPC revenue for a 1.48x ROAS and 67.5% ACOS, while TACoS improved to 33.0% from 36.32% as organic sales carried more of the week. Ad conversion held at 6.47% on a 0.25% ad CTR, and we are tightening bids on high-ACOS terms to bring ROAS back toward the prior week's 1.86x.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit search terms to reduce bids on high-spend, low-converting keywords and shift budget toward the Refresh Kit, Signature Kit and Waterproof Shoe Spray, which grew 79.9% on 12 units. We will also test new Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.25%.
