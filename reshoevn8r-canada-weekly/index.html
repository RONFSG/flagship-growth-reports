Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada delivered $5,127 in total sales across 110 units sold during Week 33, with PPC driving $3,102 of that revenue at a strong 3.56x ROAS. CVR held at an impressive 12.45%, reflecting solid buyer intent from shoppers who clicked through to the listing. Tuesday August 11 was the peak sales day, anchoring a steady week of performance across the market.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 28.1% and TACoS at 17.0% are areas we are actively working to tighten as campaign optimizations continue to take shape. The 5.3% WoW dip in total sales is a normal fluctuation we are addressing through refined targeting and bid adjustments this week. As spend becomes more efficient and we sharpen our focus on highest-converting keywords, we expect both ACOS and overall revenue to move in a favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $870 in ad spend generating $3,102 in PPC revenue, the 3.56x ROAS reflects a healthy return that we are working to build on further. Ad CTR at 0.19% indicates room to strengthen Sponsored Product ad creative performance, and we are testing headline and ad copy variations within the ad platform to improve click engagement. These adjustments are designed to drive more qualified traffic at a lower effective cost while maintaining our competitive presence in the Canadian market.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations to lift ad CTR beyond the current 0.19% and bring more high-intent shoppers into the funnel. Bid optimization will focus on reducing spend on underperforming keywords to bring ACOS closer to target while protecting top-converting terms. We will also monitor daily performance closely through the back half of the week to capitalize on any momentum following the Tuesday peak pattern observed in Week 33.
