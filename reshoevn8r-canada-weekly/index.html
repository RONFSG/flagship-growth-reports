Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada grew total sales +31.5% WoW to $3,165 (prior $2,407) on 124 units, led by organic sales more than doubling to $1,899 (+105.6%). The Essential Kit jumped to $432 (+452.0%) on 14 units, the 16oz Cleaning Solution grew to $307 (+202.5%) with CVR at 18.2%, and the Waterproof Shoe Spray reached $464 (+81.9%) on a 21.7% CVR. Thursday, Nov 19 was the peak day at $737.
►
Metrics to Watch — PPC revenue eased to $1,265 (-14.7%), primarily driven by a deliberate pullback in ad spend to $506 from $587, while organic sales more than doubled to $1,899. The Signature Kit, the top SKU at $938 (+11.2%), saw sessions rise +52.0% to 304 while CVR came in at 6.6% (-12.3%), and the 8oz Cleaning Solution held steady at $260 (-0.6%) as sessions eased -17.8%. We are focused on converting the extra Signature Kit traffic into orders next week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — TACoS improved sharply to 16.0% from 24.39%, so advertising now takes a much smaller share of total sales. ROAS held at 2.50x (prior 2.53x) on lower spend of $506, with ACOS at 40.0%, a 0.24% ad CTR and a 9.37% ad conversion rate. This efficiency-driven spend level is letting organic growth carry more of the week.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Shift budget toward the Waterproof Shoe Spray (21.7% CVR) and 16oz Cleaning Solution (18.2% CVR), the strongest converters. (2) Refine Signature Kit targeting to lift CVR back above 6.6% on its 304 sessions. (3) Test new ad creative to raise the 0.24% ad CTR while keeping TACoS near 16.0%.
