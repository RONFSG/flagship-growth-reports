Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada delivered $5,127 in total sales across 110 units sold in Week 33, with PPC driving $3,046 of that revenue at a strong 3.48x ROAS. Tuesday August 11 was the peak sales day, and a CVR of 12.24% confirms that shoppers who clicked through to the listing converted at a healthy rate, reflecting solid product-market fit and strong listing performance in the Canadian market.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 28.7% and TACoS at 17.1% are areas we are actively working to improve as we refine campaign targeting and bid efficiency week over week. The 5.3% WoW dip in total sales is a normal fluctuation and not a structural concern — we are focused on stabilizing volume through smarter spend allocation. As campaign optimizations take effect, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend downward while maintaining strong conversion momentum.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $874 in ad spend generating $3,046 in PPC revenue, the underlying efficiency of our campaigns remains solid, and we are making proactive adjustments to bring ACOS closer to target range. Ad CTR at 0.19% represents an opportunity to improve click volume through sponsored ad creative testing — including headline copy variations and ad imagery within the ad platform — to better capture attention in a competitive category. These adjustments are expected to improve click efficiency and support a stronger revenue-to-spend ratio heading into Week 34.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit underperforming keywords and reallocate budget toward the highest-converting search terms to improve ACOS and protect ROAS above 3x. Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations will be tested this week to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.19% and drive more qualified traffic. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to ensure spend is concentrated around historically stronger mid-week windows, building on Tuesday's peak day performance.
