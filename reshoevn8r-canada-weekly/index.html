Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada posted a strong Week 32, with total sales reaching $5,411 across 116 units sold — a healthy 9.3% week-over-week increase with Monday August 3 driving the peak. PPC contributed $3,215 in revenue at a solid 3.42x ROAS, reflecting continued campaign momentum and strong buyer intent heading into August.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 29.2% and TACoS at 17.4% remain areas we are actively working to bring tighter as campaigns mature and spend becomes more concentrated in top-performing keywords. Ad CTR of 0.29% on Sponsored Product ads is an area where we see room to grow, and we are testing ad creative variations within the platform to improve click engagement. With CVR holding strong at 12.20%, the conversion foundation is already there — improving upstream click quality will compound those results.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $940 in ad spend against $5,411 in total sales, the account is generating meaningful revenue relative to investment, and we are continuing to refine targeting to improve that ratio further. We are pulling back on lower-efficiency placements and reallocating budget toward the keyword segments driving the strongest return, which we expect to put downward pressure on ACOS over the coming weeks. Protecting Reshoevn8r's category position in the Canadian marketplace remains a priority as we optimize, ensuring efficiency gains do not come at the cost of visibility.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords contributing to elevated ACOS without proportional revenue return, redirecting that budget toward the top converters already proven in Week 32 data. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product campaigns will be expanded to identify headline and imagery combinations that improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.29%. We will also monitor Monday performance closely given its role as peak day to ensure campaigns are fully funded and competitive at the start of the week.
