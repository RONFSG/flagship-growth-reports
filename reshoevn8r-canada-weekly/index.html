Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 32 delivered $5,411 in total sales across 116 units sold, marking a strong +9.3% week-over-week increase that signals continued positive momentum for Reshoevn8r Canada. PPC contributed $3,552 in revenue against $939 in ad spend, producing a healthy 3.78x ROAS. Monday August 3 stood out as the peak sales day, anchoring a consistently strong week across the board.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 26.5% and TACoS at 17.4% are areas we are actively working to bring down as campaign optimizations continue to take shape. Ad CTR of 0.30% is an area of ongoing focus, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations within the ad platform to improve click engagement from impressions to visits. With CVR already performing well at 13.39%, strengthening the top of the funnel through better ad creative will compound the results we are already seeing at conversion.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $939 in ad spend driving $3,552 in PPC revenue, the campaign structure is generating real returns while we continue refining where budget is allocated for maximum efficiency. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift spend toward the highest-converting terms and reduce drag from underperforming placements. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position in the Canadian category while improving the overall cost profile week over week.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level spend review to consolidate budget into top-converting terms and reduce inefficient spend pulling ACOS above target. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will be prioritized to improve ad CTR from its current 0.30% and drive stronger click volume from existing impressions. We will also monitor daily pacing closely following the strong Monday peak to ensure budget is distributed effectively across the full week.
