Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r Canada closed Week 44 with $2,092 in total sales and 72 units sold, anchored by a strong peak on Monday, October 26. PPC drove $1,388 of that revenue, demonstrating that sponsored campaigns continue to generate meaningful, trackable returns. A CVR of 7.55% confirms that shoppers landing on the listing are converting at a healthy rate, reflecting strong product-market fit in the Canadian market.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 54.7% and TACoS at 36.3% remain areas of active focus as we continue refining campaign targeting and bid efficiency. The WoW decline of 50.9% is being evaluated in the context of prior-week performance levels, and our optimization work this week is aimed at stabilizing revenue and improving spend ratios. As campaign adjustments take effect, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend in a more favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $760 in ad spend generating a ROAS of 1.83x, we are actively working to improve return by reallocating budget toward the highest-performing keywords and pausing or reducing bids on lower-efficiency terms. Ad CTR at 0.25% presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to improve click-through performance and drive more qualified traffic. These adjustments are designed to protect market position in Canada while improving the overall efficiency of every dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level bid review to reduce spend on high-ACOS terms and shift budget toward converting search terms that are driving the 7.55% CVR. Ad creative testing will be prioritized within the ad platform to lift ad CTR above the current 0.25% baseline through improved Sponsored Product headlines and ad visuals. We will also monitor daily performance closely coming off the Week 44 peak to identify budget pacing opportunities that sustain momentum through the end of the month.
