►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 30.3% and TACoS at 19.7% are areas we are actively working to improve through tighter keyword pruning and bid adjustments on underperforming targets. ►

These optimizations are designed to protect Reshoevn8r's competitive positioning in the Canadian shoe care category while sustaining consistent revenue output.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords exceeding 35% ACOS while scaling budget into proven converters driving the strongest return. Sponsored Product ad creative will be refreshed with new headline and imagery variations tested directly within the ad platform to work toward improving the 0.38% ad CTR. We will also monitor daily pacing closely coming off last week's peak to ensure spend is allocated efficiently as demand patterns normalize through the week.
