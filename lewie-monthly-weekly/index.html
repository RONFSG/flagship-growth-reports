Monthly Insights & Actions
►
Monthly Highlights — September 2026 brought in $1,495 in total sales across 110 units sold, reflecting a 26.7% month-over-month decline that signals an important inflection point for the account. Organic revenue held steady at $748, nearly matching PPC revenue of $746, which shows the catalog has real organic demand to build from. With 62 orders and 17 new-to-brand customers, there is a foundation of buyer acquisition happening that we can scale more efficiently.
►
Metrics to Watch — Current ROAS of 0.48x and ACoS of 209.0% indicate ad spend is outpacing returns, and bringing ACoS below 80% over the next 60 days is the primary efficiency target. TACoS at 104.4% confirms that total business profitability needs tightening, though the 5.92% CVR is a genuine bright spot showing that shoppers who do click are converting at a healthy rate. Protecting and growing that CVR while reducing wasted impressions across 254,155 served will be central to improving these ratios.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — September saw $1,560 in ad spend generate only $746 in PPC revenue, meaning the account spent $2.09 for every $1.00 returned through ads, which is the clearest area to address heading into Q4. The good news is that organic revenue is nearly equal to PPC revenue, meaning organic traction exists and spend does not need to carry the full weight of the business. Tightening bid controls and reallocating budget toward the highest-converting ASINs and keywords will improve the spend-to-sales ratio meaningfully by October close.
►
Actions Next Month — Audit all active campaigns and pause or reduce bids on any keyword with ACoS above 150% and fewer than 3 orders in September, redirecting that budget toward the top 10 converting search terms driving the 5.92% CVR. Launch a targeted Sponsored Brand campaign focused on the 17 new-to-brand customers acquired in September to build repeat purchase behavior and improve total revenue without proportionally increasing spend. Set a hard daily budget cap to keep October ad spend at or below $1,200 while targeting $1,800 in total sales, which would move TACoS toward a more sustainable 67% range.
