Monthly Insights & Actions
►
Monthly Highlights — September 2026 brought in $1,495 in total sales across 110 units sold, reflecting a 26.7% month-over-month decline that signals an important inflection point for Lewie's account. Organic revenue held steady at $748, nearly matching PPC revenue of $746, which shows genuine product-market traction that we can build on. With 17 new-to-brand customers acquired and a CVR of 5.92%, the foundation for growth remains solid.
►
Metrics to Watch — ROAS of 0.48x and ACoS of 209.0% indicate that ad spend is currently outpacing returns, and bringing these into healthier ranges will be the central focus heading into October. TACoS at 104.4% confirms that total revenue needs to scale relative to spend, and even modest sales growth of 20-25% while holding spend flat would meaningfully improve this ratio. Monitoring these three metrics weekly will allow us to course-correct quickly and protect margin as we optimize.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $1,560 exceeded total sales of $1,495, which means current campaigns are being used as a learning and visibility investment rather than a direct profit driver. The 254,155 impressions and 1,047 clicks generated demonstrate strong top-of-funnel reach, and converting more of that traffic into orders will shift the efficiency equation favorably. Reducing wasted spend on low-converting keywords while protecting high-CVR placements will be the priority lever to pull.
►
Actions Next Month — Pause or significantly reduce bids on keywords with zero conversions over the past 30 days and reallocate that budget toward the top 10 converting search terms driving the 5.92% CVR. Launch a targeted Sponsored Brands campaign leveraging the 17 new-to-brand customers as an audience signal to improve ROAS toward a 1.0x target by end of October. Conduct a full search term report audit to identify irrelevant traffic consuming spend, and set a hard daily budget cap to ensure ad spend does not exceed $1,200 next month while sales are being rebuilt.
