Monthly Insights & Actions
►
Monthly Highlights — eptember 2026 brought in $1,495 in total sales across 110 units sold, reflecting a 26.7% month-over-month decline that signals an opportunity to recalibrate our strategy heading into Q4. Organic revenue held steady at $748, matching PPC revenue dollar for dollar, which shows the brand has a meaningful organic foundation to build on. With 17 new-to-brand customers acquired, Lewie is still reaching fresh audiences and expanding its customer base despite the softer sales month.
►
Metrics to Watch — OAS of 0.48x and ACoS of 209.0% indicate that ad spend is currently outpacing the revenue it generates, and bringing these metrics back toward a target ROAS of 1.5x or higher will be the primary focus for October. TACoS at 104.4% confirms that total business health needs attention, but the 5.92% CVR is a genuine bright spot showing that customers who do click are converting at a solid rate. Strengthening bid efficiency and keyword targeting will directly improve these numbers and position the account for a stronger Q4.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — 1,560 in ad spend generated $746 in PPC revenue this month, meaning each dollar spent returned approximately $0.48 in tracked ad sales. The gap between spend and return is significant, but the 5.92% CVR tells us the product page and offer are resonating once traffic arrives, so the fix lies in smarter spend allocation rather than a full strategy overhaul. Redirecting budget toward the highest-converting campaigns and pausing underperformers can meaningfully reduce wasted spend within 30 days.
►
Actions Next Month — onduct a full search term audit to eliminate irrelevant or high-spend, zero-conversion keywords, targeting a 30% reduction in wasted spend to move ACoS toward the 80 to 100% range by end of October. Increase bids on the top 10 converting keywords by 15 to 20% to capture more high-intent traffic and leverage the strong 5.92% CVR more effectively. Launch a Sponsored Display retargeting campaign aimed at the 1,047 September clickers who did not convert, using a modest $150 budget to re-engage warm audiences ahead of the holiday shopping season.
