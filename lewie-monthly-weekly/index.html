Monthly Insights & Actions
►
Monthly Highlights — September 2026 brought in $1,495 in total sales across 110 units sold, reflecting a 26.7% month-over-month decline that signals a need for immediate strategic realignment. Encouragingly, organic revenue held strong at $748, nearly matching PPC revenue of $746, which shows the brand has real organic traction to build on. With 17 new-to-brand customers acquired and a solid CVR of 5.92%, the foundation for recovery is firmly in place.
►
Metrics to Watch — Current ROAS of 0.48x and ACoS of 209.0% indicate ad spend is outpacing returns, presenting a clear opportunity to tighten targeting and improve profitability. TACoS at 104.4% reflects that total business spend relative to total sales needs rebalancing, and bringing this below 30% should be the near-term benchmark. The 5.92% CVR is a genuine strength, confirming that shoppers who find the listings are converting well, meaning traffic quality improvements will directly lift ROAS.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $1,560 in ad spend against $1,495 in total sales, September resulted in a net spend deficit of $65, making spend efficiency the top priority heading into October. The gap between PPC revenue ($746) and ad spend ($1,560) shows that current campaigns are not yet self-sustaining, but the organic revenue match provides a path to profitability with smarter budget allocation. Redirecting spend toward highest-converting keywords and pausing underperformers can meaningfully close this gap within 30 to 60 days.
►
Actions Next Month — Audit all active campaigns and pause any keywords with ACoS above 100% and fewer than 3 orders in the last 30 days to immediately reduce wasted spend and improve overall ROAS toward a target of 1.5x. Increase bids on the top 10 to 15 converting search terms driving organic sales to capture more high-intent traffic at efficient cost. Launch a dedicated Sponsored Brand campaign targeting the 17 new-to-brand customers acquired in September to drive repeat purchase rates and grow lifetime customer value.
