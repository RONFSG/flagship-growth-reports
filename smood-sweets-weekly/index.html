Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Smood Sweets moved 69 units and generated $1,034 in total sales during Week 32, with peak volume landing on Sunday, August 2. PPC contributed $705 in revenue, demonstrating that paid traffic continues to drive meaningful conversion activity. CVR held at a solid 5.85%, confirming that shoppers who engage with the brand are converting at a healthy rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 102.6% and TACoS at 69.9% reflect the current investment level in paid visibility, and both are active areas of campaign optimization this week. As we refine keyword targeting, adjust bids on lower-performing terms, and shift budget toward higher-efficiency placements, we expect these figures to improve. The 5.85% CVR is a strong foundation to build on as we tighten ad spend.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $723 in ad spend against $1,034 in total sales, we are actively working to improve the ratio between investment and return as ROAS currently sits at 0.97x. We are making strategic adjustments to reduce spend on inefficient keywords while protecting visibility on core branded and high-converting terms. These optimizations are designed to improve margin contribution while sustaining the unit velocity and category presence we have built.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit and reduce bids on search terms contributing to high ACOS without meaningful conversion, reallocating that budget toward proven performers. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be explored to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.76% and drive more qualified clicks. We will also monitor daily performance closely to identify any emerging high-conversion windows following the Sunday peak pattern observed in Week 32.
