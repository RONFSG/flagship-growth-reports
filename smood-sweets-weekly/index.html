Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 33 delivered $1,634 in total sales across 109 units sold, marking a strong +58.0% week-over-week surge with Friday August 14 standing out as the peak sales day. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 66.9% and TACoS at 50.3% are areas we are actively working to bring down as campaign structure matures and spend is refined toward higher-performing keywords. The strong +58.0% WoW growth indicates the investment is building real traction, and as we shift budget toward proven converters we expect efficiency to follow. ROAS of 1.50x gives us a clear baseline to beat as optimizations take hold over the coming days.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $822 in ad spend against $1,634 in total sales, we are in an aggressive growth phase where capturing visibility and category presence is the near-term priority, and we are proactively making adjustments to improve that ratio week over week. Ad CTR of 1.03% shows our Sponsored Product creatives are generating engagement, and testing refined ad copy and headline variations within the ad platform will be a key lever for improving click quality and downstream ROAS. We are focused on protecting the sales momentum built this week while gradually improving the efficiency curve.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS data to pause or reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds, reallocating that budget toward the converting search terms that drove Friday's peak performance. Ad creative testing — including Sponsored Product headline and ad copy variations — will be initiated to strengthen ad CTR beyond the current 1.03% and bring in higher-intent traffic. The goal is to carry the +58.0% growth trajectory forward while progressively tightening ACOS toward a more sustainable range.
