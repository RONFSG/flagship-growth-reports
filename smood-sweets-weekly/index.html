Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Smood Sweets closed Week 40 with $1,244 in total sales and 83 units sold, driven by a strong Wednesday September 30 peak day. PPC contributed $507 in revenue, and the 8.06% conversion rate reflects solid shopper intent from those clicking through to the product.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 84.2% and TACoS at 34.3% are areas we are actively optimizing through bid adjustments and campaign refinement. The 1.18% ad CTR is performing within range, and we are testing ad creative variations — including headline copy and imagery within the ad platform — to strengthen click-through performance over the coming weeks. As these optimizations take effect, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend toward more efficient levels.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $427 in ad spend against $507 in PPC revenue, we are making targeted adjustments to shift budget toward the highest-converting placements and keywords. The 1.19x ROAS reflects the current investment in maintaining category visibility and competitive positioning, and we are proactively tightening spend on underperforming targets to improve return. These steps are expected to meaningfully improve efficiency while sustaining sales momentum through the remainder of Q4.
►
Actions This Week — We will reduce bids on keywords with high spend and low conversion to bring ACOS closer to target, while protecting budget for top-performing placements that drove the Wednesday peak. Ad creative testing within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product headline variations — will continue to build on the 1.18% ad CTR. We will also review dayparting opportunities to concentrate spend during peak conversion windows identified from Week 40 data.
