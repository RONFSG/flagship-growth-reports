Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Smood Sweets closed Week 34 with $1,664 in total sales and 111 units sold, marking a +1.8% week-over-week gain that shows steady, positive momentum. PPC revenue contributed $1,289, demonstrating that paid campaigns are driving the majority of top-line growth. Saturday August 22 was the peak sales day, a strong close to the week that reflects solid consumer demand heading into the back half of August.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 88.3% and TACoS at 68.4% reflect the current investment level in building paid visibility, and both are being actively monitored as part of our ongoing campaign optimization. We are focusing on improving bid efficiency and reallocating spend toward the highest-converting placements to bring these figures down progressively. With CVR already performing well at 8.01%, the conversion foundation is strong, and tightening ad spend targeting will allow that strength to flow through to improved efficiency ratios.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $1,138 against $1,289 in PPC revenue reflects a ROAS of 1.13x, and we are actively working to expand that margin through smarter bid management and campaign restructuring. We are prioritizing spend on placements and keywords that are delivering the strongest return while scaling back on lower-efficiency terms, protecting both margin and market position. These adjustments are designed to improve efficiency progressively while maintaining the competitive visibility that is supporting consistent week-over-week growth.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS to identify and reduce bids on underperforming terms, redirecting that budget toward top-converting placements to improve overall ROAS. Ad creative will be tested within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations — to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.93% and drive stronger click volume from existing impressions. We will also analyze Saturday's peak-day performance to identify which campaigns contributed most and apply those learnings to weekend budget allocation going forward.
