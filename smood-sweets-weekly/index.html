►
Weekly Highlights — Week 38 delivered strong momentum with $1,964 in total sales and 131 units sold, marking a +31.0% week-over-week increase that reflects meaningful category traction. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 64.7% and TACoS at 41.5% remain elevated, and we are actively working through campaign-level optimizations to bring those figures down progressively over the coming weeks. ROAS at 1.55x reflects the current investment phase as we build ranking and visibility, and targeted bid adjustments on lower-performing keywords are already underway to improve returns. Ad CTR of 0.87% is an area we are working to strengthen through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing, which we expect to lift click volume and improve overall campaign efficiency.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $814 in ad spend against $1,259 in PPC revenue, we are in an active optimization phase focused on improving the return on every dollar invested in advertising. We are refining keyword targeting, tightening match types, and reallocating budget toward the highest-converting campaigns to steadily improve ROAS while protecting the sales velocity and rank gains achieved this week. These adjustments are designed to improve margin efficiency without sacrificing the competitive positioning we are building in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level audit to pause or reduce bids on underperforming targets that are contributing to elevated ACOS, while protecting spend on top converters that are driving the 12.01% CVR. Ad creative variations within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be tested to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.87% and drive more efficient click-through volume. We will also monitor Tuesday performance closely to determine whether mid-week traffic patterns present a budget allocation opportunity worth scaling into.
