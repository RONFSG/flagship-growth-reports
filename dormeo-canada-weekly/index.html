Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered $33,223 in total sales across 99 units sold in Week 44, driven by a strong Monday Oct 26 peak day that anchored the week's performance. TACoS of 9.0% confirms that paid advertising is working hard relative to total business revenue.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 11.9% and a CVR of 2.26% are both areas we are actively optimizing to improve conversion efficiency as campaigns mature. Ad CTR of 0.46% is an opportunity we are addressing through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing — refining ad copy and headline variations within the ad platform to drive stronger engagement. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both CVR and ad CTR to trend upward in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $2,992 deployed and an 8.42x ROAS, the team is efficiently managing spend while protecting Dormeo's market position in the Canadian sleep category. We are proactively reviewing campaign-level performance to reallocate budget toward the highest-converting keywords and ad groups, ensuring every dollar works harder. These adjustments are expected to sustain strong ROAS while creating incremental room to improve ACOS and overall contribution margins.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a targeted bid review across underperforming ad groups to tighten ACOS toward a sub-11% range while maintaining top-of-search visibility. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will continue, with new headline and copy variations being evaluated to lift the 0.46% ad CTR. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to ensure spend efficiency holds through the remainder of the month.
