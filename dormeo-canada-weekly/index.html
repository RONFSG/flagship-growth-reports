Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered $34,061 in total sales for Week 45, up 3.8% from $32,826 the prior week, on 93 units. The Queen Octaspring Topper led the lift at $17,017 (+29.3%) on 43 units, with conversion climbing to 6.1%. Organic sales grew 26.7% to $7,514, and Thursday, Nov 5 was the peak day at $6,820.
►
Metrics to Watch — The King eased to $6,447 (-6.6%) and the Full to $3,747 (-16.7%) as sessions dipped, while their conversion improved to 2.6% and 2.5%. The Twin XL ($2,278, -33.4%) and Twin ($1,392, -39.9%) softened with conversion at 1.9% and 1.3%, so these smaller sizes are our focus for recovery this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend came down to $2,754 from $2,979 while PPC revenue held at $26,546 (-1.3%), lifting ROAS to 9.64x from 9.03x with ACOS at 10.4%. TACoS improved to 8.1% from 9.08%. Ad CTR was 0.51% with ad conversion at 2.06%.
►
Actions This Week — We will lean further into the Queen Topper campaigns that are converting at 6.1%, and refine targeting and bids on the Twin and Twin XL to lift their 1.3% and 1.9% conversion. We will also test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative to push ad CTR above 0.51% while keeping ACOS near 10.4%.
