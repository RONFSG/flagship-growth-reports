Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered a strong Week 35, generating $71,969 in total sales across 148 units — a solid +3.0% week-over-week gain that signals continued positive momentum heading into fall. PPC drove $45,444 of that revenue at an impressive 9.34x ROAS, confirming that paid campaigns are working efficiently and converting well. Monday August 24 led all days as the peak, setting a strong tone for the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR came in at 0.35% and CVR at 2.36%, both of which represent active optimization opportunities we are working through now. On the CTR side, we are testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to sharpen click-through performance. As those creative tests gather data and we lean into higher-converting segments, we expect both CTR and CVR to improve in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $4,867 in ad spend, TACoS sits at 6.8% and ACOS at 10.7%, both reflecting a well-managed paid strategy relative to the 9.34x ROAS being generated. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward the highest-efficiency terms and reduce waste in lower-converting placements. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position in the category while continuing to drive revenue growth sustainably.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad visual formats within the ad platform — to improve ad CTR from its current 0.35% baseline. Budget will be reallocated toward top-performing keywords and campaigns to further reduce ACOS while sustaining the strong 9.34x ROAS. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to capitalize on any mid-week demand spikes and ensure ad spend is deployed when conversion intent is highest.
