►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR at 0.37% represents an active area of optimization, and we are currently testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations and headline copy within the ad platform to improve click engagement. ►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $3,481 in ad spend generating $48,788 in PPC revenue, ROAS remains exceptional at 14.02x and TACoS of 4.3% reflects a very healthy relationship between ad investment and total business revenue. ACOS at 7.1% is within a manageable range, and we are proactively refining keyword bids and reallocating budget toward the highest-converting placements to further sharpen efficiency while sustaining the growth trajectory we saw this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product ad creative and headline variations within the ad platform to drive improvement in ad CTR beyond the current 0.37%. We will double down on top-converting keywords that contributed to this week's 14.02x ROAS, shifting budget away from lower-efficiency terms to protect TACoS at or below 4.3%. We will also analyze Saturday's peak-day data to identify any targeting or dayparting opportunities that can extend strong weekend performance into Week 37.
