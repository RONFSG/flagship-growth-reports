Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada grew total sales to $71,464 (USD) in Week 47, up 73.9% from $41,099 the prior week, on 254 units. Organic sales rose to $19,786 (+117.6% WoW). The King Topper more than doubled to $14,841 (+133.6%) on 39 units (+178.6%), the Queen Topper led at $29,808 (+44.2%) on 92 units with a 7.3% conversion rate, and the Twin XL jumped to $6,606 (+194.7%). Sunday, Nov 22 was the peak day at $17,578.
►
Metrics to Watch — Sales were softer midweek, at $3,779 on Tue Nov 17 and $3,484 on Wed Nov 18, before building from $11,562 on Thursday to $17,578 on Sunday. Twin XL sessions eased 14.2% to 549, but its conversion rose to 5.1%, so sales still grew. Conversion on the Full (3.3%) and Twin (2.6%) trails the Queen's 7.3%, and ad CTR is 0.44%. We are reviewing budget pacing to carry the late-week momentum through the whole week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Efficiency improved as spend grew. ROAS rose to 9.71x from 7.86x and TACoS fell to 7.4% from 9.91%. Ad spend of $5,319 (prior $4,073) drove PPC revenue of $51,678 (+61.5%) at a 10.3% ACOS and 3.83% ad conversion.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Review daily budget caps and pacing so ads stay fully live Tuesday–Wednesday, aiming to lift midweek days toward Monday's $7,456. (2) Scale spend on the Queen (7.3% CVR) and King (39 units, +178.6%) while holding ACOS near 10.3%. (3) Test new Sponsored Brands ad creative to push ad CTR above 0.44%.
