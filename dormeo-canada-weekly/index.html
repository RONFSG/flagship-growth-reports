Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 40 delivered $57,744 in total sales across 130 units, marking an impressive +11.1% week-over-week growth with Saturday October 3 standing out as the peak sales day. PPC drove $39,081 of that revenue at a strong 10.01x ROAS, confirming that paid campaigns are converting efficiently and contributing meaningfully to overall momentum. TACoS of 6.8% reflects a healthy relationship between ad spend and total business revenue, signaling that organic sales continue to support the channel alongside paid efforts.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 10.0% and ad CTR at 0.40% are both areas we are actively optimizing to push performance further as the account scales into Q4. Ad CTR in particular represents an opportunity — we are testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations, including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform, to improve click engagement and drive more qualified traffic. As these optimizations take effect, we expect both CTR and ACOS to trend in a favorable direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $3,903 in ad spend generating $39,081 in PPC revenue, the campaigns are operating with strong efficiency and delivering clear return on investment heading into the competitive Q4 window. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to ensure spend is concentrated on the highest-converting terms, while making strategic adjustments to protect market position in a category that sees increased advertiser competition this time of year. These refinements are designed to sustain growth momentum while continuously improving the quality of every dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad visuals within the ad platform — to build on the current 0.40% ad CTR and capture more high-intent clicks. Budget will be reallocated toward top-converting keywords and campaigns to maintain the 10.01x ROAS while working to bring ACOS closer to target. We will also monitor daily performance closely given the strong Saturday peak, ensuring bid strategies are positioned to capitalize on any mid-week or weekend demand spikes through Week 41.
