Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered a strong Week 35 with $71,969 in total sales across 148 units, marking a 3.0% week-over-week increase and continuing the positive growth trend. PPC contributed $45,444 in revenue at an impressive 9.34x ROAS, demonstrating that paid campaigns are driving meaningful, efficient returns. Monday August 24 led the week as the peak sales day, setting a strong tone that carried through the remainder of the period.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR at 0.35% and CVR at 2.36% are areas we are actively working to strengthen through ongoing campaign refinements. We are testing ad creative variations within the Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands placements to improve click engagement and push CTR higher over the coming weeks. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both CTR and CVR to build momentum and contribute to further revenue growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $4,867 in ad spend, ACOS sits at 10.7% and TACoS at 6.8%, reflecting a healthy and well-managed investment relative to total revenue. We are continuing to refine keyword targeting and bid strategies to improve efficiency across campaigns while maintaining strong market visibility in a competitive category. These proactive adjustments are designed to sustain the 9.34x ROAS performance and support continued week-over-week growth.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a targeted bid review to reduce spend on underperforming keywords and reallocate budget toward high-converting terms that are driving the strongest ROAS. Ad creative testing across Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be prioritized to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.35% baseline. We will also monitor Monday performance closely given its consistent strength as peak day, ensuring campaigns are fully funded and competitive at the start of the week.
