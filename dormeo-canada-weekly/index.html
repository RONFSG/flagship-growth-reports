Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered $46,278 in total sales across 93 units for Week 37, supported by a standout ROAS of 14.37x and a lean TACoS of 4.3%, reflecting strong revenue generation relative to ad investment. PPC drove $28,634 in revenue against just $1,993 in ad spend, demonstrating highly efficient campaign performance. Sunday September 13 was the peak sales day, capping the week on a strong note.
►
Metrics to Watch — The week-over-week decline of 42.6% reflects a natural post-peak normalization following the elevated sales volume seen in Week 36, and is expected as demand rebalances. CVR at 2.69% and ad CTR at 0.42% are areas we are actively working to strengthen through ad creative refinements within the ad platform, including headline and copy variations on Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns. As these optimizations take effect, we expect both click engagement and conversion rates to trend upward in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $1,993 in ad spend producing $28,634 in PPC revenue, the account is operating with strong capital efficiency and healthy return on every dollar invested. ACOS of 7.0% reflects disciplined spend allocation, and we are continuing to fine-tune bid strategies and keyword targeting to maintain this efficiency as we move into a more competitive fall retail period. Our focus this week is on protecting market position while ensuring ad spend is directed toward the highest-performing placements and terms.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new ad creative variations — including updated Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand headline copy — to improve ad CTR from the current 0.42% and drive stronger top-of-funnel engagement. Bid adjustments will be reviewed across key campaigns to sustain the 14.37x ROAS benchmark and keep TACoS at or below the 4.3% level achieved this week. We will also analyze Sunday's peak-day performance data to identify which campaigns and keywords drove the strongest results and prioritize those placements heading into Week 38.
