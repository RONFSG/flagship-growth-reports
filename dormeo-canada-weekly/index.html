Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered a strong Week 39, with total sales reaching $51,968 on 109 units sold — a 19.0% week-over-week increase that signals genuine momentum heading into the fall season. PPC drove $38,164 of that revenue against just $3,228 in ad spend, producing an impressive 11.82x ROAS and a TACoS of 6.2%, reflecting healthy overall account efficiency. Saturday September 26 was the peak sales day, suggesting strong weekend demand that we can continue to build on.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR came in at 0.36%, which is an area we are actively working to improve through ad creative testing within the Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands placements. CVR of 3.07% is solid and confirms that shoppers who engage are converting at a healthy rate, so lifting CTR further will have a meaningful compounding effect on total PPC revenue. ACOS at 8.5% is performing well in context, and continued bid refinements and keyword pruning this week are expected to sustain or improve that efficiency.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $3,228 in ad spend generating $38,164 in PPC revenue, the account is operating from a position of strong efficiency, and we are proactively optimizing to maintain that as we scale into a competitive fall period. We are reviewing keyword-level performance to reallocate budget toward the highest-converting terms and ensure spend is concentrated where it drives the most incremental growth. These adjustments will help protect our market position and support continued revenue expansion through the remainder of Q3.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new ad creative variations within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.36%, focusing on headline copy and ad imagery at the campaign level. Budget will be strategically shifted toward top-performing keywords and ASINs that drove the strongest CVR during the Week 39 peak, particularly those contributing to Saturday's high-volume day. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to ensure ad coverage remains strong through the upcoming weekend and that we carry this 19.0% growth trend into Week 40.
