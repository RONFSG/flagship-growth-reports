Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered $83,653 in total sales across 179 units for Week 32, backed by a strong ROAS of 10.39x and a healthy TACoS of 6.7%. PPC drove $58,535 in revenue, reflecting solid campaign contribution, and Saturday August 8 stood out as the peak sales day of the week. CVR held at a healthy 2.94%, confirming that shoppers who reached the listings converted at a strong rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — The 9.0% WoW dip in total sales is a normal week-over-week fluctuation and does not reflect any structural issue with the account. ACOS at 9.6% is within a manageable range, and as we continue refining keyword bids and shifting budget toward top-performing ad groups, we expect this figure to tighten further. Ad CTR at 0.42% is an area we are actively working to improve through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing, with stronger creative expected to lift click volume and drive higher conversion throughput.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $5,634 in ad spend generating $58,535 in PPC revenue, the account is running efficiently with ROAS holding above 10x, which is a strong position for the category. We are proactively reviewing campaign-level spend allocation to ensure budget is concentrated on the highest-returning placements and keyword segments. These ongoing adjustments are designed to sustain and improve margin efficiency while maintaining Dormeo's competitive visibility in the Canadian sleep category.
►
Actions This Week — We will test refreshed Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creatives to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.42% and capture more qualified click volume. Budget will be reallocated away from underperforming keywords and toward top-converting ad groups to bring ACOS down from 9.6% while protecting overall revenue momentum. We will also monitor daily pacing closely through the week to capitalize on any demand spikes and ensure Saturday's strong performance pattern is replicated or exceeded.
