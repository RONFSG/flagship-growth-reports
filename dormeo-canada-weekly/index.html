Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo Canada delivered $52,537 in total sales for Week 33, with PPC driving $28,037 of that revenue at a strong 5.06x ROAS. TACoS held at a healthy 10.5% and the team captured 118 units sold, with Sunday August 16 standing out as the peak sales day of the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 19.8% and a CVR of 1.51% are active areas of optimization as we refine keyword targeting and bid strategies across campaigns. Ad CTR of 0.37% reflects room to improve ad creative performance, and we are testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand headline variations to drive higher engagement. As these adjustments take effect, we expect both CVR and ad CTR to trend upward in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $5,541 in ad spend generating $28,037 in PPC revenue, the 5.06x ROAS demonstrates that our campaigns are working efficiently at the core. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward the highest-converting placements and reduce waste on lower-performing terms. These adjustments are designed to protect our category position while improving overall margin contribution week over week.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine keyword bids and pause underperforming ad groups to bring ACOS closer to target while maintaining strong PPC revenue output. Ad creative testing across Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will continue, with a focus on improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.37%. We will also analyze Sunday August 16 peak-day data to identify which campaigns and placements drove the highest conversion volume and replicate those conditions heading into Week 34.
