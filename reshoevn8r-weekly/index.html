Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r closed Week 33 with $56,386 in total sales and 2,062 units sold, driven by strong PPC contribution of $29,524 at a healthy 3.53x ROAS. Sunday August 9 was the peak sales day, setting a strong tone for the week. Conversion rate held impressively at 16.25%, reflecting strong shopper intent and product confidence from customers landing on our ads.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 28.4% and TACoS at 14.8% are areas we are actively working to improve as campaign optimizations continue to take shape. The 8.0% WoW dip in total sales is expected given prior week performance and we are focused on rebuilding week-over-week momentum through refined targeting and budget allocation. As inefficient spend is pulled back and higher-performing campaigns are scaled, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend in a favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $8,371 in ad spend generating $29,524 in PPC revenue, the core ad engine is working well and we are focused on stretching that efficiency further. Ad CTR at 0.50% presents a clear opportunity, and we are actively testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve click-through performance within the ad platform. These creative optimizations are expected to improve traffic quality and bring ACOS closer to target range in the coming weeks.
►
Actions This Week — We will consolidate budget toward top-performing campaigns and keywords driving the strongest ROAS while reducing spend on lower-efficiency placements contributing to elevated ACOS. Ad creative testing across Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand formats will be prioritized this week to improve ad CTR from its current 0.50% baseline. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to protect strong conversion rates and capitalize on any mid-week demand surges.
