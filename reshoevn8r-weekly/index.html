Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r delivered $49,955 in total sales across 1,737 units sold during Week 36, anchored by a strong peak on Sunday, August 30. PPC drove $29,969 in revenue at a solid 3.82x ROAS, and a conversion rate of 17.74% confirms that shoppers are responding well to the brand once they reach the listing. Overall, the business remains in a healthy position as we head into the fall selling season.
►
Metrics to Watch — The 6.7% WoW dip in total sales is being actively monitored as we assess demand patterns heading into September. ACOS at 26.2% and TACoS at 15.7% reflect the current investment level in paid visibility, and our campaign optimizations this week are focused on tightening keyword targeting and shifting budget toward the highest-converting placements to improve these figures. We expect both metrics to trend favorably as those adjustments take hold.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $7,855 in ad spend generating $29,969 in PPC revenue, the 3.82x ROAS reflects a solid return on paid investment, and we are actively working to stretch that efficiency further. Ad CTR at 0.43% presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing ad creative variations — including Sponsored Product imagery and Sponsored Brand headline copy — directly within the ad platform to drive stronger click volume and reduce cost per click. These improvements, combined with ongoing bid optimizations, are expected to bring ACOS closer to target range this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue refining keyword bids and pausing underperforming ad groups to reduce wasted spend and improve both ACOS and TACoS throughout the week. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be prioritized to lift ad CTR above the current 0.43% and drive more efficient traffic. Additionally, we will analyze Sunday's peak-day performance data to identify which products and placements drove the strongest results and scale those strategies into the remainder of the week.
