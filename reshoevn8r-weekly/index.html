Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r closed Week 33 with $55,425 in total sales and 2,025 units sold, anchored by a strong Sunday open on August 9 that set the pace for the week. PPC drove $33,927 in revenue at a solid 4.06x ROAS, reflecting continued strength in our paid channel execution. A CVR of 18.65% confirms that shoppers reaching our listings are converting at a healthy and competitive rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 24.6% and TACoS at 15.1% are areas we are actively working to tighten as campaign optimizations continue to roll through. The -9.1% WoW dip in total sales is a natural settling point we are monitoring as we dial spend toward higher-efficiency placements. As bid adjustments and negative keyword refinements take hold, we expect both efficiency metrics to trend more favorably in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $8,357 in ad spend generating $33,927 in PPC revenue, our paid campaigns are producing meaningful returns and we are focused on improving the efficiency ratio further. We are proactively reallocating budget away from underperforming ad groups and concentrating investment in the placements and match types delivering the strongest return. These adjustments are designed to protect our category visibility and ranking while improving overall margin contribution.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine ad creative testing within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, including headline variations and ad imagery, to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.48% and drive more qualified traffic into the funnel. Budget will be shifted toward top-converting campaigns to continue building on the strong 18.65% CVR while reducing spend in low-efficiency areas pulling ACOS up. We will review Sunday performance data from August 9 to identify audience and placement patterns we can replicate and scale into the back half of the month.
