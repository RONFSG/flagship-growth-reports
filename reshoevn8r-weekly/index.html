Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 31 delivered $58,557 in total sales across 2,106 units sold, marking a strong +34.6% week-over-week surge with Saturday August 1 standing out as the peak day. PPC drove $29,181 in revenue at a solid 3.53x ROAS, reflecting strong ad-driven demand and customer response. CVR of 15.55% confirms that shoppers are converting at a healthy rate once they engage with the brand.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 28.3% and TACoS at 14.1% are areas we are actively optimizing as we scale — both are expected to improve as we tighten keyword targeting and shift budget toward the highest-converting ad groups. Ad CTR of 0.46% is performing within a range where incremental creative testing on Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ads can move the needle further. As these optimizations take hold over the coming week, we expect to see improved efficiency alongside continued revenue growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $8,266 in ad spend generating $29,181 in PPC revenue, the 3.53x ROAS reflects a strong return on investment and confirms that campaigns are driving meaningful incremental sales. We are proactively reallocating budget away from underperforming keywords and toward top-converting targets to further strengthen efficiency. These adjustments are designed to protect our competitive market position while improving margin contribution as volume scales.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creatives to improve ad CTR beyond 0.46% and drive more qualified traffic into the funnel. Budget will be shifted toward the highest-ROAS campaigns and away from keywords contributing to ACOS elevation, with a target of bringing ACOS below 26%. We will also analyze Saturday August 1 peak day data to identify which campaigns, keywords, and ad formats drove the strongest performance and replicate that structure going into Week 32.
