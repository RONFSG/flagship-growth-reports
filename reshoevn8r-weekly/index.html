Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 31 delivered a strong $58,557 in total sales across 2,106 units sold, marking a 34.6% week-over-week increase and signaling powerful momentum heading into August. PPC contributed $29,181 in revenue at a healthy 3.53x ROAS, while a 15.55% CVR confirms that shoppers are arriving with high purchase intent and converting at an exceptional rate. Saturday, August 1st closed out the week as the peak sales day, adding an exclamation point to what was an outstanding performance across the board.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 28.3% and TACoS at 14.1% are areas we are actively refining as campaign structures mature and spend is directed toward the highest-performing placements. CTR of 0.46% on paid ad creatives reflects room to build on, and we are testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations to sharpen click-through performance in the weeks ahead. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to trend in a favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $8,266 in ad spend driving $29,181 in PPC revenue reflects solid returns, and we are proactively evaluating keyword-level efficiency to ensure every dollar is working as hard as possible. Within competitive branded and category terms, maintaining visibility is a deliberate strategy that supports both ranking position and long-term organic growth. We are making targeted adjustments to reduce inefficient spend while protecting the market position that fueled this week's strong growth.
►
Actions This Week — We will reallocate budget away from underperforming keywords and toward the placements and match types that drove peak-day performance on Saturday. Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing will be expanded to work toward improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.46%. We will also set clear ACOS targets by campaign type to systematically bring overall ACOS closer to our efficiency benchmark while sustaining the sales momentum built in Week 31.
