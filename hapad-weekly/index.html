Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered a strong Week 30, generating $17,480 in total sales across 1,105 units sold — a 6.5% week-over-week increase that reflects continued momentum heading into late July. PPC drove $9,551 in revenue at a solid 2.94x ROAS, and a 16.82% conversion rate confirms that shoppers are landing on listings and buying with confidence. Monday July 20 stood out as the peak day, anchoring a consistently productive week across the board.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 34.0% and TACoS at 18.6% are areas we are actively working to improve as campaigns mature and spend becomes more targeted. We are identifying lower-efficiency keywords and reallocating budget toward placements with stronger return, which we expect to bring ACOS down incrementally over the coming weeks. CTR at 0.55% also presents a clear opportunity — creative and copy testing underway is aimed at improving click-through and compounding the already-strong 16.82% CVR.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $3,244 in ad spend against $17,480 in total sales, we are maintaining a competitive presence while actively refining where and how that budget is deployed. Campaigns are being reviewed to reduce waste on broad or low-converting terms while protecting visibility on high-intent, high-converting placements that sustain our category position. These optimizations are designed to improve margin contribution without sacrificing the growth trajectory we have built over recent weeks.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords exceeding a 40% ACOS threshold and redirect that spend toward top-performing exact-match terms driving the strongest CVR. A/B testing of main image and title copy will be initiated to target a CTR improvement toward the 0.65–0.70% range. We will also monitor daily performance closely following the strong Monday peak to identify and capitalize on any recurring traffic patterns through the remainder of Week 31.
