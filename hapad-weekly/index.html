Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered $15,911 in total sales across 1,044 units sold in Week 31, with PPC driving $9,551 of that revenue at a strong 5.89x ROAS. TACoS held at 10.2% on $1,621 in ad spend, reflecting disciplined investment relative to total revenue. Friday July 31 was the peak sales day, showing healthy mid-week momentum building into the weekend.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 17.0% and a 0.65% ad CTR are both active areas of optimization within our campaign structure. We are refining keyword targeting and adjusting bids on lower-performing ad groups to bring ACOS closer to target range over the coming weeks. CVR remains a standout at 19.52%, confirming that traffic reaching the listing is converting at a strong rate and providing a solid foundation as we work to improve top-of-funnel efficiency.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $1,621 in ad spend generating $9,551 in PPC revenue, our 5.89x ROAS reflects healthy returns on every dollar invested in advertising. We are proactively reviewing campaign-level performance to reallocate budget toward the highest-converting placements and ad creative variations, ensuring spend continues working efficiently. This approach positions us to protect category visibility and sustain revenue momentum as we move through the back half of the summer period.
►
Actions This Week — We will test revised Sponsored Product ad creative including updated headlines and ad copy variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.65% baseline. Bid adjustments will be applied to keywords contributing to elevated ACOS, with a target of pulling that figure below 15% over the next two to three weeks. We will also analyze Friday peak-day patterns to identify opportunities to increase budget delivery heading into weekends and capture demand at its highest point.
