Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered $17,337 in total sales across 1,070 units sold during Week 36, with PPC driving $7,424 in revenue at a strong 3.59x ROAS. Conversion rate came in at an impressive 20.56%, reflecting strong shopper intent and product-market fit. Thursday, September 3 was the peak sales day, anchoring a steady week with only a marginal -0.4% WoW shift.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 27.9% and TACoS at 11.9% are within an actively managed range, and we are refining keyword bids and match types to improve efficiency as campaigns mature. Ad CTR at 0.60% reflects an opportunity to strengthen ad creative performance within the ad platform, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations to drive higher engagement. These optimizations are expected to compound positively over the coming weeks as spend becomes more targeted.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,068 in ad spend generating $7,424 in PPC revenue, the 3.59x ROAS confirms that paid campaigns are producing meaningful returns and supporting overall sales volume. We are proactively reviewing campaign structures to shift budget toward top-performing ad groups and reduce waste in lower-efficiency placements. This reallocation will help protect margin while sustaining Hapad's visibility and competitive positioning in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will tighten bids on underperforming keywords and reallocate that budget toward high-converting search terms to push ACOS closer to target. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands will be prioritized to improve ad CTR from its current 0.60% baseline. We will also monitor Thursday performance closely given last week's peak, ensuring budget remains available mid-to-late week to capture demand at its highest point.
