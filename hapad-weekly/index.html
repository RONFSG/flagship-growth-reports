Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered $16,416 in total sales across 1,095 units sold in Week 33, with PPC driving $7,424 of that revenue at a strong 3.70x ROAS. Conversion rate held exceptionally well at 22.49%, reflecting strong buyer intent and product-market fit. Monday August 10 was the peak sales day, anchoring a solid week of performance.
►
Metrics to Watch — The week-over-week dip of 3.6% is modest and within normal range as we move through mid-August. ACOS at 27.0% and TACoS at 12.2% are areas we are actively optimizing by refining keyword bids and reallocating spend toward top-converting terms. As those campaign adjustments take effect, we expect both metrics to tighten and overall efficiency to improve heading into Week 34.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $2,006 in ad spend generating $7,424 in PPC revenue, the 3.70x ROAS reflects a healthy return on investment that we are working to extend further. Ad CTR at 0.70% represents an opportunity we are addressing through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing — including headline variations and ad copy refinements within the ad platform — to drive higher click volume from qualified shoppers. These proactive adjustments are designed to improve traffic quality and protect our competitive position in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue bid optimization on high-spend, lower-converting keywords to bring ACOS closer to target while maintaining category visibility. Ad creative testing will focus on Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand headline and copy variations to improve ad CTR above the current 0.70% benchmark. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to ensure ad spend is distributed efficiently across the week and capitalize on any demand spikes beyond Monday.
