Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered a strong Week 34 with $17,334 in total sales and 1,053 units sold, marking a +5.6% week-over-week gain that signals steady, healthy momentum. PPC drove $9,551 in revenue at a compelling 4.68x ROAS, and our CVR of 22.04% reflects strong purchase intent from shoppers reaching our listings. Thursday, August 20 stood out as the peak day, suggesting mid-week demand patterns worth building around in upcoming campaigns.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 21.4% remains an active area of optimization, and we are working to bring it closer to target through tighter keyword bid management and spend reallocation toward top-converting terms. Ad CTR of 0.66% is an area where we see room to grow, and we are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve click engagement week over week. TACoS of 11.8% reflects a healthy relationship between ad spend and total revenue, and continued efficiency gains on the PPC side will push that figure even lower.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $2,040 in ad spend generating $9,551 in PPC revenue, the account is operating with strong return, and we are proactively refining campaign structure to extend that efficiency further. We are identifying lower-performing placements and keywords to reallocate budget toward the segments driving the highest ROAS, protecting our competitive positioning while improving margin contribution. These adjustments are designed to sustain our growth trajectory and strengthen spend efficiency heading into the back half of August.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level bid review across Sponsored Product campaigns, pulling back on terms with elevated ACOS and scaling spend into the highest-converting, lowest-cost placements. Ad creative testing will be prioritized to improve ad CTR from its current 0.66%, with new headline variations and ad imagery being introduced within the ad platform this week. We will also analyze Thursday's peak-day data to inform dayparting and budget pacing decisions, ensuring spend is concentrated during the highest-converting windows.
