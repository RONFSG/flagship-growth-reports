Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered $16,463 in total sales across 1,098 units sold during Week 33, supported by strong PPC revenue of $9,551 at a 4.76x ROAS. Conversion rate came in at an impressive 21.85%, reflecting strong buyer intent and product-market fit. Monday August 10 was the peak sales day, showing a healthy start to the week that anchored overall performance.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 21.0% and TACoS at 12.2% are within a manageable range and represent an active area of optimization as we refine targeting and bid efficiency across campaigns. The 3.4% WoW dip in revenue is being monitored closely as we assess demand patterns and adjust spend allocation accordingly. As campaign optimizations take effect, we expect both ACOS and overall revenue trend to stabilize and improve in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $2,006 in ad spend generating $9,551 in PPC revenue at 4.76x ROAS, the campaigns are delivering solid returns and demonstrating efficient use of budget. Ad CTR sits at 0.70%, and we are actively testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including ad copy and imagery within the ad platform — to strengthen click-through performance and capture a greater share of high-intent traffic. These adjustments are designed to improve efficiency while maintaining competitive visibility and sustaining our sales momentum.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue refining keyword bids and reallocating budget toward top-converting placements to bring ACOS closer to target while protecting revenue volume. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product campaigns will be prioritized this week to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.70% baseline. We will also closely track daily sales pacing to identify any mid-week opportunities to scale spend on high-performing ad groups and build on Monday's strong performance.
