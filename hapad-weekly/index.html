Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered a strong Week 35 with $17,425 in total sales and 1,080 units sold, reflecting a positive +0.5% week-over-week gain and continued momentum heading into late August. PPC contributed $7,424 in revenue at a healthy 3.66x ROAS, demonstrating that paid campaigns are driving meaningful, efficient returns. Monday August 24 stood out as the peak sales day, signaling strong early-week demand that we can continue to build on.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 27.3% and TACoS at 11.6% are areas we are actively refining as part of ongoing campaign optimization. We are reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward the highest-converting terms and reduce spend on lower-efficiency placements, which we expect to bring ACOS closer to target in the coming week. Ad CTR at 0.66% is an opportunity we are addressing through ad creative testing within the ad platform, including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations to improve click quality.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $2,029 in ad spend generating $7,424 in PPC revenue, the campaign structure is producing solid returns, and we are focused on building on that efficiency through continued bid and targeting refinements. We are proactively adjusting spend allocation to protect strong-performing placements while pulling back on segments where efficiency can be improved. These adjustments are designed to sustain our current sales velocity and market position while improving overall margin contribution week over week.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level bid review to reallocate budget from underperforming terms toward high-converting, high-ROAS placements with the goal of reducing ACOS while maintaining revenue volume. We will test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations within the ad platform to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.66% and drive stronger click-to-conversion flow. We will also monitor daily sales patterns closely given Monday's peak performance, ensuring campaigns are paced to capture early-week demand again in Week 36.
