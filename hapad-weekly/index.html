Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered a solid Week 35 with $17,415 in total sales and 1,080 units sold, holding steady with a +0.5% week-over-week gain. PPC drove $7,424 in revenue at a strong 3.66x ROAS, and a 21.38% conversion rate confirms that shoppers are arriving with high purchase intent. Monday August 24 was the peak day, providing a useful anchor for scheduling future campaign boosts.
►
Metrics to Watch — TACoS at 11.7% and ACOS at 27.3% are within a manageable range and reflect the current investment in maintaining ad visibility and category presence. As we continue refining keyword bids, pausing underperforming targets, and shifting budget toward top-converting placements, both metrics are expected to trend more efficiently in the coming week. The strong CVR of 21.38% confirms the demand is there — the focus now is on capturing it more cost-effectively.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $2,029 in ad spend generated $7,424 in PPC revenue, reflecting a healthy return on every dollar deployed in campaigns. Ad CTR at 0.66% presents an active opportunity to improve click volume, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to lift engagement from existing impressions. These adjustments are expected to improve click efficiency while protecting our competitive position within the category.
►
Actions This Week — Shift budget toward the highest-converting ad groups and placements that contributed most to Monday's peak performance, reinforcing what is already working. Test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations to improve ad CTR from its current 0.66%, focusing on messaging that speaks directly to purchase intent. Continue bid optimization across keywords to gradually bring ACOS below 25% while sustaining the sales momentum built in Week 35.
