Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 33 delivered $17,333 in total sales across 958 units sold, marking a solid +1.8% week-over-week gain and continuing Hapad's positive growth trajectory. PPC drove $9,551 in revenue at an impressive 8.74x ROAS, reflecting strong campaign efficiency and buyer intent. Monday August 10 stood out as the peak day, signaling healthy early-week demand that can be leveraged going forward.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 11.4% and TACoS at 6.3% are within a manageable range and reflect the natural balance between paid and organic contribution at this sales volume. Ad CTR at 0.67% is an area we are actively working to improve through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing to drive higher engagement from impressions. As these optimizations take effect, we expect CTR gains to compound into stronger conversion volume and lower cost per acquisition over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $1,093 in ad spend generating $9,551 in PPC revenue represents a highly efficient deployment of budget, and we are continuing to fine-tune targeting and bid strategies to sustain this performance. With CVR holding strong at 20.26%, the conversion foundation is solid, and incremental improvements on the traffic quality side will only strengthen overall returns. We are proactively monitoring keyword-level efficiency to protect margin while maintaining competitive positioning in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.67% and drive more qualified clicks into the funnel. Bid optimization will continue on high-spend, lower-efficiency keywords to bring ACOS closer to target while protecting top-performing campaigns. We will also analyze Monday's peak-day performance to identify demand patterns that can inform budget scheduling and dayparting adjustments for Week 34.
