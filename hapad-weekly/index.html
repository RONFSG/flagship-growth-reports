Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered a strong Week 30 with $17,472 in total sales across 1,102 units sold, marking a solid +6.4% week-over-week gain. PPC revenue contributed $9,551, reflecting healthy ad-driven demand, while a 16.82% conversion rate confirms strong shopper intent and product-market fit. Monday July 20 was the peak sales day, anchoring what was an overall positive and consistent week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 34.0% and TACoS at 18.6% are areas we are actively working to improve through bid refinements and keyword-level optimizations across campaigns. Ad CTR of 0.55% reflects room to grow, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations and headline copy within the ad platform to drive higher click engagement. As these adjustments take hold, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $3,244 in ad spend against a 2.94x ROAS, the campaigns are generating meaningful return while we continue fine-tuning for greater efficiency. We are proactively reviewing keyword targeting and bid strategies to reduce spend on lower-performing terms and redirect budget toward what is driving the strongest return. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position and sustain the growth momentum reflected in this week's +6.4% WoW increase.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine bids on underperforming keywords and shift budget toward top-converting targets to push ROAS above 3.0x and bring ACOS closer to the 30% range. Ad creative testing will continue within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns, with a focus on headline variations aimed at lifting the current 0.55% ad CTR. We will also monitor Monday performance closely given it was the peak day last week, ensuring budget is well-positioned to capture early-week demand.
