►

►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR at 0.69% is an area we are actively working to strengthen through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing, including headline variations and ad imagery within the ad platform. ACOS at 20.9% reflects the current investment in visibility and is being monitored closely as campaign optimizations take hold. As we refine targeting and shift budget toward higher-performing ad groups, we expect both CTR and ACOS to improve in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $2,000 in ad spend generating $9,551 in PPC revenue, the 4.78x ROAS demonstrates that our paid strategy is working efficiently to capture demand. TACoS at 11.5% reflects a balanced investment relative to total sales, and we are continuing to fine-tune keyword bids and match types to protect that efficiency while sustaining our category position. These proactive adjustments are designed to maintain growth momentum while improving the overall return on every dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including updated headlines and ad-level imagery — to build on the strong CVR and push ad CTR above 0.69%. Budget allocation will be reviewed to shift spend toward top-converting keywords and reduce waste in lower-performing segments, targeting a measurable improvement in ACOS. We will also analyze Monday's peak-day performance patterns to identify opportunities to front-load bidding early in the week and capture demand at its highest point.
