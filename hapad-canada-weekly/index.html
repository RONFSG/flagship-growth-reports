Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad Canada closed Week 37 with $89 in total sales across 6 units sold, with Sunday September 6th standing out as the peak sales day of the week. PPC contributed $29 in attributed revenue, and the team continues to build visibility in the Canadian market as campaigns run and gather data. WoW performance reflects normal weekly variability as we refine our targeting and bidding strategy.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 441.3% and TACoS at 143.5% reflect a phase where ad spend is outpacing current revenue, which is expected as we work through campaign optimization and identify the highest-performing keywords. Ad CTR of 0.41% and CVR of 1.94% give us clear levers to pull — improving ad creative relevance will drive more qualified clicks and strengthen conversion efficiency. As we continue refining keyword bids and pausing low-performers, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to improve meaningfully in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $128 in ad spend against $89 in total sales, we are in an active optimization phase where the focus is on tightening spend allocation toward the keywords and placements delivering the strongest returns. We are proactively reducing budget on underperforming targets while protecting visibility on terms that support brand presence and category ranking in Canada. These adjustments are designed to progressively improve ROAS from its current 0.23x baseline as campaign learnings compound.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords with zero conversions over the past two weeks and redirect that budget toward the top-converting search terms driving the 6 units sold. Ad creative will be reviewed and tested within the Sponsored Products campaign — including headline copy and ad messaging variations — to improve ad CTR above the current 0.41% baseline. We will also set tighter daily spend caps to ensure budget is concentrated during peak conversion windows, with Sunday as a confirmed high-performance day to prioritize.
