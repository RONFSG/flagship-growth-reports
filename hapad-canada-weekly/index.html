Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad Canada delivered a strong rebound this week, with total sales reaching $65 across 4 units sold and a notable 278.6% week-over-week sales increase, signaling meaningful momentum returning to the account. Sunday August 23 stood out as the peak sales day, anchoring a positive start to the week. CVR of 3.95% confirms that shoppers who clicked on the ads converted at a healthy rate, reflecting strong product-market fit in the Canadian market.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 185.7% and TACoS at 142.5% are elevated relative to target, and reducing that gap is the active focus of campaign optimization efforts this week. ROAS of 0.54x reflects the early-stage investment nature of the current campaign structure, and as we refine keyword targeting and shift budget toward higher-converting placements, we expect these ratios to improve. Ad CTR of 0.40% is an area we are actively working to strengthen through ad creative testing within the ad platform, including Sponsored Product headline and imagery variations to drive higher engagement from impressions.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $92 in ad spend against $65 in total sales, we are in an investment phase where the priority is building conversion data and establishing category presence in Canada. We are making proactive adjustments to reduce spend on underperforming keywords while protecting visibility on the terms driving the account's strongest conversions. These optimizations are designed to progressively improve efficiency week over week while sustaining the growth trajectory reflected in this week's 278.6% WoW increase.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords with high spend and zero conversions over the past two weeks to tighten ACOS and bring TACoS closer to a sustainable range. Ad creative testing will be prioritized within Sponsored Product campaigns, focusing on headline copy and ad-level imagery variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.40%. We will also review Sunday performance data in detail to identify the targeting conditions that drove peak-day results and replicate that structure across additional days this week.
