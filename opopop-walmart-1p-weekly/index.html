Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Performance — Opopop generated $11,948.00 in PPC revenue on $4,612.03 in ad spend this week, delivering a 2.59x ROAS across 1,740 orders. Spend increased 44.0% week-over-week from $3,202.45, however ROAS declined from 3.24x to 2.59x, indicating the incremental spend did not scale efficiently. Impressions reached 3,711,584 with 7,673 clicks, reflecting solid top-of-funnel volume but warranting a closer look at spend allocation by campaign.
►
MTD Pacing — The week's $4,612.03 in spend represents a notable step-up in investment, and if this run rate continues the monthly spend trajectory will need to be monitored closely against budget thresholds. The current 2.59x ROAS is trailing last week's 3.24x benchmark, suggesting mid-month pacing may come in below the target efficiency threshold if the trend is not corrected. Prioritizing higher-converting campaigns for remaining MTD budget will be critical to defending overall monthly ROAS.
►
Ad Efficiency — ACoS of 38.6% is elevated and above an efficient target range of 25-30% for Walmart 1P, signaling that some spend is likely going toward lower-converting or over-bid placements. CPC of $0.60 is reasonable, and a CVR of 22.7% is a strong conversion signal indicating that landing page and item content are performing well. The disconnect between solid CVR and high ACoS points to bid levels or keyword targeting breadth as the primary lever to address rather than product page quality.
►
Actions This Week — Audit the top 20% of keywords by spend and reduce bids by 15-20% on any term delivering ACoS above 50%, prioritizing Sponsored Products campaigns where the ROAS decline was most pronounced. Reallocate the savings toward high-CVR search terms and item page placements that demonstrated efficiency during the prior week at 3.24x ROAS. Additionally, review campaign-level budget caps on Walmart Connect to ensure daily limits are not causing spend to shift to lower-priority campaigns during peak traffic windows
