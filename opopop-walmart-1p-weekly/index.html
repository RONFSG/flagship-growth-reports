Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Performance — Opopop generated $11,948.00 in PPC revenue on $4,612.03 in ad spend this week, delivering a 2.59x ROAS across 1,740 orders. Spend increased 44.1% week over week from $3,202.45, however ROAS declined from 3.24x to 2.59x, indicating the additional spend did not scale efficiently. Impressions reached 3,711,584 with 7,673 clicks, reflecting strong top-of-funnel volume but softer revenue returns relative to investment.
►
MTD Pacing — The week's $4,612.03 in spend represents a meaningful acceleration in budget deployment, and if this run rate holds the monthly spend will finish significantly higher than prior periods. The ROAS compression from 3.24x to 2.59x MTD warrants attention to ensure the monthly blended ROAS does not fall below the 3.0x threshold. Budget pacing should be monitored daily this week to avoid overspend in lower-performing placements that are diluting overall returns.
►
Ad Efficiency — ACoS came in at 38.6%, which is elevated and suggests some keyword or item-level waste that should be addressed before spend scales further. CPC of $0.60 remains efficient and is a positive signal that bid discipline is largely intact across the campaign structure. CVR of 22.7% is a strong conversion rate and indicates that shoppers who click are highly purchase-intent, so the priority should be tightening targeting to protect that quality audience and reduce spend on lower-converting segments.
►
Actions This Week — Pull a search term report and identify keywords with ACoS above 55% and zero or one orders, then apply negative matches or reduce bids by 20-25% to recover efficiency. Reallocate the saved budget toward the top 10 converting items driving the 22.7% CVR, increasing bids on Sponsored Product exact match terms for those SKUs to defend share. Submit a Walmart Connect optimized bid request for top placements on the popcorn and snack category pages to test whether higher-funnel inventory can maintain CVR while improving ROAS back toward the 3.0x target.
