Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Opopop delivered 1,387 orders and $9,384.61 in PPC revenue during WK38, sustaining strong volume on Walmart 1P with a 21.5% CVR that reflects highly qualified traffic reaching the sponsored placements. Impressions topped 6.7 million across the week, demonstrating solid shelf presence heading into the fall season. While revenue stepped back week-over-week, the underlying click and conversion fundamentals remain a strong foundation to build on.
►
MTD Pacing — September MTD PPC spend stands at $12,078.83 against $21,025.59 in PPC sales, reflecting a blended ROAS of 1.74x through the first portion of the month. The account is tracking at a healthy spend-to-sales ratio and maintaining positive momentum with roughly two weeks remaining in the month. Continued investment at this pace positions the brand well to close September with meaningful top-line contribution from paid media.
►
Ad Efficiency — WK38 ACoS came in at 60.9% and ROAS at 1.64x, a modest softening from the prior week's 56.2% ACoS and 1.78x ROAS, presenting a clear opportunity to fine-tune bid efficiency across active campaigns. Average CPC held steady at $0.89, which remains a manageable cost floor given the strong 21.5% CVR driving conversion volume. Targeted bid adjustments on lower-performing ad groups will be the primary lever to move ROAS back toward the prior-week benchmark.
►
Actions This Week — Conduct a keyword-level bid review to identify terms with ACoS above 70% and reduce bids by 15–20% to improve overall campaign efficiency without sacrificing impression share on top converters. Reallocate a portion of budget from underperforming placements toward the highest-CVR keywords to reinforce conversion momentum and recover ROAS toward the 1.75x+ range. Continue monitoring daily pacing closely to ensure MTD blended ROAS holds at or above 1.74x through the remainder of September.
