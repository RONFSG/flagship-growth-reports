Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Performance — Opopop generated $13,989.52 in PPC revenue on $7,583.95 in ad spend this week, delivering a 1.84x ROAS. This represents a significant week-over-week decline from the prior 2.86x ROAS despite spend increasing by $2,971.92, suggesting the additional budget was deployed inefficiently. Impressions of 4,970,898 and 8,973 clicks indicate solid top-of-funnel reach, but revenue efficiency lagged behind the volume gains.
►
MTD Pacing — The sharp spend increase week-over-week signals an accelerated burn rate that could pressure the monthly budget if not recalibrated mid-cycle. With ROAS dropping from 2.86x to 1.84x as spend scaled, the current pacing trajectory risks closing the month below profitability targets. Budget reallocation toward higher-converting placements is critical to protect MTD ROAS before further spend accumulates.
►
Ad Efficiency — ACoS of 54.2% is elevated and warrants attention, particularly given that a healthy 1P Walmart benchmark typically targets 30-40% depending on category margins. CPC of $0.85 is relatively controlled, and CVR of 21.7% is a strong signal that landing page and item content are converting well once shoppers arrive. The primary efficiency gap appears to be bid and budget allocation rather than conversion quality, meaning tightening keyword-level bids on low-ROAS terms could meaningfully improve ACoS without sacrificing volume.
►
Actions This Week — Pull a keyword-level ROAS report and pause or reduce bids by 20-30% on any search terms with ACoS above 70% that did not contribute to the top 50% of orders. Reallocate that recovered budget into Sponsored Product campaigns on the top 3 converting items where CVR is strongest to defend efficient revenue. Additionally, review placement-level performance in Walmart Ad Center to confirm whether auto or manual placements drove the ROAS decline and adjust accordingly.
