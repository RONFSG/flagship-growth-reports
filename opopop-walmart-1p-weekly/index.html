Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Performance — Opopop generated $11,948 in PPC revenue on $4,612 in ad spend this week, delivering a 2.59x ROAS. Impressions reached 3.7M with 7,673 clicks and 1,740 orders, reflecting strong volume but softer revenue efficiency relative to last week.
►
MTD Pacing — The week-over-week spend acceleration of $1,410 suggests monthly budget is pacing above plan if this trajectory continues through the remainder of the month. The current 2.59x ROAS sits below last week's 3.24x benchmark, which may pressure MTD blended ROAS targets if efficiency does not recover. Close monitoring of daily spend caps is recommended to avoid over-delivery in lower-efficiency periods.
►
Ad Efficiency — ACoS at 38.6% is elevated and warrants attention, particularly given the spike in spend without a proportional revenue lift. CPC of $0.60 remains relatively controlled, suggesting the efficiency gap is more related to keyword mix or bid inflation on lower-converting terms than to click costs alone. CVR of 22.7% is a strong signal that landing page and product content are converting well, so optimization focus should shift to tightening keyword targeting rather than creative or listing changes.
►
Actions This Week — Audit the incremental keywords and placements that absorbed the additional $1,410 in spend and pause or reduce bids on any terms delivering ACoS above 50% to bring blended ACoS back toward the 30-33% range. Increase bids by 10-15% on the top converting search terms driving the 22.7% CVR to capture additional efficient volume before pulling back on waste. Review Walmart Sponsored Products placement reports to identify if Buy Box or search in-grid placements are underperforming relative to cost, and reallocate budget accordingly.
