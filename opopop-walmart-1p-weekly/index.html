Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Performance — Opopop generated $13,419.73 in PPC revenue on $7,583.95 in ad spend this week, delivering a ROAS of 1.77x across 1,873 orders. This represents a significant week-over-week decline from the prior ROAS of 2.86x despite a $2,971.92 increase in spend, suggesting the incremental budget did not scale efficiently. Impressions reached 4,970,898 with 8,973 clicks, indicating strong top-of-funnel reach but deteriorating return at higher spend levels.
►
MTD Pacing — The jump in weekly spend to $7,583.95 from $4,612.03 the prior week signals an accelerated budget pace that is likely putting MTD ROAS under pressure if this trend holds. If spend continues at this week's rate, the account is on track to significantly exceed a normalized monthly budget while ROAS compresses. Close monitoring of daily spend caps is recommended to ensure MTD ROAS does not fall further below the 2.0x threshold.
►
Ad Efficiency — ACoS at 56.5% is elevated and indicates ads are consuming over half of attributed revenue, which warrants immediate bid and targeting review. CPC of $0.85 is relatively controlled, so the inefficiency appears driven more by campaign mix or keyword targeting than by cost-per-click inflation. CVR of 20.9% is a strong signal that converting traffic is healthy, meaning the primary lever to improve ACoS is reducing spend on low-converting impressions rather than addressing on-page conversion.
►
Actions This Week — Audit the top 20% of keywords by spend that are delivering below 1.5x ROAS and reduce bids by 15 to 20% to bring ACoS back toward the 40% target. Shift incremental budget toward Sponsored Products placements and exact-match keywords where CVR is proven, pulling back on broad and auto campaigns that likely drove the impression spike without proportional revenue. Submit a Walmart Connect optimization request to review placement-level performance and confirm search in-grid vs. buy box spend allocation is weighted toward historically efficient placements.
