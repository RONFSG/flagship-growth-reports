Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Opopop delivered a strong week on Walmart 1P, generating $9,384.61 in PPC revenue from $5,719.38 in ad spend, a meaningful reduction in spend week over week. With 1,387 orders driven across 6.7 million impressions, the brand maintained solid visibility and transaction volume heading into the next optimization cycle.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR came in at an impressive 21.5%, signaling that shoppers who engage with Opopop ads are converting at a highly efficient rate, which is a strong foundation to build on. ROAS sits at 1.64x and ACoS at 60.9%, both areas where targeted bid and budget adjustments this week can close the gap back toward last week's 1.78x ROAS benchmark.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Spend decreased by $772.39 week over week from $6,491.77 to $5,719.38, reflecting tighter budget management while still producing over $9,300 in attributed revenue. As we refine keyword targeting and reallocate spend toward the highest-converting placements, we expect to see the revenue-to-spend ratio improve in the coming weeks.
►
Actions This Week — The team will audit search term reports to identify top-converting keywords and increase bids on placements driving the strongest CVR to recover ROAS momentum. We will also review item-level performance across Sponsored Products to shift budget away from high-spend, low-conversion SKUs and consolidate investment behind Opopop's core hero items on Walmart Connect.
