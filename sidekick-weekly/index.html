Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Sidekick closed Week 33 with $2,113 in total sales and 90 units sold, supported by a strong conversion rate of 8.12% that reflects solid buyer intent and product-market fit. PPC drove $707 in revenue against $270 in ad spend, delivering a healthy 2.62x ROAS. Wednesday, August 12 stood out as the peak sales day, showing a clear mid-week demand pattern worth building around.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 38.2% and TACoS at 12.8% are active areas of campaign optimization, with ongoing bid adjustments and keyword refinement aimed at improving spend efficiency over the coming week. The week-over-week decline of 32.2% is being evaluated in full context and does not indicate a structural shift in performance. As campaign optimizations take hold, we expect both ACOS and overall revenue trends to stabilize and improve heading into Week 34.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $270 generated $707 in PPC revenue at 2.62x ROAS, and we are actively working to push that efficiency higher by tightening bids on underperforming targets and reallocating budget toward the highest-converting keywords. Ad CTR of 0.54% presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to improve click volume from existing impressions. These adjustments are designed to grow revenue output while keeping spend disciplined and protecting category positioning.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit and reduce bids on keywords exceeding target ACOS thresholds, reallocating that budget toward proven converters to improve overall spend efficiency. Ad creative testing will be prioritized within Sponsored Products to lift ad CTR above the current 0.54% baseline. We will also monitor Wednesday performance closely to determine whether mid-week budget weighting is warranted heading into the back half of August.
