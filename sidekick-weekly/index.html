Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Sidekick closed Week 39 with $3,877 in total sales and 145 units sold, with Saturday September 26 standing out as the peak sales day of the week. PPC drove $2,088 in revenue at a ROAS of 2.46x, reflecting solid conversion momentum with an 8.46% CVR — a strong signal that shoppers who reach the listing are buying.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 40.7% and TACoS at 21.9% are areas we are actively working to bring into a tighter range as campaign optimizations continue to take shape. The -10.6% WoW dip in total sales is being evaluated in the context of normal weekly fluctuation, and current CVR strength at 8.46% gives us a healthy foundation to build from. As bid adjustments and keyword pruning take effect, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $849 in ad spend generating $2,088 in PPC revenue, the campaigns are producing returns and we are focused on pushing that efficiency further. Ad CTR at 0.45% represents an opportunity we are addressing through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand creative testing — refining ad copy and headline variations within the ad platform to drive higher engagement from impressions already being captured. These improvements, combined with tightening spend on lower-performing targets, are designed to improve ROAS and bring ACOS closer to our target range.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue refining keyword bids, pausing underperforming targets, and reallocating budget toward the highest-converting search terms to improve ACOS efficiency. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be a priority this week, with headline and copy variations tested to lift ad CTR above the current 0.45% baseline. We will also monitor Saturday performance closely given its status as last week's peak day, ensuring adequate budget is available heading into the weekend.
