Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Sidekick closed Week 35 with $2,278 in total sales and 96 units sold, with Saturday August 29 standing out as the peak revenue day of the week. PPC drove $1,052 in tracked revenue at a 2.35x ROAS, and the conversion rate held strong at 10.38%, reflecting solid purchase intent from shoppers who clicked through to the listing.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 42.6% and TACoS at 19.7% are active areas of optimization as we work to improve the ratio of ad spend to total revenue. The WoW decline of 23.2% is being evaluated in the context of the prior week's performance baseline, and we are focused on stabilizing volume through more efficient targeting. As campaign adjustments take effect, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $448 in ad spend against $1,052 in PPC revenue, we are actively refining keyword bids and match types to improve return on each dollar deployed. Ad CTR of 0.38% on Sponsored Product campaigns presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing ad creative variations — including updated ad copy and imagery within the ad platform — to improve click quality and volume. These adjustments are designed to strengthen efficiency while maintaining competitive visibility in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS to identify and reduce spend on terms performing above target, redirecting budget toward high-converting, lower-funnel keywords. Sponsored Product ad creative testing will continue with new copy and visual variations aimed at improving ad CTR from the current 0.38% baseline. We will also review dayparting and bidding schedules around weekend performance, given that Saturday was the peak day, to better capture that demand pattern going into Week 36.
