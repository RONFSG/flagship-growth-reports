►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 30.7% and TACoS at 19.1% are areas we are actively working to improve as campaign optimizations continue to take shape. As these refinements compound over the coming days, we expect both efficiency and traffic quality to trend in a favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $828 in ad spend generated $2,701 in PPC revenue this week, reflecting a productive but improvable spend structure that we are proactively refining. We are identifying lower-performing keywords and reallocating budget toward the placements and targets driving the strongest returns, while maintaining visibility in competitive positions to protect the momentum built during this growth week. These adjustments are designed to improve margin contribution while sustaining the strong sales trajectory established in Week 38.
►
Actions This Week — We will review keyword-level performance to pause or reduce bids on targets with high spend and low return, while scaling budget toward the highest-converting ad groups driving the 3.26x ROAS. Ad creative will be refreshed with new Sponsored Product headline and copy variations to work toward improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.51% benchmark. Conversion rate at 9.52% will be monitored closely to ensure traffic quality remains strong as we shift spend toward more efficient targets.
