Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Sidekick had a strong Week 32, generating $3,114 in total sales across 128 units sold — a 13.4% week-over-week increase that signals solid and accelerating momentum. PPC contributed $1,363 in revenue at a 3.20x ROAS, and Saturday August 8 stood out as the peak sales day, suggesting strong end-of-week demand. CVR of 8.58% reflects that shoppers are converting at a healthy rate once they reach the listing.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 31.3% and TACoS at 13.7% are areas we are actively working to improve as campaigns continue to mature and optimize. Ad CTR at 0.60% has room to grow, and we are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product imagery and headline copy — to improve click-through performance. As these optimizations take hold over the coming week, we expect both efficiency and traffic quality to improve.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $426 in ad spend driving $1,363 in PPC revenue, the 3.20x ROAS shows that paid campaigns are generating meaningful return, and we are focused on pushing that efficiency further. We are actively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward top-converting terms and reduce waste on underperforming placements. These adjustments are designed to strengthen margin contribution while sustaining the growth trajectory that delivered a 13.4% WoW increase.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS to identify and reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds, reallocating that budget toward proven converters. Ad creative testing will continue within the Sponsored Product campaigns, focusing on ad copy and imagery variations to lift the 0.60% ad CTR. We will also monitor daily performance closely following the Saturday peak to identify any repeatable demand patterns worth amplifying through dayparting or budget scheduling adjustments.
