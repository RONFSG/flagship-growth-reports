Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Sidekick posted $4,159 in total sales and moved 151 units during Week 42, with PPC contributing a strong $1,825 in revenue. Conversion rate came in at an impressive 9.28%, reflecting solid product-market fit and healthy shopper intent among those clicking through. Sunday, October 11 was the peak sales day, providing a strong start to the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 28.6% and TACoS at 12.6% are being actively monitored as we continue refining campaign targeting and adjusting bids toward our most efficient placements. Ad CTR of 0.52% is an area we are actively working to improve through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing — including headline variations and ad copy refinements within the ad platform. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both click quality and conversion efficiency to continue trending in the right direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend was deliberately pulled back to $523 this week, and the resulting ROAS of 3.49x shows the remaining spend is working effectively. We are prioritizing efficient allocation by concentrating budget on top-performing keywords and ad types while reducing investment in underperforming segments. This proactive approach positions Sidekick to maintain category presence and scale back up efficiently as demand conditions normalize.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue tightening keyword bids on lower-converting terms to improve ACOS while protecting spend on placements driving the strongest return. Ad creative will be refreshed within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns, testing new headline variations and ad copy to strengthen ad CTR from the current 0.52% baseline. We will also analyze Sunday's peak-day performance data to identify which traffic sources and placements drove the strongest results and apply those learnings to the remainder of the week.
