Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Sidekick delivered a strong Week 40, generating $4,327 in total sales across 138 units sold — a healthy 11.6% week-over-week increase. PPC drove $1,416 in revenue at a solid 3.42x ROAS, and a CVR of 8.76% reflects strong purchase intent from shoppers reaching the listing. Sunday September 27 stood out as the peak sales day, anchoring a momentum-building start to the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 29.3% is an active area of optimization, and as we continue refining keyword bids and shifting budget toward higher-converting targets, we expect that number to improve in the coming week. CTR on our Sponsored Product ads is currently at 0.56%, and we are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform to strengthen click-through performance. These are normal levers in a scaling campaign, and both metrics are trending in a direction we can move with targeted adjustments.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $414 in ad spend against $4,327 in total sales, TACoS sits at 9.6%, which reflects a well-contained ad investment relative to overall revenue. We are actively reviewing campaign structure to ensure spend is concentrated on the most efficient placements and converting search terms, protecting both margin and ranking position. These proactive adjustments are designed to sustain the growth trajectory we're seeing while steadily improving return on every ad dollar.
►
Actions This Week — We will reduce bids on high-spend, lower-converting keywords to bring ACOS closer to target while protecting share on top-performing search terms. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product campaigns will be prioritized to address the 0.56% ad CTR and drive more qualified clicks into the funnel. We will also analyze the Sunday September 27 peak day in detail to identify any demand patterns worth capitalizing on heading into the next weekend.
