Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Sidekick posted a strong Week 36 with $2,453 in total sales and 94 units sold, marking a solid +7.7% week-over-week gain. Sunday August 30 was the peak sales day, helping set a positive tone heading into September. PPC drove $1,078 in revenue at a 2.05x ROAS, confirming that paid traffic continued to contribute meaningful volume to overall performance.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 48.9% and TACoS at 21.5% reflect an active investment phase, and both are being closely monitored as campaign optimizations progress. CVR of 5.63% is a genuine bright spot, showing that shoppers who reach the listing are converting at a healthy rate. As we continue refining keyword bids and reallocating budget toward top-performing ad groups, we expect ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $527 in ad spend against $2,453 in total sales, we are actively working to improve the ratio between paid investment and total revenue output. Campaign-level bid adjustments and a sharper focus on converting search terms will help shift spend toward higher-efficiency placements over the next week. These moves are designed to protect the sales momentum we built this week while improving the return profile of every ad dollar deployed.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit current keyword bids and pause or reduce spend on search terms delivering clicks without conversions, redirecting that budget toward proven performers. Ad creative will be reviewed and tested within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations — to work toward improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.52%. We will also monitor daily performance closely through the week to capitalize on any momentum from the strong Sunday peak and maintain the +7.7% growth trajectory.
