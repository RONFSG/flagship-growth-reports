Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 32 delivered $121,771 in total sales across 351 units sold, marking a strong +8.5% week-over-week increase and confirming continued positive momentum heading into August. PPC drove $54,329 in revenue at an impressive 5.97x ROAS, and Sunday August 9 emerged as the peak sales day, reflecting healthy consumer demand across the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR at 0.49% and CVR at 3.16% are areas we are actively working to improve through ongoing ad creative refinements and bid strategy adjustments. ACOS at 16.8% reflects the current investment level in growth and is being monitored closely as we optimize toward greater efficiency. As campaign tuning continues this week, we expect both conversion rate and ad click quality to trend in a favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $9,105 in ad spend against $54,329 in PPC revenue, the 5.97x ROAS demonstrates that our campaigns are generating strong returns and working efficiently at scale. TACoS of 7.5% reflects a healthy relationship between ad spend and total business revenue, and we are proactively refining keyword targeting and bid allocation to sustain and improve this balance. Our focus this week is on protecting high-performing placements while pulling back on lower-efficiency segments to sharpen overall spend quality.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and imagery — to strengthen ad CTR above the current 0.49% benchmark. Bid adjustments will be applied to underperforming keywords and ad groups to bring ACOS closer to target while preserving top-of-search visibility on converting terms. We will also analyze Sunday August 9 peak-day traffic patterns to identify targeting and scheduling opportunities that can be leveraged to replicate that performance in the coming week.
