Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo closed Week 31 with $112,486 in total sales across 321 units, delivering a strong ROAS of 9.68x on just $4,322 in ad spend. PPC drove $41,840 in revenue, accounting for a healthy share of overall volume, and TACoS held at a lean 3.8%, reflecting solid organic momentum alongside paid performance. Sunday August 2 was the peak sales day, capping the week on a high note with essentially flat WoW performance — a stable result that sets a strong baseline heading into August.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 10.3% is within a manageable range and remains steady as we continue refining keyword targeting and bid strategies across active campaigns. Ad CTR of 0.52% represents an ongoing area of focus, and we are actively testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headlines and ad copy within the ad platform — to drive higher engagement from impressions. CVR at 3.12% is solid and we expect continued improvement as optimized campaigns funnel higher-intent traffic to the listings.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $4,322 in total ad spend generating nearly $42K in PPC revenue, the account is operating with strong efficiency and disciplined budget allocation. We are continuing to evaluate keyword-level performance to shift spend toward top-converting terms while pulling back on lower-efficiency placements. This proactive approach is designed to protect our market position in the category while pushing ACOS closer to target as we move through August.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine bids on high-spend, lower-converting keywords to improve ACOS and maximize the efficiency of the existing $4,322 spend level. Ad creative testing will continue within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands — specifically rotating headline and copy variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.52%. We will also monitor Sunday performance closely given its status as peak day in Week 31, ensuring budget is allocated to capture elevated weekend demand again in Week 32.
