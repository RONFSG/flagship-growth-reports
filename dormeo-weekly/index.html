Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo closed Week 31 with $112,486 in total sales across 321 units sold, holding steady with a flat WoW change that reflects consistent demand and strong baseline momentum. PPC drove $41,840 in revenue at an impressive 9.68x ROAS, confirming that paid campaigns are delivering exceptional returns on every dollar spent. Sunday August 2 was the peak sales day, capping the week on a high note and signaling strong end-of-week consumer engagement.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 10.3% and ad CTR at 0.52% are both areas we are actively optimizing within the ad platform to improve efficiency and click-through performance. ACOS is currently flat and as we continue refining keyword targeting and adjusting bids on lower-performing ad groups, we expect gradual improvement in the coming week. Ad CTR optimization is underway through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand creative testing, and stronger creative engagement will support improved conversion flow across campaigns.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $4,322 in ad spend against $112,486 in total revenue, TACoS sits at a healthy 3.8%, reflecting strong organic sales velocity supporting the overall business. We are continuing to make proactive adjustments to campaign structure and bid strategy to ensure every dollar of the ad budget is working efficiently and protecting our category position. These ongoing optimizations are designed to sustain our competitive standing while steadily improving the ratio of spend to total revenue.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.52% and drive stronger engagement from high-intent shoppers. Bid and keyword refinements will continue across underperforming ad groups to bring ACOS closer to our target threshold while maintaining the 9.68x ROAS benchmark. We will also monitor Sunday performance closely given its status as the Week 31 peak day, ensuring campaigns are structured to capture elevated weekend demand heading into Week 32.
