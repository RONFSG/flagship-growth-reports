Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 38 delivered $16,536 in total sales across 104 units sold, marking a strong +20.9% week-over-week increase and confirming solid momentum heading into fall. PPC drove $9,699 in revenue at a 3.58x ROAS, and Thursday September 17 stood out as the peak sales day, reflecting strong mid-week customer demand.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 27.9% and TACoS at 16.4% are areas we are actively optimizing as campaign efficiency continues to develop. CVR of 4.66% is a healthy conversion signal, and as we refine keyword targeting and shift budget toward top-performing ad groups, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming week. Ad CTR at 0.56% is an area where continued creative testing within the ad platform will help drive higher click volume and improve overall campaign leverage.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,706 in ad spend generating $9,699 in PPC revenue, the campaigns are producing meaningful returns while we continue to sharpen efficiency across the account. We are proactively adjusting bids on lower-performing targets and reallocating spend toward the placements and keywords driving the strongest conversion, which positions us well to improve ROAS beyond the current 3.58x. These optimizations are designed to protect our competitive market position while improving the ratio of profitable spend over time.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.56% and drive higher-quality traffic into the funnel. Budget will be redistributed toward the highest-converting keywords and placements identified during Week 38, with underperforming targets receiving bid reductions to bring ACOS closer to target. We will also closely monitor Thursday performance to determine whether mid-week demand patterns are repeatable and worth weighting campaign budgets toward.
