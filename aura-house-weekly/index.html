Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 38 delivered $16,536 in total sales across 104 units sold, marking a strong +20.9% week-over-week increase that demonstrates real momentum heading into the fall season. PPC contributed $9,699 in revenue at a ROAS of 3.58x, confirming that paid campaigns are driving meaningful, high-quality traffic. Thursday September 17 stood out as the peak sales day, suggesting strong mid-week purchase intent that we can continue to build around.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 27.9% and TACoS at 16.4% remain areas of active focus as we work to improve the ratio of organic to paid revenue over time. CTR at 0.56% on our ad creative reflects room to test stronger hooks and visual formats within the ad platform, and we are actively exploring Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand creative variations to lift click-through rates. As these optimizations take hold alongside our improving CVR of 4.66%, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $2,706 in ad spend generated $9,699 in PPC revenue this week, reflecting a solid 3.58x ROAS that confirms our campaigns are working efficiently at scale. We are continuing to refine keyword targeting and bid strategies to shift budget toward the highest-converting placements, protecting our category position while improving overall spend efficiency. As organic velocity builds on the back of this week's strong unit sales, we expect the paid-to-organic revenue balance to improve and TACoS to compress further.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including updated ad copy and headline formats within the ad platform — to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.56% and drive more qualified clicks into the funnel. We will also conduct a bid-level review across active campaigns to reallocate spend away from underperforming keywords and double down on terms driving the strongest CVR and ROAS. Finally, we will analyze Thursday's peak-day performance data to identify any campaign, placement, or dayparting patterns that can be replicated and scaled across the full week.
