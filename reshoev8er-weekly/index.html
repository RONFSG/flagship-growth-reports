Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 30 delivered $44,500 in total sales across 1,550 units sold, marking a strong 14.1% week-over-week growth that reflects solid momentum heading into late July. PPC drove $23,073 in revenue at a healthy 3.21x ROAS, and Friday July 24 stood out as the peak day, indicating strong end-of-week consumer demand. CVR of 13.82% confirms that traffic arriving at our listings is converting at a highly efficient rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 31.2% and TACoS at 16.2% are areas we are actively working to tighten as campaign optimizations continue rolling out. CTR of 0.67% reflects an opportunity to sharpen creative and targeting to pull more qualified clicks at a lower cost per visit. As bid adjustments and negative keyword refinements take effect, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend favorably in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $7,192 in ad spend generating $23,073 in PPC revenue, the core efficiency of our campaigns is sound and continuing to improve. We are proactively reallocating budget away from lower-performing placements and toward the ad groups and keywords driving the strongest return, which positions us well for sustained margin improvement. Protecting our category visibility while refining spend allocation remains the priority as we scale into the back half of summer.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit and pause underperforming keywords contributing to elevated ACOS, redirecting that budget toward top-converting search terms to strengthen overall campaign efficiency. Creative testing on primary listing images and ad copy will be prioritized to improve CTR beyond the current 0.67% baseline. We will also analyze Friday July 24 peak-day traffic patterns to identify replicable triggers and apply those insights to upcoming daily bid scheduling.
