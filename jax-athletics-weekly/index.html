►
Weekly Highlights — JAX Athletics delivered $2,237 in total sales for Week 46, up 32.0% from $1,694 the prior week, on 34 units. ►
Metrics to Watch — Organic sales eased to $952 (-8.4% WoW) and Tuesday, Nov 10 recorded $0 in sales, so daily volume was uneven. The Platinum White Strap Cuff Batting Gloves drew 38 sessions (-15.6%) at a 5.3% conversion rate; we are focused on building steadier day-to-day organic momentum.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $482 (prior $424) generated $1,284 in PPC revenue for a 2.66x ROAS, up from 1.54x, at a 37.6% ACOS. TACoS improved to 21.6% from 25.06%, with a 0.29% ad CTR and a 2.60% ad conversion rate.
►
Actions This Week — We will shift budget toward the strongest-converting gloves, including the Blue Raspberry Blast Small (18.2% CVR) and Baby Blue Strap Cuff (8.0% CVR), and trim bids on low-converting search terms to keep ACOS moving down from 37.6%. We will also test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.29% heading into the holiday season.
