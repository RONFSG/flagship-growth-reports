Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — JAX Athletics grew total sales to $2,259 in Week 46, up 33.3% from $1,694 the prior week, on 34 units. PPC revenue more than doubled to $1,359 (+107.6%), and Saturday, Nov 14 was the peak day at $639. The Graphite Grey Strap Cuff Batting Gloves reached $149 (+100.0%) and the Pink Pro Cuff Batting Gloves $119 (+100.0%).
►
Metrics to Watch — With PPC revenue up 107.6%, more of this week's sales came through ads, and organic sales eased to $899 (-13.5% WoW). Tuesday, Nov 10 recorded $0 in sales, and the Platinum White Strap Cuff Batting Gloves drew 38 sessions (-15.6%) at a 5.3% conversion rate. The good news is that ad efficiency improved sharply, and we are working to turn that ad-driven visibility into steadier organic sales day to day.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — ROAS improved to 2.82x from 1.55x, as $481 in ad spend (prior $423) generated $1,359 in PPC revenue at a 35.4% ACOS. TACoS fell to 21.3% from 24.97%, with a 0.29% ad CTR and a 2.76% ad conversion rate.
►
Actions This Week — We will shift budget toward the strongest-converting gloves, including the Blue Raspberry Blast Small (18.2% CVR) and Baby Blue Strap Cuff (8.0% CVR), while trimming bids on low-converting search terms to bring ACOS below 35.4%. We will review the search terms behind the Graphite Grey Strap Cuff's 95 sessions (2.1% CVR) to focus spend on higher-intent traffic, and test new Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.29% heading into the holiday season.
