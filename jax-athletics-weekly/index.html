►
Weekly Highlights — JAX Athletics posted $985 in total sales across 14 units sold during Week 42, with Monday October 12 standing out as the peak revenue day. The team is maintaining market presence while managing spend efficiently through this softer demand period.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 92.6% and TACoS at 38.1% reflect the current environment where ad spend is proportionally higher relative to total sales volume, and both metrics are being actively monitored as spend adjustments take effect. Ad CTR of 0.25% and CVR of 1.32% present clear opportunities for improvement, and ongoing ad creative testing within Sponsored Product campaigns is underway to drive stronger engagement. As spend continues to be right-sized and campaign targeting is refined, we expect efficiency metrics to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $375 in ad spend generating a 1.08x ROAS, the current focus is on reallocating budget toward the highest-performing placements and pulling back on underperforming ad groups to improve overall return. We are actively making adjustments to keyword bids and match types to ensure spend is concentrated where it drives the most efficient revenue. These proactive optimizations position JAX Athletics to improve ROAS meaningfully as category demand stabilizes.
►
Actions This Week — Conduct a bid-level review across all active Sponsored Product campaigns to reduce investment in high-spend, low-converting keywords and redirect budget toward proven converters. Test updated ad creative variations including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform to improve ad CTR above the current 0.25% benchmark. Continue monitoring daily spend pacing closely to ensure the budget pullback strategy stays on track while protecting JAX Athletics' visibility in key category placements.
