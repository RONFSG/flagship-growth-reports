We are refining keyword targeting this week to bring ad returns back in line.

Actions This Week — Lower bids on search terms that drew clicks without orders to bring ACOS down from 58.6%. Reallocate that budget to the Platinum White Strap Cuff (6.1% conversion) and Pink Strap Cuff (7.7% conversion) gloves. Test new ad creative to lift the 0.57% ad CTR.
