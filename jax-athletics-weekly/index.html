►
Weekly Highlights — JAX Athletics closed Week 35 with $3,035 in total sales and 45 units sold, marking a solid +4.7% week-over-week increase that reflects continued positive momentum heading into late August. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 42.0% and TACoS at 23.6% are areas we are actively working to bring down through ongoing bid refinements and keyword-level optimizations. As these adjustments take hold, we expect both efficiency metrics and ad engagement to trend in a favorable direction over the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $715 in ad spend against $3,035 in total sales, we are making proactive adjustments to improve cost efficiency while sustaining the growth trajectory that produced a +4.7% WoW gain. We are focusing on reallocating budget away from underperforming keywords and toward the placements and match types generating the strongest return. These shifts are designed to protect our market position and improve ROAS beyond the current 2.38x benchmark over the near term.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine bids on high-spend, low-converting keywords to bring ACOS closer to a target range below 38% while preserving spend on top-performing terms. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands will continue with a focus on headline variations aimed at improving ad CTR above the current 0.56%. We will also analyze Thursday's peak-day data to identify the campaign elements that drove August 27 performance and replicate those conditions earlier in the week.
