Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 39 delivered $2,436 in total sales across 35 units sold, marking a strong +55.2% week-over-week surge with Monday September 21 standing out as the peak sales day. PPC drove $1,301 in revenue against $749 in ad spend, confirming that paid campaigns are actively contributing to growth momentum. The overall trajectory is encouraging and sets a solid foundation heading into the final stretch of Q3.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 57.6% and TACoS at 30.8% are areas we are actively working to bring down as campaign optimizations take hold across the account. We are refining keyword targeting and bid adjustments to push spend toward the highest-converting placements, which we expect to improve efficiency over the coming week. CVR at 2.95% is a strong signal that shoppers who engage are converting well, giving us a healthy base to build more efficient volume from.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At a 1.74x ROAS, we are generating positive returns on ad spend while operating in a competitive category where maintaining visibility is essential to sustaining the growth we saw this week. We are making proactive bid and budget adjustments to concentrate spend on proven performers and reduce waste on underperforming keywords and match types. These refinements are designed to improve ROAS and bring TACoS closer to target without sacrificing the momentum and ranking gains we are building.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS across Sponsored Product campaigns and pause or reduce bids on terms exceeding efficiency thresholds, redirecting that budget toward top-converting search terms. Ad creative variations within the Sponsored Product campaigns will be tested to improve ad CTR from its current 0.44%, with a focus on headline copy and ad-level imagery to drive stronger click volume. We will also monitor Monday performance closely given its role as the peak day, ensuring budgets are structured to capture demand early in the week.
