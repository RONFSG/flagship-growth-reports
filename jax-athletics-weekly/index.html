Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — JAX Athletics closed Week 34 with $2,900 in total sales and 45 units sold, marking a positive +1.4% week-over-week gain that reflects continued steady momentum. PPC drove $2,430 of that revenue, confirming that paid campaigns remain a strong engine for growth. Monday August 17 stood out as the peak sales day, giving us a clear anchor to build around as we plan promotions and bid strategies going forward.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 68.8% and TACoS at 57.6% reflect a heavy investment phase where we are actively working to sharpen targeting and redirect spend toward our highest-converting keywords. CVR of 2.53% is a solid foundation to build on, and as we continue tightening campaign structure and reducing inefficient spend, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend downward in the coming week. These are active optimization levers we are turning right now, and improvement is the clear near-term trajectory.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $1,671 in ad spend against $2,430 in PPC revenue, we are in an active phase of recalibrating spend allocation to protect efficiency while maintaining our competitive position in the category. We are making targeted adjustments to underperforming ad groups and prioritizing budget toward placements and keywords that are delivering the strongest return. These steps are designed to improve ROAS beyond the current 1.45x while sustaining the volume and visibility gains we have built.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level performance to pause or reduce bids on terms driving spend without sufficient conversion, redirecting that budget toward proven high-CVR placements. We will test updated ad creative variations within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.32% and drive more qualified traffic. We will also analyze Monday's peak day data to identify patterns in timing and placement that we can replicate through strategic bid adjustments earlier in the week.
