Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Paulina Fitness TikTok Shop recorded its first-ever sale in Week 46 — $32.86 GMV from 1 order on Wednesday, November 12. The peak impression day was Saturday, November 8 at 9,891 impressions.
►
Metrics to Watch — While the first sale is a milestone, conversion rate is very low at 0.06% — 1 checkout from roughly 1,600 product page views. This suggests the product listing, pricing, or video-to-product link setup may need attention. Active creator count also declined from 3 in WK45 to 1 in WK46, meaning 2 previously active creators stopped posting — this needs follow-up. No new outreach contacts or samples were sent this week, so the pipeline depends entirely on @paulinahefferan continuing to post. Ad metrics (ad spend, ROAS, TACoS) are not available this week.
►
Content & Reach — 4 new videos were posted this week (down from 6 in WK45), all from @paulinahefferan, who now has 10 total videos live. The average views per video was 2,402 — strong early engagement, down slightly from WK45's 3,067. Likes reached 1,766 and comments 72, indicating solid audience interaction. The challenge is converting that engagement into purchases: with 26,246 impressions and only 1 sale, the content is reaching people but not driving enough purchase intent.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Re-engage the 2 creators who were active in WK45 but went silent in WK46 — check in on posting plans and whether they need additional support. (2) Review @paulinahefferan's product links and commission setup to improve the 0.06% conversion rate — ensure product pages are optimized and commission is competitive. (3) Launch new outreach this week targeting at least 500+ creators to build the pipeline and prevent over-reliance on a single active creator.
