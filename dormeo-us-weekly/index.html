Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 38 delivered $103,720 in total sales across 289 units, marking a +1.3% week-over-week gain and continuing the brand's positive sales momentum heading into fall. PPC drove $47,745 in revenue at a strong 6.36x ROAS, confirming that paid campaigns are generating high-quality, converting traffic. Wednesday, September 16 stood out as the peak sales day, reflecting solid mid-week demand across the catalog.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR came in at 0.52%, and as we continue refining ad creative and testing headline variations within the ad platform, we expect this figure to trend upward in the coming weeks. ACOS at 15.7% reflects room for continued campaign-level optimization, and with targeted bid adjustments already underway, we anticipate gradual improvement without sacrificing traffic volume. CVR at 2.97% is performing well and reinforces that shoppers reaching the listing are engaging with strong purchase intent.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $7,507 in ad spend generating $47,745 in PPC revenue, the campaign structure is delivering meaningful returns, and we are actively fine-tuning keyword bids and budget allocation to push efficiency further. TACoS at 7.2% reflects a healthy relationship between total ad investment and overall revenue, and our focus this week is on pulling back on lower-performing placements while protecting share on high-converting terms. These proactive adjustments are designed to strengthen margin contribution while sustaining the brand's competitive position in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit underperforming keywords and shift budget toward top-converting ASINs and search terms to drive ACOS improvement from 15.7% toward our target range. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will focus on headline variations aimed at improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.52% baseline. We will also monitor Wednesday performance closely given its consistent peak-day pattern and ensure campaigns are fully funded and optimized entering mid-week.
