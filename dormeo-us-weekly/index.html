Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo US closed Week 37 with $101,995 in total sales and 285 units sold, anchored by a strong Sunday September 13 peak day that drove meaningful volume to close the week. PPC contributed $55,627 in revenue against $8,062 in ad spend, producing an impressive 6.90x ROAS that reflects well-optimized campaign targeting. TACoS at 7.9% signals healthy overall advertising efficiency relative to total business revenue.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 14.5% and ad CTR at 0.78% are active areas of optimization as we work to improve click quality and conversion flow across Sponsored Product campaigns. CVR at 2.80% shows shoppers are converting at a solid rate, and continued bid refinement on high-intent keywords will help push that figure higher in the coming week. The WoW decline of 12.7% reflects normal week-over-week fluctuation and is expected to stabilize as campaign adjustments take effect.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $8,062 deployed and a 6.90x ROAS, ad spend is performing efficiently and we are actively refining keyword targeting to sustain and improve that return. We are reviewing underperforming ad groups and reallocating budget toward placements and match types driving the strongest conversion rates. These proactive adjustments are designed to protect our competitive position in the category while continuing to scale revenue efficiently.
►
Actions This Week — We will test revised Sponsored Product ad creative including headline copy and ad imagery variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.78% and drive stronger top-of-funnel engagement. Budget will be reallocated from lower-performing keywords toward high-converting search terms that contributed to Sunday's peak day performance. Bid optimization across Sponsored Product campaigns will target ACOS improvement while maintaining the strong 6.90x ROAS we delivered this week.
