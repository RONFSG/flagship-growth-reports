Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 43 delivered $54,796 in total sales across 161 units, with PPC driving $26,819 — nearly half of all revenue — at a strong 3.98x ROAS. Saturday, October 24 was the peak sales day, and TACoS held at a healthy 12.3%, reflecting solid overall ad efficiency relative to total business volume.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 25.1% and CVR at 2.40% are both active areas of optimization, with campaign-level adjustments underway to improve conversion quality and reduce wasted spend. Ad CTR at 0.53% presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to drive stronger click-through performance. As these refinements take hold, we expect both CVR and ad CTR to trend upward in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $6,744 deployed against $26,819 in PPC revenue, the 3.98x ROAS demonstrates that our ad spend is working efficiently at the campaign level. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward the highest-converting terms and pull back on lower-efficiency placements, which will strengthen overall margin contribution. These adjustments are designed to protect our competitive positioning in the category while improving the quality of spend week over week.
►
Actions This Week — We will reallocate ad budget toward top-converting keywords and pause or reduce bids on underperforming placements to bring ACOS closer to target. Creative testing across Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ads will continue, with a focus on improving ad CTR from its current 0.53% by refining headline copy and ad-level imagery. We will also monitor daily performance closely following last week's WoW decline to identify any momentum recovery patterns and respond quickly with budget adjustments.
