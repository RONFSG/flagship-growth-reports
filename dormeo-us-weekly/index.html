Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo US closed Week 39 with $102,606 in total sales and 279 units sold, maintaining strong six-figure revenue for the week. PPC drove $49,245 of that total at an efficient 5.68x ROAS, reflecting solid paid channel performance. A TACOS of 8.4% and a CVR of 2.91% further underscore healthy conversion quality across the account, with Sunday September 27 serving as the strongest sales day of the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 17.6% and ad CTR at 0.55% are both active areas of optimization as we work to sharpen efficiency and engagement across campaigns. We are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand headline and imagery adjustments — to improve click-through quality and drive ACOS lower over time. The WoW dip of -1.1% is modest and expected to normalize as these campaign refinements take effect.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $8,663 in ad spend generating $49,245 in PPC revenue, the account is producing strong returns at a 5.68x ROAS, and we are proactively refining keyword targeting and bid allocations to push efficiency further. We are identifying lower-performing ad groups and reallocating budget toward top-converting terms to protect and grow our category position. These adjustments are designed to reduce wasted spend while sustaining the revenue momentum we have built through September.
►
Actions This Week — We will review and adjust bids on underperforming keywords to bring ACOS closer to our target range while protecting high-converting traffic. Ad creative testing across Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand formats will continue this week with a focus on improving ad CTR beyond 0.55%. We will also analyze Sunday's peak performance data to identify what drove conversion lift and apply those learnings to mid-week campaign pacing.
