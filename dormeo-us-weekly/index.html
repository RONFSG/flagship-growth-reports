Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo US closed Week 40 with $86,550 in total sales and 243 units sold, supported by strong ad efficiency with a 4.66x ROAS and a healthy TACoS of 10.8%. PPC drove $43,629 in revenue against $9,354 in ad spend, and Sunday October 4 emerged as the peak sales day, capping the week on a strong note.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 21.4% and ad CTR at 0.46% are both areas we are actively working to improve through ongoing campaign refinements and ad creative testing within the ad platform. The 15.6% WoW decline is being monitored in the context of normal weekly volume fluctuation, and we are focused on building momentum heading into Week 41. As optimizations take hold, we expect both conversion efficiency and click quality to strengthen in the coming days.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $9,354 in ad spend generating $43,629 in PPC revenue, the 4.66x ROAS reflects a solid and defensible spend structure that we are actively fine-tuning for further improvement. We are reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward the highest-converting placements and reduce investment in lower-efficiency terms, including any areas where branded category competition may be inflating costs. These adjustments are designed to improve CVR beyond the current 2.59% while sustaining our market position and revenue trajectory.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations to improve ad CTR from its current 0.46%, focusing on headline messaging and ad imagery within the ad platform. Budget will be reallocated toward top-performing keywords and match types to drive ACOS closer to target while protecting PPC revenue. We will also analyze Sunday's peak-day performance data to identify audience and placement patterns worth scaling into the rest of Week 41.
