Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo US grew Total Sales to $64,783 for Week 46, up 6.2% from $61,013 the prior week, on 195 units. The King Octaspring Topper led the lift at $14,621 (+11.7%) with units up 18.5% to 32 and conversion climbing to 4.4%, while the Queen Topper reached $23,860 (+3.1%) on 64 units (+10.3%). Saturday, Nov 14 was the peak day at $15,968.
►
Metrics to Watch — Organic sales eased to $24,367 (-7.1%) mainly because more of the week's demand was captured through ads, with PPC revenue up 16.2% to $40,415. The Twin Topper slipped to $3,049 (-21.5%) as its conversion dipped to 2.5% even though sessions grew 30.0% to 446, while the Full and Twin XL held units steady at 28 and 15 with conversion improving to 4.1% and 8.2%. We are focusing ad traffic on the Twin's best-converting search terms to turn its added sessions into sales.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend was stepped up to $5,897 (prior $4,367) to capture more demand into the weekend peak, and it delivered PPC revenue of $40,415 (+16.2%) at a healthy 14.6% ACOS. ROAS moved to 6.85x from 7.96x and TACoS to 9.1% from 7.16%, so we are trimming bids on lower-converting terms to bring TACoS back toward last week's level. Ad CTR was 0.80% with ad conversion at 2.87%.
►
Actions This Week — We will shift budget toward the Queen and King Topper campaigns, where conversion is running at 3.6% and 4.4%, to build on their unit gains. On the Twin Topper we will narrow targeting to the search terms that convert, aiming to lift its conversion back above 2.5%. We will also test new Sponsored Brand ad creative to push ad CTR above 0.80% while holding ACOS near 14.6%.
