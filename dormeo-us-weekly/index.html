Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo US grew total sales to $103,106 in Week 47, up 48.8% from $69,311 the prior week, on 318 units. Organic sales led the way at $48,486 (+88.1% WoW). The Queen Topper anchored the week at $39,417 (+54.4%) on 117 units with a 5.4% conversion rate, the King Topper climbed to $25,938 (+63.8%) with a 7.0% conversion rate, and the Twin Topper more than doubled to $7,205 (+119.1%). Saturday, Nov 21 was the peak day at $21,120.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Full Topper eased to $9,721 (-11.3%) on 25 units, driven primarily by a 47.3% drop in sessions to 416. The silver lining is that shoppers who did land converted better: Full conversion rose to 6.0% (+69.3%). Queen sessions also eased 3.1% to 2,157, but its conversion gain more than offset that. We are rebuilding Full Topper traffic so its stronger conversion turns into volume again.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ads became more efficient as they scaled. ROAS improved to 8.07x from 7.40x and TACoS fell to 6.6% from 8.49%. Ad spend of $6,767 (prior $5,885) drove PPC revenue of $54,619 (+25.5%) at a 12.4% ACOS, with a 0.75% ad CTR and 4.11% ad conversion.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Raise ad visibility on the Full Topper to win back sessions from 416 toward last week's level, leaning on its 6.0% conversion rate. (2) Shift incremental budget to the King (7.0% CVR) and Twin XL (9.5% CVR) while holding ACOS near 12.4%. (3) Test new Sponsored Brands ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.75%.
