Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo US grew total sales to $69,311 in Week 46, up 12.5% from $61,625 the prior week, on 195 units. The Queen Octaspring Topper led at $25,536 (+10.3%) on 64 units, and the King Topper delivered the biggest lift at $15,834 (+20.3%) with units up 18.5% to 32 and conversion rising to 4.4%. Organic sales also grew 7.7% to $28,895, and Saturday, Nov 14 was the peak day at $17,254.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Twin Topper eased to $3,289 (-15.4%) on 11 units as its conversion dipped to 2.5%, even though its sessions grew 30.0% to 446, so the traffic is there and the opportunity is in converting it. Elsewhere the lineup held up well: the Full Topper reached $10,953 (+2.3%) with conversion up to 4.1%, and the Twin XL held at $4,875 with conversion improving to 8.2%. We are focusing Twin ad traffic on its best-converting search terms to turn those extra sessions into orders.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend rose to $5,897 (prior $4,367) and returned $40,415 in PPC revenue (+16.2%) at a healthy 14.6% ACOS, with ad CTR at 0.80% and ad conversion at 2.87%. ROAS moved to 6.85x from 7.96x and TACoS to 8.5% from 7.09%, so we are trimming bids on lower-converting terms to bring TACoS back toward last week's level while keeping the sales growth.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Shift budget toward the Queen and King Topper campaigns, converting at 3.6% and 4.4%, to build on their unit gains heading into the holiday season. (2) Narrow Twin Topper targeting to its converting search terms, aiming to lift its conversion back above 2.5%. (3) Test new Sponsored Brands ad creative to push ad CTR above 0.80% while holding ACOS near 14.6%.
