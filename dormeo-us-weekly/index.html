Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Dormeo US delivered $75,173 in total sales across 230 units for Week 42, with PPC driving $36,029 in revenue at a strong 3.74x ROAS. Sunday October 18 stood out as the peak sales day, capping off a week where paid channels continued to perform efficiently relative to spend. TACoS of 12.8% reflects a healthy ratio of ad spend to total revenue, reinforcing that the overall business is generating meaningful organic contribution alongside paid performance.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 26.8% and ad CTR at 0.59% are both active areas of optimization as campaigns are refined following the post-tentpole slowdown period. Reduced ad spend during this phase naturally shifts the focus toward higher-efficiency placements, and we expect ACOS to trend more favorably as lower-performing campaigns are deprioritized. CVR at 2.54% remains a solid baseline, and continued bid adjustments toward converting audiences will help sustain that rate as traffic patterns normalize.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $9,643 deployed this week, ad spend has been deliberately pulled back in line with the post-tentpole demand cool-down, and the resulting 3.74x ROAS confirms the remaining spend is working efficiently. We are actively reallocating budget toward top-performing campaigns and highest-converting placements to protect revenue share while maintaining cost discipline. This measured approach positions Dormeo US to scale back up strategically as category demand recovers in the coming weeks.
►
Actions This Week — Continue monitoring ROAS and TACoS daily to identify the right inflection point for incrementally scaling spend back up as demand normalizes. Test ad creative variations within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve ad CTR from its current 0.59% and drive more efficient traffic. Prioritize budget concentration in campaigns with demonstrated conversion efficiency to maintain strong CVR above 2.5% through the remainder of the recovery period.
