►
Weekly Performance — RationAle Brewing generated $645.66 in PPC revenue on $357.25 in ad spend this week, delivering a ROAS of 1.81x across 51 orders. ►
MTD Pacing — With $357.25 in weekly spend logged, MTD pacing should be reviewed against the monthly budget cap to ensure the brand does not outpace allocation given the recent spend acceleration. The current 1.81x ROAS is trending below the prior week benchmark of 1.94x, which may pressure MTD blended returns if efficiency does not recover. Budget reallocation toward higher-converting placements should be prioritized to protect MTD ROAS.
►
Ad Efficiency — ACoS at 55.3% is elevated and signals that current keyword or item-level targeting is driving spend without proportional return, particularly given a category like non-alcoholic beer where basket size may be limited. CPC of $0.73 is relatively efficient, and CVR of 10.5% from 487 clicks to 51 orders is a strong conversion signal showing shoppers who do click are buying. The primary lever to improve ACoS is tightening keyword targeting to reduce low-converting impression volume rather than pulling back spend broadly.
►
Actions This Week — Pull a search term report and identify keywords with zero orders and more than $5.00 in spend, then apply negative keywords or reduce bids by 30-40% on those terms to relieve ACoS pressure.
