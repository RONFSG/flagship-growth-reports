Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoev8er Canada closed Week 30 with $4,413 in total sales across 94 units sold, anchored by a strong Sunday July 19 peak day. PPC drove $2,807 in revenue at a solid 3.07x ROAS, reflecting strong buyer intent from our sponsored campaigns. CVR held at an impressive 9.63%, confirming that shoppers who engage with our ads are converting at a healthy rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 32.6% and TACoS at 20.7% are areas we are actively working to bring tighter as campaigns are refined this week. CTR at 0.35% on our ad creative has room to grow, and we are testing headline and ad copy variations within the ad platform to drive higher engagement from impressions. With CVR already strong at 9.63%, improving ad CTR will allow us to convert a greater share of our existing traffic and improve overall efficiency in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $915 generated $2,807 in PPC revenue, and we are proactively adjusting bids and targeting to improve the return on every dollar deployed. We are focusing on pulling back on lower-performing keywords and reallocating budget toward the placements and match types delivering the strongest ROAS. These adjustments are designed to tighten ACOS while protecting our category visibility and sustaining the strong conversion performance we built this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS this week, pausing or reducing bids on terms exceeding target thresholds while scaling spend behind top-converting placements. Ad creative testing will continue within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, focusing on headline copy and ad imagery variations to lift ad CTR above the current 0.35%. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to ensure the $915 spend level is allocated efficiently across the full week rather than concentrating on a single peak day.
