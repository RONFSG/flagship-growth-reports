Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r US delivered $44,883 in total sales for Week 45, up 4.0% from $43,170 the prior week, on 1,555 units. Organic revenue grew 15.2% to $26,939, led by the 8oz Cleaning Solution at $2,997 (+46.4%) and the 16oz Cleaning Solution at $2,974 (+28.4%). Sunday, Nov 1 was the peak day at $7,295.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Essential Kit eased to $13,237 (-5.8%) as sessions fell 18.6% to 1373, although its CVR improved to 24.8% (+15.4%). The Signature Kit held steady at $6,674 (-0.7%) while sessions jumped 91.7% to 2068 and CVR dipped to 5.9% (-48.2%), so we are reviewing the traffic driving visits to this listing.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $5,662, down from $6,638, generated $17,943 in PPC revenue for a 3.17x ROAS (prior 2.98x) and 31.6% ACOS. TACoS improved to 12.6% from 15.38%, and ad conversion is strong at 17.02% on a 0.30% ad CTR.
►
Actions This Week — We will reinvest part of the ad savings into the 8oz and 16oz Cleaning Solutions, where CVR reached 29.7% and 44.9%, and refine Signature Kit targeting toward higher-intent search terms to lift its 5.9% CVR. We will also test new Sponsored Brand and video ad creative to raise ad CTR above 0.30% and grow PPC revenue back from $17,943 (-9.3%).
