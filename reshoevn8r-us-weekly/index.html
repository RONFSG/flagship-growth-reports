►

►
Metrics to Watch — The 7.1% WoW dip in total sales is a natural fluctuation we are actively monitoring as we head into the September stretch. ACOS at 25.6% is an area we are working to tighten through continued bid refinement and spend reallocation toward top-performing keywords, with improvement expected as those adjustments take hold this week. TACoS of 15.8% reflects a healthy overall spend-to-revenue ratio and gives us a solid baseline to work from as optimizations compound.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $7,851 in ad spend generating $30,668 in PPC revenue, the 3.91x ROAS confirms our campaigns are working efficiently and delivering strong returns relative to investment. Ad CTR at 0.43% presents an opportunity we are actively addressing through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing — refining ad copy and imagery variations within the ad platform to improve click volume on existing spend. These adjustments are designed to protect our category positioning while driving incremental efficiency gains in the weeks ahead.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue bid optimizations on high-ACOS keywords, reallocating that budget toward proven converters to bring ACOS closer to our target range while sustaining revenue momentum. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be a priority focus to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.43% and generate more clicks from existing ad spend. We will also monitor daily pacing closely following the Sunday peak to ensure budget allocation is front-weighted on high-traffic days throughout the week.
