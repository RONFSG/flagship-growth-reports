Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r US delivered $44,570 in total sales for Week 45, up 3.5% from $43,082 the prior week, on 1,543 units. Organic revenue grew 13.5% to $26,306, led by the 8oz Cleaning Solution at $2,997 (+46.7%) and the 16oz Cleaning Solution at $2,944 (+27.0%). Sunday, Nov 1 was the peak day at $7,295.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Essential Kit eased to $13,237 (-5.8%) with sessions down 2.9% to 1637 and CVR at 20.8%. The Signature Kit held steady at $6,625 (-1.4%) while sessions jumped 104.9% to 2211 and CVR dipped to 5.5% (-52.0%), so we are reviewing the traffic driving visits to this listing.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $5,659, down from $6,637, generated $18,264 in PPC revenue for a 3.23x ROAS (prior 3.00x) and 31.0% ACOS. TACoS improved to 12.7% from 15.41%, and ad conversion is strong at 17.43% on a 0.30% ad CTR.
►
Actions This Week — We will reinvest part of the ad savings into the 8oz and 16oz Cleaning Solutions, where CVR reached 25.1% and 37.5%, and refine Signature Kit targeting toward higher-intent search terms to lift its 5.5% CVR. We will also test new Sponsored Brand and video ad creative to raise ad CTR above 0.30% and grow PPC revenue back from $18,264 (-8.3%).
