Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 39 delivered $53,478 in total sales across 1,802 units sold, marking a solid +3.6% week-over-week gain that demonstrates continued momentum heading into fall. PPC contributed $29,897 in revenue at a strong 4.59x ROAS, confirming that paid campaigns are driving meaningful, efficient growth. A CVR of 23.34% reflects strong purchase intent from shoppers reaching the listing, a clear indicator of healthy product-market fit.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR came in at 0.38%, and as we continue refining Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative — including headline variations and ad imagery — we expect to steadily improve click volume and qualified traffic over the coming weeks. ACOS at 21.8% and TACoS at 12.2% are within an acceptable range and reflect the natural balance between scale and efficiency at our current spend level of $6,509. Continued bid and targeting optimizations are underway and expected to bring both metrics tighter as the campaigns mature.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $6,509 in ad spend generating $29,897 in PPC revenue at a 4.59x ROAS, the campaigns are delivering strong returns and maintaining a competitive position in the shoe care category. We are actively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward the highest-efficiency placements and reduce spend on lower-converting terms. These adjustments are designed to improve ACOS incrementally while sustaining the sales velocity and market visibility we have built through Week 39.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including updated ad copy and headline formats — to improve ad CTR from its current 0.38% and drive more qualified traffic into the funnel. Bid optimizations will continue at the keyword level with a focus on reducing ACOS from 21.8% by reallocating budget toward terms delivering the strongest conversion efficiency. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to ensure ad spend remains productive through the full week and carries the +3.6% WoW growth trend into Week 40.
