Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r US grew total sales to $46,341, up +12.4% WoW from $41,229, on 1728 units. The Signature Kit was the standout at $10,467 (+89.9%) with units up +119.8% and CVR up +40.3% to 13.8%, while the Waterproof Shoe Spray led the catalog at $11,770 (+7.3%) on 581 units and a 29.8% CVR. Thursday, Nov 19 was the peak day at $8,451, and the week closed strong with $7,736 on Friday and $8,126 on Saturday.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Essential Kit eased to $9,568 (-21.4%), driven mainly by sessions dipping -9.3% to 1491 alongside a CVR of 17.2% (-10.1%). The good news is that the rest of the line more than offset it: organic sales grew +4.5% to $26,044 and PPC revenue rose +24.5% to $20,297. We are directing more traffic back to the Essential Kit to return it to growth next week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend increased to $7,267 (prior $5,111) to capture demand, and it delivered $20,297 in PPC revenue (+24.5%) with a strong 18.03% ad conversion rate and a 0.34% ad CTR. ROAS came in at 2.79x (prior 3.19x) with ACOS at 35.8%, and TACoS moved to 15.7% (prior 12.40%) as we invested ahead of growth; we are now tightening bids on lower-converting terms to bring ROAS back toward 3.19x.
►
Actions This Week — (1) Scale budget behind the Signature Kit, which grew +89.9% with a 13.8% CVR, to keep its momentum. (2) Add targeted Sponsored Products coverage for the Essential Kit to rebuild sessions above 1491 and lift CVR back above 17.2%. (3) Trim high-ACOS search terms to hold ACOS at or below 35.8% while protecting the 18.03% ad conversion rate.
