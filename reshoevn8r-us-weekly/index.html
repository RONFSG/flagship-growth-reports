Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r US delivered $41,584 in total sales for Week 46 on 1428 units. Organic sales grew +1.5% WoW to $25,934, and the Waterproof Shoe Spray led the gains at $11,136 (+11.7%) on 493 units (+9.8%), with CVR up to 28.1%. Sunday, Nov 8 was the peak day at $7,226.
►
Metrics to Watch — The -6.6% WoW easing in total sales (prior week $44,505) was primarily driven by lower ad-attributed volume, with PPC revenue at $15,650 (-17.4%) on ad spend of $5,128 (prior $5,648), while organic sales held up and grew to $25,934. The Signature Kit came in at $5,567 (-16.3%) as sessions fell -53.7% to 1023, though its CVR improved +80.7% to 10.0%, and the Essential Kit held at $12,208 (-7.6%) on steady sessions of 1644 (+0.4%). We are focused on rebuilding Signature Kit traffic to bring volume back.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $5,128 produced $15,650 in PPC revenue for a 3.05x ROAS (prior 3.36x) and 32.8% ACOS, while TACoS improved to 12.3% from 12.69%. Ad conversion is strong at 16.21% on a 0.31% ad CTR, so the priority is recovering ROAS toward 3.36x by tightening low-return targets rather than cutting reach.
►
Actions This Week — We will rebuild traffic to the Signature Kit with targeted Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns to take advantage of its improved 10.0% CVR, and add budget behind the Waterproof Shoe Spray while it converts at 28.1%. We will also test new ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.31% and prune low-converting search terms to push ROAS from 3.05x back toward 3.36x heading into the holiday season.
