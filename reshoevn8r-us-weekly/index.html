►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r closed Week 40 with $49,432 in total sales across 1,674 units sold, supported by a strong ROAS of 3.99x and an impressive CVR of 17.74%. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 25.1% is an active area of optimization, and as we continue refining keyword bids and reallocating budget toward higher-converting ad groups, we expect efficiency gains to build through the coming week. The WoW decline of 7.6% reflects normal week-to-week variability and is not a concern at this stage. Ad CTR at 0.36% presents a clear opportunity, and testing new Sponsored Product ad creative and headline variations within the ad platform will be our lever to improve click volume and overall ad efficiency.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $6,340 in ad spend generating $25,263 in PPC revenue at a 3.99x ROAS, the account is operating efficiently and delivering strong returns on every dollar invested. We are proactively reviewing campaign structure to identify any underperforming placements or keywords where budget can be redeployed toward top-performing segments. These adjustments will help protect our category positioning while pushing TACoS further below the 12.8% mark in the weeks ahead.
►
Actions This Week — We will test updated Sponsored Product ad creative and Sponsored Brand headline copy variations to drive improvement in ad CTR beyond the current 0.36%. Budget will be shifted toward the highest-converting campaigns to maintain CVR strength at 17.74% while applying downward pressure on ACOS. We will also monitor daily pacing closely against last week's Sunday peak to identify mid-week opportunities for bid increases and budget scaling.
