Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r closed Week 45 with $44,997 in total sales and 1,548 units sold, marking a solid +3.8% week-over-week gain that reflects continued momentum heading into November. PPC drove $18,178 in attributed revenue at a strong 3.21x ROAS, and a 17.33% conversion rate confirms that shoppers arriving at the listing are highly purchase-ready. Sunday, November 1 led all days in sales volume, giving the week a strong opening foundation.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 31.1% remains an active area of optimization, and our focus this week is on tightening keyword bids and reallocating spend toward the highest-converting ad groups to bring that figure down incrementally. TACoS of 12.6% reflects a healthy relationship between ad spend and total revenue, and continued organic growth will naturally improve that ratio as the week progresses. Ad CTR at 0.30% is an area where we see near-term upside, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to lift click volume from already-qualified audiences.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $5,660 in ad spend against $18,178 in PPC revenue, the account is generating strong returns while maintaining competitive visibility in a category that sees meaningful branded and non-branded competition this time of year. We are proactively reviewing placement-level performance to ensure budget is concentrated where efficiency is highest, protecting market position while improving margin contribution. These adjustments are designed to sustain the week-over-week growth trend while improving the return profile across all active campaign types.
►
Actions This Week — We will reduce bids on underperforming keywords contributing to ACOS inefficiency and redirect that budget toward top-converting terms that are driving the majority of PPC revenue. Ad creative testing will continue across Sponsored Product campaigns, with a focus on ad copy and visual variations designed to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.30% baseline. We will also monitor daily pacing closely given Sunday's strong performance, ensuring budget availability is maintained through the full week to capture consistent demand across all seven days.
