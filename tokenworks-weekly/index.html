Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — The IDVisor Smart V2 ID Scanner led the week at $14,193 (+61.3%) on 16 units (+77.8%). PPC revenue surged to $9,333 (+247.6%) as ROAS jumped to 12.70x from 3.80x, and Saturday, Nov 21 was the peak day at $7,324. The IDVisor Smart V3 ID Scanner added $1,752 on 2 units.
►
Metrics to Watch — The -8.5% easing in total sales (from $20,693 to $18,926) was primarily driven by the IDVisor Smart Plus ID Scanner, which eased to $2,786 (-68.7%) on 3 units, alongside lower accessory volume from the Charging Cradle at $130 (-75.0%). Organic revenue came in at $9,592 (-46.7%) as more V2 buyers arrived through ads, while paid revenue more than tripled. We are focused on rebuilding Smart Plus volume to restore total sales growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — ROAS improved to 12.70x from 3.80x and ACOS is a lean 7.9%, so every ad dollar is working far harder. Ad spend held steady at $734 (prior $706), keeping TACoS low at 3.9% (prior 3.41%), with a 0.34% ad CTR and a 2.16% ad conversion rate. With efficiency this strong, there is room to expand spend while holding ACOS near 7.9%.
►
Actions This Week — Extend the campaign approach driving the account's 12.70x ROAS to the IDVisor Smart Plus ID Scanner to rebuild it from 3 units. Build dedicated campaigns for the IDVisor Smart V3 ID Scanner to grow on its $1,752 week. Run an ad creative test on the IDVisor Smart V2 to lift ad CTR above 0.34% while keeping ACOS at or below 7.9%.
