How conversion rate is calculated
  1. Conversion rate = units ordered ÷ sessions for each product line in each month (Amazon’s Unit Session Percentage).
  2. Units and sessions come from Sellerise’s summary by ASIN. Lines with several ASINs (sizes or colours) add them together.
  3. All products = total units ÷ total sessions, not an average of the line rates.
  4. YTD = all units {{YTD_RANGE}} ÷ all sessions {{YTD_RANGE}}, so busier months count for more.
  5. {{MTD_MONTH}}* {{MTD_NOTE_SHORT}}
  6. Shading: darker = higher rate. Months with fewer than 50 sessions are greyed out and not shaded, because a few orders swing the rate too far.
