►

►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 121.7% and TACoS at 45.8% reflect the current investment phase of campaign scaling, and both are areas we are actively optimizing as spend becomes more targeted. ►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,511 in ad spend this week, we are making deliberate adjustments to channel budget toward the keywords and placements driving the strongest returns, while maintaining the visibility that supported the 72.7% sales surge. These optimizations are underway and are expected to improve efficiency ratios while protecting the competitive position we have built.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS data to identify and reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds, reallocating that budget toward the highest-converting campaigns. Ad creative testing will be prioritized within the ad platform, focusing on Sponsored Product copy and headline variations to lift ad CTR above the current 0.40% and drive more efficient clicks. We will also monitor daily performance closely relative to Monday's peak to identify which targeting segments are sustaining conversion and scale those accordingly.
