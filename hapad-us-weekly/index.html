Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered a strong Week 38, generating $17,043 in total sales across 1,191 units sold — a 10.5% week-over-week increase that reflects solid and sustained momentum. PPC drove $7,424 in revenue at a healthy 3.68x ROAS, and a CVR of 17.48% confirms that shoppers are converting at an excellent rate once they reach the listing. Monday, September 14 was the peak sales day, providing a strong start to the week that carried performance forward.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR at 0.64% is an area we are actively working to improve through ongoing ad creative testing within the ad platform, including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations to drive higher click volume. ACOS at 27.2% reflects the current investment level needed to compete for visibility, and as campaign optimizations take effect we expect to see continued improvement in efficiency. These are normal levers in an active growth phase, and the strong CVR of 17.48% confirms the underlying demand is there to support the spend.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,019 in ad spend against $17,043 in total sales, TACoS of 11.8% reflects a manageable and competitive investment level as we continue scaling the brand. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to reduce waste on lower-efficiency placements while protecting share on top-converting terms. These adjustments are designed to improve ACOS progressively while sustaining the volume and ranking gains we have built.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.64% and capture more qualified traffic from existing impressions. Keyword-level bid adjustments will be applied to shift spend toward the highest-converting terms and bring ACOS closer to target. We will also monitor Monday performance closely given last week's September 14 peak, to identify whether early-week demand patterns can be reinforced through strategic bid scheduling.
