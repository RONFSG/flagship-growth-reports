Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Organic revenue jumped +22.6% WoW to $14,234, lifting total sales to $18,172 (+2.9%) on 1302 units. The Metatarsal Pad 1/4" Small 6-Pack was the standout at $1,285 (+46.8%) as conversion rose to 19.1% (+67.1%), and the Scaphoid Pad grew to $719 (+30.7%) with a 42.9% conversion rate. Friday, Nov 20 was the peak day at $3,065.
►
Metrics to Watch — PPC revenue eased to $3,938 (-34.9%), primarily driven by a deliberate pullback in ad spend to $1,978 (prior $2,461) — an efficiency-driven move that organic growth more than covered. The Metatarsal Pad 5/16" Medium 1 Pair dipped to $860 (-9.2%) as conversion softened to 27.8% (-15.4%), even though sessions grew +14.6% to 338, so shoppers are still finding it. We are focused on turning that extra traffic back into orders on the 1 Pair.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — TACoS improved to 10.9% from 13.94%, so advertising now takes a smaller share of total sales while organic carries more of the load. ROAS came in at 1.99x (prior 2.46x) with ACOS at 50.2%, a 0.72% ad CTR and a strong 20.50% ad conversion rate. We are trimming low-converting search terms and refreshing ad creative to raise CTR and bring ROAS back toward 2.46x.
►
Actions This Week — Shift ad budget toward the Metatarsal Pad 1/4" Small 6-Pack and the Scaphoid Pad, whose conversion rates of 19.1% and 42.9% make them the most efficient places to add spend. Cut search terms on campaigns running above the 50.2% ACOS to lift ROAS back toward 2.46x. Test new ad creative on the Metatarsal Pad 5/16" Medium 1 Pair to convert its 338 sessions more efficiently.
