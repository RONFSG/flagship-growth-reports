Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 44 delivered $19,132 in total sales across 1,219 units sold, marking a strong +9.7% week-over-week increase that reflects continued momentum heading into the fall selling season. PPC drove $7,424 in revenue at a solid 2.42x ROAS, and a 19.14% conversion rate confirms that shoppers landing on the product are highly motivated to purchase.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 41.3% and TACoS at 16.0% are areas we are actively working to bring tighter as campaign optimizations continue to roll through. CTR on our ad creative came in at 0.66%, and we are testing headline and ad copy variations within the ad platform to improve click efficiency and bring more qualified traffic at a lower cost per click. As these adjustments take effect, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to trend in a favorable direction over the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $3,069 in ad spend against $19,132 in total sales, we are maintaining solid market presence while actively refining where and how that budget is allocated. We are focusing optimization efforts on reducing spend on lower-performing targeting segments while protecting visibility on the highest-converting placements that are driving the strong 19.14% CVR. This proactive rebalancing is designed to improve ROAS above the current 2.42x while sustaining the growth trajectory we saw this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue refining campaign targeting to shift budget toward the highest-converting ad groups and reduce inefficient spend pulling ACOS above our target threshold. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will focus on headline and copy variations aimed at improving the 0.66% ad CTR and driving down cost per click. With Wednesday, October 28 confirmed as the peak sales day, we will also analyze dayparting patterns to ensure bids are optimized around high-conversion windows heading into Week 45.
