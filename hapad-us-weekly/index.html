Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered $15,417 in total sales across 1,018 units sold during Week 37, with PPC driving $7,424 in revenue against a 3.86x ROAS — a strong return demonstrating the efficiency of our ad investment. CVR held at an impressive 18.76%, confirming that shoppers who engage with Hapad listings are converting at a high rate. TACoS of 12.5% reflects a healthy relationship between ad spend and total revenue for the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 25.9% is an area we are actively working to bring down through bid refinements and campaign restructuring, and we expect to see incremental improvement as those adjustments take hold. The 11.0% WoW sales dip is a natural fluctuation we are monitoring closely as we head into the back half of September. With CVR remaining strong at 18.76%, the core product-market fit is solid, and our focus is on driving more high-intent traffic to capitalize on that conversion strength.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $1,924 in ad spend generating $7,424 in PPC revenue, the campaign is producing meaningful returns and we are continuing to fine-tune targeting to maximize every dollar invested. We are actively reviewing keyword-level performance to reduce spend on lower-efficiency terms and reallocate budget toward top-converting placements. These adjustments are designed to improve ACOS from 25.9% while maintaining our competitive visibility within the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine Sponsored Product bids on underperforming keywords to bring ACOS closer to target, while protecting spend on high-converting terms that are sustaining our 3.86x ROAS. Ad creative variations within the Sponsored Product campaigns will be tested this week to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.62% and drive more qualified clicks. We will also analyze Thursday September 10 peak-day data to identify which campaigns and placements drove the strongest results and weight our strategy accordingly heading into Week 38.
