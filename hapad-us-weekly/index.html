Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered a strong Week 39 with $18,627 in total sales and 1,200 units sold, marking a 9.3% week-over-week increase that signals healthy and accelerating momentum. PPC contributed $7,424 in revenue against $2,010 in ad spend, generating an impressive 3.69x ROAS. Thursday, September 24 was the peak sales day, reflecting strong mid-week demand that we will look to capitalize on with targeted scheduling going forward.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR came in at 0.61%, and as we continue testing ad creative variations within the Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns — including headline copy and ad imagery — we expect to see click volume improve in the coming week. ACOS at 27.1% reflects the current investment level needed to sustain our growth trajectory, and as campaign optimizations take hold we anticipate gradual improvement toward our efficiency targets. CVR of 19.27% is a genuine strength and confirms that shoppers who do click are converting at a high rate, which gives us a strong foundation to build on as we drive more qualified traffic.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — TACoS of 10.8% reflects a balanced investment in paid visibility relative to total revenue, and we are actively refining keyword bids and reallocating budget toward our highest-performing ad groups to sharpen that ratio further. At $2,010 in weekly ad spend driving $7,424 in PPC revenue, the core efficiency of the account is solid, and our ongoing adjustments are focused on compounding that return while protecting our market position. We will continue monitoring competitive activity on branded and category terms and make proactive bid adjustments to ensure we are capturing high-intent traffic efficiently.
►
Actions This Week — We will shift additional budget toward the top-performing campaigns that drove Thursday's peak sales day, with the goal of replicating that demand pattern earlier in the week. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands — including headline variations and ad imagery — will be prioritized to push ad CTR above the current 0.61% benchmark. We will also conduct a keyword-level bid review to identify underperforming spend and redirect it toward terms demonstrating strong CVR alignment with our 19.27% account average.
