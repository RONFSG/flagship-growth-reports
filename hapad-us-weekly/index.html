Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Hapad delivered $17,327 in total sales across 1,069 units sold during Week 36, holding steady with just a -0.3% WoW variance — a strong result that reflects consistent consumer demand. PPC drove $7,424 in revenue at a healthy 3.59x ROAS, and Thursday September 3 stood out as the peak sales day of the week. A 20.84% conversion rate continues to signal that shoppers who find Hapad are highly motivated buyers.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 27.9% and TACoS at 11.9% are areas we are actively refining as part of our ongoing campaign optimization cadence. Ad CTR at 0.60% is an opportunity we are addressing through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing to sharpen relevance and improve click efficiency. As these adjustments take effect, we expect both click quality and spend efficiency to trend in a favorable direction this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $2,068 in ad spend generating $7,424 in PPC revenue, the 3.59x ROAS reflects a solid return on invested ad dollars, and we are proactively working to extend that efficiency further. We are reviewing keyword-level performance to reallocate budget toward the highest-converting terms and reduce drag from underperforming placements. These adjustments are designed to protect Hapad's market position and category visibility while improving overall margin contribution.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level bid review, pausing or reducing spend on terms with high ACOS and low conversion contribution, while increasing bids on top-performing, high-CVR keywords. Ad creative variations within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be tested to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.60% benchmark. We will also monitor Thursday pacing closely given its status as last week's peak day, ensuring budget availability is prioritized heading into mid-week.
