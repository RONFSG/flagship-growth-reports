Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 41 delivered a strong $23,696 in total sales across 1,718 units sold, marking a +38.0% week-over-week surge that reflects meaningful momentum heading into Q4. Wednesday, October 7 stood out as the peak sales day, anchoring a consistently strong week across the board. A CVR of 18.57% confirms that shoppers who engaged with Hapad converted at a high rate, signaling strong product-market fit and compelling offer presentation.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 47.5% and TACoS at 14.9% reflect the investment level associated with a high-growth week, and both are expected to improve as we refine targeting and reduce spend on lower-performing keywords. Ad CTR at 0.63% is an area of active focus — we are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform, including headline copy and ad imagery, to drive stronger click engagement from our target audience. As these optimizations take effect, we expect to see CTR trend upward alongside continued CVR strength.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $3,527 in ad spend driving $7,424 in PPC revenue, ROAS came in at 2.10x — a solid foundation that we are actively building on through campaign-level bid adjustments and keyword pruning. We are prioritizing spend toward the highest-converting segments while pulling back on terms that are not contributing efficiently to revenue, a process that is already underway heading into Week 42. These proactive steps are designed to improve ROAS and reduce ACOS while protecting Hapad's competitive visibility in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue tightening bids on underperforming keywords and reallocating that budget toward proven, high-converting ad groups to improve ROAS beyond the current 2.10x. Ad creative testing within the ad platform — specifically headline variations and ad imagery — will be prioritized to push ad CTR above the current 0.63% benchmark. With CVR already performing at 18.57%, the near-term focus is on bringing more qualified traffic in at a lower cost-per-click to drive further efficiency gains in Week 42.
