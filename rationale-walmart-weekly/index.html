Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — RationAle delivered $5,057 in total sales across 292 units sold during Week 31, maintaining strong consumer demand on Walmart. Saturday August 1 stood out as the peak sales day, reflecting solid weekend momentum. Conversion rate held at an impressive 14.11%, confirming that shoppers who engage with RationAle are highly motivated buyers.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 62.3% and TACoS at 16.1% are areas we are actively optimizing as we refine campaign targeting and bid efficiency. The modest 3.0% WoW dip is a normal fluctuation we are already accounting for in our weekly pacing adjustments. As we continue tightening keyword bids and reallocating spend toward higher-converting placements, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $815 in ad spend generating $1,310 in PPC revenue at a 1.61x ROAS, we are actively working to push that return higher through smarter spend allocation. Ad CTR currently sits at 0.59%, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the Walmart ad platform — to strengthen click-through performance. These adjustments are designed to improve efficiency while protecting RationAle's visibility and competitive positioning in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will reduce bids on underperforming keywords driving high spend with low conversion and redirect that budget toward top-converting search terms to improve ROAS and bring ACOS down. Ad creative testing on Sponsored Product campaigns will be prioritized to lift ad CTR beyond the current 0.59% baseline. We will also analyze Saturday's peak-day data to identify winning placements and audience signals that can be scaled earlier in the week to smooth out daily sales distribution.
