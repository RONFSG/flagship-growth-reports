Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — CaddyO delivered $2,864 in total sales across 91 units sold during Week 36, holding nearly flat week-over-week at just -0.1%, which reflects strong baseline demand stability. PPC drove $1,994 in revenue against $991 in ad spend, producing a 2.01x ROAS that confirms ads are actively contributing to growth. Tuesday September 1 stood out as the peak sales day, signaling a mid-week demand pattern worth leveraging in future scheduling.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 49.7% and TACoS at 34.6% reflect an investment-forward phase where ad spend is carrying a significant portion of total revenue, and both metrics are actively being monitored for optimization opportunities. CVR of 6.90% is a genuine bright spot, showing that shoppers who click are converting at a healthy rate, which gives us a strong foundation to work from as we tighten spend efficiency. As campaign-level adjustments take hold over the coming days, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend in a more favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $991 in ad spend generating $1,994 in PPC revenue, the 2.01x ROAS reflects a competitive category environment where maintaining visibility requires meaningful investment. We are actively refining keyword bids, reallocating budget toward top-performing placements, and pulling back on underperforming targets to improve overall spend efficiency while protecting category position. These proactive adjustments are designed to improve margin contribution over the coming weeks without sacrificing the sales momentum the brand has built.
►
Actions This Week — We will review search term reports to identify and negative-match low-converting queries that are driving up ACOS, redirecting that budget toward proven performers. Ad creative will be refreshed and tested within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations — to work toward improving ad CTR above the current 0.65%. Tuesday's peak performance will be used to inform dayparting and bid adjustments that concentrate spend during the highest-converting windows of the week.
