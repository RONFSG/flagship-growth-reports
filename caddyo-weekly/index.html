►

►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 47.8% and TACoS at 30.8% reflect an active investment phase, and we are focused on tightening keyword targeting and shifting budget toward the highest-converting placements to bring these figures down. We expect ACOS and TACoS to improve as campaign refinements take effect over the coming week.
►

These shifts are expected to strengthen the ROAS trend while maintaining competitive visibility in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword bids and pause low-performing search terms to reduce wasted spend and bring ACOS closer to target. Ad creative variations within Sponsored Products will be tested to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.86%, focusing on copy and imagery within the ad platform. Budget will be redistributed toward the top-converting campaigns to protect unit velocity and build on the strong CVR signal already in place.
