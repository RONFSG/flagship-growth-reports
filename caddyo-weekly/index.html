Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — CaddyO closed Week 36 with $2,864 in total sales across 91 units sold, holding virtually flat week-over-week at just -0.1%, which reflects strong baseline demand stability. PPC drove $1,994 in revenue against $991 in ad spend, and a CVR of 6.90% confirms that shoppers landing on the product are converting at a healthy rate. Tuesday, September 1 stood out as the peak sales day, indicating mid-week momentum worth monitoring as we head into Week 37.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 49.7% and TACoS at 34.6% remain elevated, and bringing these figures down is a clear focus area as we continue refining campaign targeting and bid efficiency. CVR at 6.90% is a genuine strength in the funnel, meaning the primary opportunity is driving higher-quality traffic at a lower cost-per-click rather than any issue with conversion. As campaign optimizations take hold over the coming week, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend in a more favorable direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At a 2.01x ROAS, ads are generating positive returns, and our focus this week is on stretching that efficiency further by reallocating spend toward the highest-performing keywords and ad creatives. Ad CTR of 0.65% represents an opportunity to improve click quality, and we will be testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to identify combinations that drive stronger engagement. These proactive adjustments are designed to improve cost efficiency while maintaining the visibility and sales velocity that support our competitive position in the category.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level bid review to reduce spend on terms contributing to elevated ACOS while protecting top-converting placements that are driving the strong 6.90% CVR. Sponsored Product ad creative testing will be initiated to work toward improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.65%, focusing on ad copy and visual variations within the Amazon ad platform. We will also monitor daily pacing closely following the Tuesday peak-day pattern, ensuring budget is allocated to capture mid-week demand as it builds through Week 37.
