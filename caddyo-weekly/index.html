Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — CaddyO closed Week 37 with $2,663 in total sales across 88 units sold, with PPC driving $1,447 of that revenue. Wednesday, September 9 stood out as the peak sales day, and a 4.90% conversion rate reflects solid shopper intent and strong product-page performance for visitors who clicked through.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 67.5% and TACoS at 36.7% are areas we are actively working to improve through ongoing campaign refinements and bid adjustments. The 7.0% WoW dip in total sales is within a manageable range and gives us a clear signal to tighten targeting and reduce wasted spend on lower-performing keywords. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both efficiency metrics to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $977 in ad spend generating a 1.48x ROAS, we are focused on improving return through smarter budget allocation across campaigns. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift spend toward higher-converting terms and pause or reduce bids on placements that are not contributing efficiently. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position while steadily improving the revenue-to-spend ratio.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword bids at the campaign level, pausing or reducing spend on terms with high spend and low conversion contribution, while increasing bids on proven converters to capitalize on the strong 4.90% CVR. Ad creative testing within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations — will be explored to improve the 0.61% ad CTR and drive more qualified clicks. We will also set daily spend guardrails to ensure budget is concentrated during high-performing time windows, using Wednesday's peak-day pattern as a scheduling reference point.
