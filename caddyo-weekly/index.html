Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — CaddyO moved 88 units and generated $2,663 in total sales during Week 37, with PPC contributing $1,447 in revenue — a strong indication that paid campaigns are actively driving purchase intent. Wednesday September 9 stood out as the peak sales day, showing clear mid-week momentum worth building on. CVR held at 4.90%, reflecting solid shopper-to-buyer conversion when traffic reaches the listing.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 67.5% and TACoS at 36.7% are areas we are actively working to improve through campaign-level bid adjustments and keyword pruning to shift spend toward higher-performing terms. The 7.0% WoW dip in total sales is being addressed by reallocating budget toward the ad groups and ASINs that showed the strongest return this week. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both ACOS and overall revenue efficiency to trend in a better direction in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $977 in ad spend against $1,447 in PPC revenue, the current ROAS of 1.48x reflects a period of active campaign refinement rather than a settled state. We are making targeted adjustments to reduce spend on underperforming keywords while protecting visibility on the terms driving the majority of conversions. These moves are designed to improve efficiency while maintaining the market presence needed to sustain sales volume and category positioning.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS to identify and reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds, reallocating that budget toward the highest-converting ad groups. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products — including headline copy variations and ad image selection — will be evaluated to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.61%. We will also analyze Wednesday's peak-day data to identify which campaigns were active and performing well, and ensure those are adequately funded heading into next week.
