Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — CaddyO delivered $1,841 in total sales for Week 45, up 21.2% from $1,518 the prior week, on 66 units. Organic sales grew 232.5% to $552, led by the Blue Wrapsulator at $513 (+81.1%) and a strong lift from the Dual Travel Tote at $134 (+217.6%). Wednesday, Nov 4 was the peak day at $301.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Black Wrapsulator eased to $185 (-29.6%) as its CVR slipped to 4.3% even though sessions rose 59.4%. We are watching its conversion closely and will support it with tighter ad targeting so it can return to its prior velocity alongside the rest of the Wrapsulator line.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend rose to $1,882 from $692, generating $1,288 in PPC revenue for a 0.68x ROAS and 146.1% ACOS, with TACoS at 102.2%. Ad CTR is 0.27% with a 2.58% ad conversion rate, so our priority this week is bringing spend back in line with returns while protecting the organic gains.
►
Actions This Week — We will reduce bids on high-ACOS targets and pull budget back toward the prior $692 level, concentrating spend on proven converters like the Blue Wrapsulator (6.5% CVR) and the Slim Can Cooler (8.2% CVR). We will also test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.27%.
