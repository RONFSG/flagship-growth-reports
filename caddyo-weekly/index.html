Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — CaddyO moved 64 units and generated $2,029 in total sales during Week 38, with PPC driving $1,160 of that revenue. Wednesday September 16 stood out as the peak sales day, showing strong mid-week demand that we can build targeting strategies around going forward.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 55.5% and TACoS at 31.7% reflect campaigns that are actively being refined to improve spend efficiency as we scale. CVR of 6.16% is a genuine strength — shoppers who reach the listing are converting at a healthy rate, which gives us a strong foundation to work from. As we tighten keyword targeting and shift budget toward higher-performing placements, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend downward in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At a ROAS of 1.80x on $644 in ad spend, we are in active optimization mode — identifying underperforming keywords and reallocating budget toward placements that are driving the most efficient returns. The WoW dip of 23.8% gives us a clear signal to sharpen our targeting rather than broadly scale, which is exactly the approach we are taking this week. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position while improving the ratio of revenue generated per ad dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords with high spend and low conversion contribution, redirecting that budget toward the placements and match types that supported Wednesday's peak performance. Ad CTR of 0.58% will be addressed through Sponsored Product ad creative testing — specifically rotating ad copy variations and imagery within the ad platform to improve click-through rates. We will also set tighter dayparting and bid adjustments to concentrate spend during the mid-week window where demand has proven strongest.
