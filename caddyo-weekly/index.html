Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — The Black Wrapsulator Can Cooler was the standout of Week 46, more than doubling to $434 (+114.3%) on 15 units at a 12.9% conversion rate, and the B0G3TDWNYR Wrapsulator doubled to $173 (+100.0%) at a 12.2% conversion rate. CaddyO delivered $1,653 in total sales on 56 units, with Sunday, Nov 8 the peak day at $318.
►
Metrics to Watch — Total sales eased to $1,653 from $2,073 (-20.3% WoW) as ad spend came down to $1,157 from $1,881 and PPC revenue moved to $1,140 (-14.2%), while organic sales came in at $513 (-31.1% WoW). Among top products, the Blue Wrapsulator ($289, -50.0%) and the B0G3T5N7YB Wrapsulator ($173, -45.5%) saw the largest declines, converting at 4.0% and 3.8% on 248 and 157 sessions. On the positive side, every ad dollar worked harder this week, and we are focused on lifting conversion on these two high-traffic coolers to restore volume.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — ROAS improved to 0.99x from 0.71x and TACoS fell to 70.0% from 90.72%, as $1,157 in ad spend generated $1,140 in PPC revenue. ACOS sits at 101.5% with a 0.29% ad CTR and a 3.86% ad conversion rate, so efficiency is moving the right way and we are continuing to tighten spend toward profitable targets.
►
Actions This Week — We will shift budget toward the Black, B0G3TDWNYR and Camo Wrapsulators, which converted at 12.9%, 12.2% and 17.4%, and trim bids on low-converting search terms for the Blue and B0G3T5N7YB Wrapsulators to bring ACOS below 101.5%. We will also test new Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.29% heading into the holiday season.
