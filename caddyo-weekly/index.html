Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 44 delivered $1,632 in total sales across 57 units sold, marking a strong +14.8% week-over-week increase that demonstrates clear momentum heading into the fall stretch. PPC drove $1,295 of that revenue at a 1.87x ROAS, with Thursday October 29 standing out as the peak sales day of the week. CVR of 5.46% reflects solid shopper intent and confirms that traffic reaching the listing is converting at a healthy rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 53.5% and TACoS at 42.5% remain elevated relative to target, and we are actively working through campaign-level optimizations to bring spend in line with revenue generated. As bid adjustments are applied to lower-performing keywords and budget is reallocated toward proven converters, we expect both metrics to trend downward in the coming week. CTR on ad creative is currently 0.38%, and testing refined ad copy and imagery variations within the ad platform is underway to improve click efficiency.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $693 in ad spend against $1,632 in total sales, we are investing meaningfully in visibility during a competitive seasonal window, and we are actively adjusting targeting parameters to improve return on that investment. Proactive bid pruning on inefficient placements and shifting budget toward high-converting ad groups will help tighten ROAS while sustaining the sales momentum we built this week. These optimizations are designed to protect our category positioning while progressively improving margin contribution over the coming weeks.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level audit within the ad platform to pause or reduce bids on terms driving spend without conversions, with the goal of pulling ACOS closer to the 40% range. Ad creative testing will be prioritized, including headline and imagery variations within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns to lift the 0.38% ad CTR. Budget savings recovered from inefficient terms will be reallocated to top-converting keywords to sustain the +14.8% WoW growth trajectory into Week 45.
