Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — CaddyO moved 57 units and generated $1,710 in total sales during Week 42, with PPC contributing $984 in revenue against a $705 ad spend. Tuesday October 13 stood out as the peak sales day, showing strong mid-week demand that we can continue to build on heading into the back half of October.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 71.6% and TACoS at 41.2% reflect an active investment phase where we are prioritizing visibility and scaling learnings across campaigns. As we refine keyword targeting and shift budget toward the highest-converting ad groups, we expect both metrics to trend more efficiently in the coming week. CVR at 4.32% is a genuine strength and confirms that shoppers who click through are converting at a healthy rate.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $705 in ad spend delivering a 1.40x ROAS, we are actively working to improve return by reallocating budget away from underperforming keywords and doubling down on placements that are driving conversions. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product campaigns — including headline variations and ad copy — is underway to improve ad CTR from its current 0.51% and pull more qualified traffic through at lower cost. These adjustments are designed to strengthen efficiency while maintaining the market presence we have built.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on low-converting search terms identified from last week's campaign data and redirect that spend toward top-performing keywords driving the strongest CVR. Ad CTR improvement will be pursued through Sponsored Product creative testing, including ad copy and headline variations within the ad platform. We will also monitor Tuesday performance closely given its role as last week's peak day to identify whether mid-week momentum can be amplified with targeted bid increases.
