►
Weekly Highlights — Total sales grew to $2,000 (+21.0% WoW) on 68 units, with organic revenue more than tripling to $960 (+203.6% WoW). ►
Metrics to Watch — PPC revenue eased to $1,039 (-22.2%), driven by ad spend being cut to $592 (prior $1,157), a deliberate efficiency move that organic growth more than made up for. The Black Wrapsulator eased to $398 (-8.3%) even as units rose +6.7% to 16 and conversion improved to 13.9%. The standard Wrapsulator held at $166 (-4.2%) on sessions down -34.4%, with conversion up +77.8% to 6.8%. We are focused on rebuilding traffic across the Wrapsulator range.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — TACoS fell sharply to 29.6% from 69.99% and ROAS improved to 1.75x from 1.16x, so each ad dollar is working much harder. ACOS came in at 57.0% with a 0.38% ad CTR and a 6.33% ad conversion rate. Spend is being directed to the SKUs converting best to keep ROAS climbing above 1.75x.
►
Actions This Week — Shift budget toward the Black Wrapsulator to capitalize on its 13.9% conversion. Relaunch targeted campaigns on the standard Wrapsulator's best terms to recover its -34.4% session dip. Keep pruning low-converting search terms to bring ACOS below 57.0%.
