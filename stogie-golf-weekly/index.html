Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Stogie Golf moved 27 units during Week 37, generating $437 in total sales with PPC contributing $343 of that revenue. Tuesday September 8 stood out as the peak sales day, showing strong mid-week momentum that can be built upon heading into the back half of September.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 165.5% and TACoS at 129.9% reflect a period of active campaign investment, and we are working through bid and targeting adjustments to bring efficiency in line with growth goals. CVR at 5.51% is a genuine bright spot, confirming that shoppers who engage with the ads are converting at a healthy rate. As we tighten spend on underperforming keywords and shift budget toward what is already converting, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to improve meaningfully this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $567 in ad spend against $437 in total sales, we are in an active optimization phase focused on reallocating budget away from low-return placements and toward the targeting segments driving real conversions. The 0.60x ROAS gives us a clear baseline to measure improvement against as campaign refinements take effect. We are prioritizing spend efficiency while maintaining enough presence to protect category visibility and sustain the conversion momentum already showing in the data.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS this week, pausing or reducing bids on terms exceeding threshold and reinforcing budget behind the converting segments that drove Tuesday's peak. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will be explored to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.90%, focusing on headline variations and ad-level imagery to drive stronger click volume. We will also review match type distribution to ensure broad and phrase terms are not inflating spend without contributing to the 5.51% CVR we are seeing on more targeted placements.
