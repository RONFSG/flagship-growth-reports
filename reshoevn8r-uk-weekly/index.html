Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 32 delivered £5,841 in total sales across 249 units sold, marking a strong +17.0% week-over-week increase that signals genuine momentum for the Reshoevn8r UK storefront. Sunday August 2 was the peak sales day, helping anchor a week where PPC contributed £2,625 in revenue and CVR held at an impressive 12.71%, reflecting strong buyer intent from shoppers reaching the listing.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 52.2% and TACoS at 23.4% remain areas of active focus, and campaign-level bid adjustments are already underway to improve efficiency as we scale. Ad CTR at 0.19% is an ongoing optimization lever, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations and headline copy within the ad platform to improve click-through rates from impressions to visits. As these refinements take hold, we expect both ACOS and CTR to trend in a more favorable direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At £1,370 in ad spend against £2,625 in PPC revenue, the current ROAS of 1.92x reflects a competitive ad environment where maintaining visibility is actively supporting the +17.0% growth trend. We are making targeted adjustments to reduce spend on lower-performing keywords while protecting share on high-converting terms that are driving the strong 12.71% CVR. These optimizations are designed to gradually improve ROAS and bring ACOS down while sustaining the sales momentum built this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine keyword bids by pausing or reducing spend on terms with high ACOS and low conversion contribution, redirecting budget toward the placements and match types driving the strongest returns. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will continue, with new headline and ad copy variations submitted to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.19% benchmark. We will also monitor Sunday performance closely given its status as last week's peak day, ensuring campaigns are structured to capture that same demand pattern in Week 33.
