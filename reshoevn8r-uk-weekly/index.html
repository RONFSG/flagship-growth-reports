Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r UK delivered £2,845 in total sales across 106 units sold during Week 34, with PPC contributing a strong £1,599 in revenue driven by an impressive 11.41% conversion rate. Friday August 21 stood out as the peak trading day, reflecting solid mid-week momentum building into the weekend. The 11.41% CVR is a genuine performance strength, confirming that shoppers who engage with the ads are converting at a healthy and consistent rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 45.3% and TACoS at 25.5% are areas we are actively working to bring down through ongoing bid refinements and keyword-level spend reallocation. The WoW decline of 33.5% is being assessed in the context of prior week performance patterns, and our campaign adjustments this week are designed to rebuild momentum and stabilise revenue week over week. With CVR holding above 11%, the underlying demand signal remains strong and positions us well for a positive recovery.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At £725 in ad spend against £1,599 in PPC revenue, the 2.21x ROAS reflects a campaign structure that is generating returns but has clear room to improve efficiency as we tighten targeting and reduce spend on lower-performing ad groups. We are proactively reallocating budget toward the best-converting keywords and ad placements to strengthen ROAS and bring ACOS closer to target over the coming weeks. These adjustments are aimed at protecting our competitive market position while improving the overall margin contribution of paid traffic.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level bid review to reduce spend on terms driving high ACOS while scaling investment behind converting search terms that are delivering efficiently. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be explored to improve ad CTR from its current 0.17%, focusing on headline variations and ad copy that better capture shopper intent. These combined actions are targeted at improving ROAS, reducing TACoS, and building a stronger revenue base heading into Week 35.
