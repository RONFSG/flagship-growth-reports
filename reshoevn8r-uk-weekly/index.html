Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r UK delivered £2,826 in total sales across 107 units sold in Week 36, with PPC driving £1,325 in attributed revenue at a solid 1.98x ROAS. Monday August 31 stood out as the peak trading day, and a CVR of 10.68% confirms that shoppers landing on the listing are converting at a healthy rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 50.5% and TACoS at 23.7% are areas we are actively working to bring into a tighter range, with campaign-level bid adjustments already in progress. The WoW decline of 31.1% is being monitored in the context of the prior week's performance and is not indicative of a structural shift. As optimizations take hold and spend is directed toward the highest-performing keywords, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favourably through Week 37.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With £668 in ad spend generating £1,325 in PPC revenue, the foundation for efficient scaling is in place, and our focus this week is on improving the return on that investment by reallocating budget toward proven top performers. Ad CTR of 0.13% on Sponsored Product ads signals an opportunity to test stronger ad creative, including headline copy variations and ad imagery within the ad platform, to drive a higher click volume from the same spend. These adjustments are aimed at improving both efficiency and market visibility as we head into the autumn selling period.
►
Actions This Week — We will review keyword-level ACOS data and pause or reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds, redirecting that budget toward keywords with proven conversion history. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will be prioritised this week to address the 0.13% ad CTR and improve qualified traffic volume. We will also assess dayparting performance around Monday peaks to ensure budget availability is concentrated during the highest-converting windows.
