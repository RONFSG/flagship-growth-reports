Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r UK delivered a strong Week 31, generating £4,991 in total sales across 199 units sold — a healthy 5.2% week-over-week increase that signals continued momentum in the market. PPC contributed £2,945 in revenue against £1,131 in ad spend, producing a solid 2.60x ROAS. Sunday July 26 stood out as the peak sales day, and a 14.64% conversion rate reflects strong buyer intent from shoppers reaching the listing.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 38.4% and TACoS at 22.7% are areas we are actively optimizing as campaigns continue to mature and spending becomes more targeted. CTR at 0.20% on the ad side presents a clear opportunity to improve click volume through Sponsored Product ad creative refinements and headline variation testing within the ad platform. As these optimizations take hold over the coming week, we expect gradual improvement in both click efficiency and overall ad profitability.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At £1,131 in weekly ad spend, we are being deliberate about where budget is allocated while protecting our market position in the competitive UK sneaker care category. We are making proactive adjustments to shift spend toward the highest-converting campaigns and reduce investment in lower-efficiency placements. These refinements are designed to bring ROAS higher and TACoS closer to our target range while sustaining the upward sales trajectory we are seeing week over week.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including updated ad copy and imagery within the ad platform — to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.20% and drive more qualified traffic at lower cost. Campaigns delivering above a 38% ACOS will be reviewed for bid reductions or keyword pruning to tighten efficiency without sacrificing visibility on core terms. We will also double down on budget allocation toward the top-performing placements that are driving the strongest conversion rates and contributing to the 5.2% week-over-week growth.
