Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 39 delivered £2,125 in total sales across 72 units sold, representing a strong +15.0% week-over-week growth and confirming positive sales momentum heading into autumn. Saturday September 26 was the peak sales day, indicating strong weekend demand that we can continue to build on. PPC contributed £972 in revenue, demonstrating that paid campaigns are actively driving a meaningful share of overall performance.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 65.8% and TACoS at 30.1% are areas we are actively optimizing, with campaign-level bid adjustments and keyword pruning underway to improve efficiency over the coming week. CVR of 7.82% is a healthy signal that shoppers who click are converting well, which gives us confidence that refining targeting to reach more of the right audience will compound results. As spend becomes more precise, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably while sustaining the +15.0% growth trajectory.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At £639 in ad spend against £972 in PPC revenue, we are generating a 1.52x ROAS, and our focus this week is on improving that ratio by reallocating budget toward the highest-converting keywords and ad placements. We are proactively reviewing campaign structure to ensure spend is concentrated where it delivers the strongest return, particularly given competitive dynamics in the UK sneaker care category. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position while steadily improving the revenue-to-spend relationship.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword bids across Sponsored Product campaigns to reduce inefficient spend on low-converting terms, redirecting that budget toward top performers that are driving the strongest CVR. We will also test ad creative variations within the ad platform — including headline copy and ad imagery — to address the 0.10% ad CTR and work toward improving click volume from our impressions. Additionally, we will analyse Saturday's peak day performance to identify which campaigns and keywords drove the surge, using those insights to replicate that momentum across the full week.
