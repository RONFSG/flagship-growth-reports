Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 30 delivered a strong performance with £4,774 in total sales across 188 units sold, marking a +37.5% week-over-week increase that reflects real momentum in the UK market. PPC drove £2,259 in revenue against £868 in ad spend, generating a solid 2.60x ROAS. Wednesday July 22 stood out as the peak day, suggesting mid-week shopper behaviour is a reliable pattern worth leaning into for future campaign scheduling.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 38.4% and TACoS at 18.2% are areas we are actively working to bring down as campaign optimisation continues across the account. CTR of 0.15% on paid ads reflects an opportunity to sharpen ad creative — we are testing Sponsored Product ad copy and imagery variations to improve click engagement and drive more qualified traffic. As these optimisations take hold, we expect both ACOS and CTR to trend in a more efficient direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With £868 in ad spend generating a 2.60x ROAS and a CVR of 13.73%, the account is converting well once shoppers engage, which confirms the core offering is resonating in the UK market. We are focused on reallocating budget away from lower-performing keywords and ad groups to concentrate spend where efficiency and conversion rates are strongest. This disciplined approach will help protect margin while sustaining the growth trajectory we saw this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will review keyword-level ACOS data to identify and reduce bids on terms pulling efficiency down, while protecting spend on high-converting keywords that are driving the strong 13.73% CVR. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will be expanded — specifically testing headline copy and ad image variations to improve the 0.15% ad CTR. Campaign scheduling will be adjusted to weight budget more heavily toward mid-week, capitalising on the Wednesday peak pattern observed this week.
