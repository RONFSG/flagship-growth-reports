Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reshoevn8r UK delivered £4,281 in total sales across 172 units during Week 33, with PPC driving £2,341 in revenue at a solid 2.19x ROAS. CVR held strong at 13.46%, reflecting healthy purchase intent from shoppers who clicked through to the product, and Monday August 10 stood out as the peak sales day of the week.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 45.6% and TACoS at 24.9% are areas we are actively working to bring down through continued bid refinements and keyword-level pruning. The WoW dip of 10.3% is being addressed through tightened targeting and spend reallocation toward the highest-converting placements. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend downward in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At £1,067 in ad spend against £2,341 in PPC revenue, the campaign is generating returns we can build on as we fine-tune efficiency across active keyword groups. We are proactively reallocating budget away from lower-performing terms and toward placements that are delivering the strongest CVR, which at 13.46% confirms that matched traffic is converting well. These adjustments are designed to improve ROAS while maintaining our competitive presence in the UK cleaning and sneaker care category.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS to identify and reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds, redirecting that spend toward top-converting keywords driving the strongest unit volume. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products will be explored to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.21%, focusing on headline variations and ad imagery within the ad platform. We will also monitor Monday performance closely given its role as peak day, ensuring sufficient budget is allocated early in the week to capitalize on that demand window.
