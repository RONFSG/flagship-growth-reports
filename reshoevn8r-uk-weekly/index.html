Metrics to Watch — The Refresh Kit eased to £208 (-16.0%), primarily driven by sessions falling -19.4% to 129, while its CVR actually improved +7.5% to 10.1%, so shoppers who reach the page are buying. ►
Actions This Week — (1) Push budget toward the 16oz Cleaning Solution (24.1% CVR) and the Shoe Cleaner Kit (9.7% CVR, +93.1%) to compound this week's gains.
