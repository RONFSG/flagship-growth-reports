Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 35 delivered a strong performance with £4,103 in total sales across 163 units sold, marking a +44.2% week-over-week increase and the best sales day falling on Sunday, August 23. PPC drove £2,118 in revenue at a 2.74x ROAS, reflecting solid return on the £772 invested in ads. A CVR of 13.93% confirms that shoppers are converting at a healthy rate once they reach the listing.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 36.5% and TACoS at 18.8% are areas we are actively working to bring down as campaigns mature and spend becomes more targeted. CTR at 0.16% on our ad creative presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad copy and imagery variations within the ad platform to improve click-through rates. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both efficiency metrics to trend in the right direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At £772 in ad spend against £4,103 in total sales, we are generating meaningful revenue while maintaining a proactive eye on efficiency as the account scales. We are making targeted adjustments to reduce waste in lower-performing keyword segments and shift budget toward the placements and match types delivering the strongest ROAS. These changes are designed to protect our market position and sustain the growth momentum shown by this week's +44.2% WoW lift.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue refining keyword bids across Sponsored Product campaigns, pausing underperforming terms and reallocating budget to high-converting placements to bring ACOS closer to target. Ad creative variations will be tested within the ad platform to improve the 0.16% ad CTR and drive more qualified traffic at a lower cost per click. We will also monitor daily performance closely following the strong Sunday peak to identify any mid-week trends worth capitalizing on before Week 36 closes.
