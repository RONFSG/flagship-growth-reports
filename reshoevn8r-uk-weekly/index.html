Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 35 delivered a strong performance for Reshoevn8r UK, with total sales reaching £4,103 across 163 units sold — a remarkable +44.2% week-over-week surge that signals strong and growing demand in the market. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 36.2% and TACoS at 18.8% are areas we are actively optimizing, with campaign-level bid adjustments and keyword refinement already underway to improve efficiency as volume scales. CTR from our sponsored ads currently sits at 0.16%, and we are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform — including headline copy and ad imagery — to drive stronger click-through rates from our target audience. As these optimizations take effect, we expect to see both ACOS and ad CTR move in a more favorable direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With £772 in ad spend generating £2,134 in PPC revenue at a 2.76x ROAS, the paid channel is delivering solid returns while contributing meaningfully to overall growth. We are proactively refining our campaign structure — pausing lower-performing keywords and reallocating budget toward top-converting search terms — to improve spend efficiency and protect margin as we continue to scale. This approach ensures we are sustaining our competitive position in the UK market while progressively improving the return on every pound invested in advertising.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue tightening keyword bids on underperforming ad groups and shift budget toward the highest-converting terms identified during Week 35 to bring ACOS closer to target. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be a priority this week, with new headline and ad image variations launched to improve sponsored ad CTR above the current 0.16%. We will also monitor daily performance closely following the strong Sunday peak to identify any emerging day-parting opportunities and ensure budget is allocated efficiently across the full week.
