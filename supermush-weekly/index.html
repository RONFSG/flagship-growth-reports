Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Total sales grew to $10,843, up +12.3% WoW from $9,652, powered by organic revenue rising +21.6% to $9,045. Daily Sleep Gummies 60 Count led at $4,061 (+16.2%) with a standout 56.4% conversion rate, while Daily Passion Gummies 60 Count climbed to $2,327 (+22.4%) and Daily Energy Gummies 60 Count reached $1,241 (+24.5%). Creatine Gummies 60 Count nearly doubled to $544 (+82.7%), and Wednesday, Nov 18 was the peak day at $1,824.
►
Metrics to Watch — PPC revenue eased to $1,797 (-18.7%) while ad spend rose to $2,854 (prior $2,296), so paid traffic converted less efficiently this week — but organic more than made up for it, growing to $9,045 (+21.6%). Daily Passion Gummies 10 Count slipped to $706 (-18.2%) as conversion dipped to 8.2% (-23.0%), even though sessions grew +9.8% to 994, so shoppers are still finding it. We are tightening ad targeting on the 10 Count to turn that traffic back into orders.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — ROAS came in at 0.63x (prior 0.96x) and TACoS at 26.3% (prior 23.79%), with ACOS at 158.8%, a 0.69% ad CTR and a 7.01% ad conversion rate. Spend was held higher to keep the brand visible while organic sales climbed +21.6%, and we are now correcting efficiency by cutting non-converting search terms and lowering bids on high-ACOS campaigns. The goal is to bring ROAS back above 0.96x and pull TACoS back under 23.79%.
►
Actions This Week — Reallocate ad budget toward Daily Sleep Gummies 60 Count, whose 56.4% conversion makes it the most efficient place to spend. Lower bids and add negative keywords on campaigns above the 158.8% ACOS, starting with Daily Passion Gummies 10 Count, to lift ROAS back toward 0.96x. Test new ad creative to raise the 0.69% ad CTR on Daily Passion Gummies 60 Count and Daily Energy Gummies 60 Count, whose conversion jumped +60.1% and +60.0%.
