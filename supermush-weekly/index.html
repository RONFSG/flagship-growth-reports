Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush delivered a strong Week 38, generating $15,299 in total sales across 578 units sold — a impressive +22.6% week-over-week increase that signals real momentum heading into Q4. Monday September 14 was the peak sales day, and a conversion rate of 7.32% confirms that shoppers who found the product were highly motivated to purchase.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 149.2% and TACoS at 53.2% reflect the aggressive ad investment driving that +22.6% growth, and both metrics are actively being optimized as we refine targeting and shift budget toward higher-performing campaigns. Ad CTR of 0.52% represents a clear opportunity, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve click volume from qualified shoppers. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both ACOS and CTR to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $8,133 in ad spend against $5,449 in PPC revenue, we are operating in an investment phase where spend is being used strategically to drive visibility and capture market share during a period of strong sales growth. We are actively reviewing campaign-level performance to pull back on inefficient keywords and reallocate budget toward placements with stronger return, protecting overall unit economics while sustaining the upward sales trajectory. These adjustments are designed to progressively improve ROAS from its current 0.67x baseline as we move through Q4.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level audit across Sponsored Product campaigns to pause or reduce bids on terms contributing to high ACOS without meaningful conversion volume, redirecting that budget toward top-converting targets. Ad creative testing will be prioritized — specifically rotating Sponsored Product headline copy and ad imagery to identify combinations that lift ad CTR above the current 0.52%. We will also set a week-over-week ACOS improvement benchmark for Week 39 to track the direct impact of these bid and creative optimizations.
