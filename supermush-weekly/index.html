Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush closed Week 37 with $12,483 in total sales and 498 units sold, anchored by a strong peak on Saturday, September 12. CVR held at a healthy 6.28%, reflecting solid buyer intent once shoppers reach the listing. The week maintained steady volume even as overall sales dipped modestly, signaling a resilient customer base heading into fall.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 143.7% and TACoS at 42.2% reflect the current investment level in paid visibility, and both are being actively managed through ongoing bid and keyword optimizations. ROAS at 0.70x and the -2.5% WoW change are expected areas of focus as we refine spend allocation toward higher-converting segments. As inefficient targeting is trimmed and budget is redirected to top-performing campaigns, we expect these ratios to improve meaningfully in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $5,262 in ad spend driving $3,662 in PPC revenue, we are actively working to close the efficiency gap by prioritizing campaigns with proven conversion data and reducing investment in underperforming placements. Ad CTR at 0.65% presents a clear opportunity to improve top-of-funnel performance through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand creative testing, including headline variations and ad imagery within the ad platform. These targeted adjustments are designed to improve qualified click volume and bring ROAS progressively closer to a profitable threshold while maintaining our presence in key category placements.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve ad CTR above the current 0.65% baseline. Bid adjustments will be applied to deprioritize keywords with high spend and low conversion contribution, with the goal of pulling ACOS down from 143.7% toward a more efficient range. We will also identify the highest-CVR traffic segments from Week 37 and increase budget allocation there to capitalize on the 6.28% conversion rate where it matters most.
