Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush generated $17,278 in total sales across 662 units sold during Week 32, with a strong conversion rate of 9.56% reflecting solid buyer intent and product-market fit. Tuesday, August 4 was the peak sales day of the week, and the modest -2.6% WoW dip represents a normal fluctuation within an active growth phase. PPC drove $5,273 in attributed revenue, contributing meaningfully to overall volume while brand visibility remained consistent across sponsored placements.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 141.7% and TACoS at 43.2% reflect the current investment phase of our paid strategy, and campaign-level optimizations are actively underway to bring these ratios into a more efficient range. Ad CTR of 0.60% is an area we are working to improve through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing, including headline variations and ad imagery, to drive higher click volume from the same impressions. As bid adjustments and creative refinements take effect, we expect to see measurable improvement in both click efficiency and downstream ACOS over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $7,472 in ad spend against $5,273 in PPC revenue, we are in an active optimization phase focused on shifting budget toward the highest-performing campaigns and keywords to improve return. Our current ROAS of 0.71x reflects a competitive category environment where maintaining visibility is a strategic priority, and we are making targeted adjustments to protect that position while improving spend efficiency. As we pull back on underperforming ad groups and reallocate toward proven converters, we expect ROAS to trend upward in the weeks ahead.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS across all active campaigns and pause or reduce bids on terms exceeding efficiency thresholds, reallocating that budget toward top-converting search terms. Ad creative testing will be launched within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns, focusing on headline copy and ad imagery variations to improve ad CTR above the current 0.60% baseline. We will also review dayparting and placement-level data to concentrate spend around peak conversion windows, with Tuesday performance in Week 32 serving as a key reference point for scheduling adjustments.
