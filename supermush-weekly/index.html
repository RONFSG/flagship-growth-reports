Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush grew total sales +9.9% WoW to $9,948 for Week 46 on 472 units, powered by organic revenue climbing +22.1% to $7,922. The Daily Sleep Gummies 60 Count led at $3,651 (+15.6%) with an excellent 61.1% conversion rate, Daily Passion Gummies 60 Count rose to $2,015 (+20.0%), and Daily Energy Gummies 60 Count jumped to $1,142 (+60.9%). Monday, Nov 9 was the peak day at $1,821.
►
Metrics to Watch — Advertising efficiency is the key area to tighten: ACOS reached 113.4% and ROAS moved to 0.88x (prior 1.25x), with PPC revenue at $2,026 (-21.0%). Friday, Nov 13 was the softest day at $814, and the Daily Sleep Gummies 28 Count eased to $597 (-15.9%).
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $2,297 (prior $2,058) generated $2,026 in PPC revenue, with TACoS at 23.1% (prior 22.74%). Ad CTR was 0.46% and ad conversion was 7.09%, so the opportunity is to shift spend toward the high-converting 60 Count gummies that are already winning organically.
►
Actions This Week — We will cut bids on search terms that spend without converting and reallocate budget to the Daily Sleep, Passion and Energy 60 Count campaigns to bring ACOS well below 113.4%. We will also test new Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.46% as we head into the holiday season.
