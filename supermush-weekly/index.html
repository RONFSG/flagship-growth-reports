Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush moved 545 units and generated $14,293 in total sales during Week 34, with Wednesday August 19 standing out as the strongest sales day of the period. Conversion rate held at a strong 9.25%, reflecting solid shopper intent and listing quality. These fundamentals provide a healthy foundation as we work to improve ad efficiency and scale profitable revenue in the weeks ahead.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 135.1% and TACoS at 45.4% reflect an aggressive ad investment phase, and we are actively working to bring spend in line with return targets as campaign optimizations take effect. ROAS of 0.74x is an area of active focus, with bid adjustments and keyword pruning underway to improve paid revenue contribution. The week-over-week dip of 11.3% is being addressed through refined targeting and spend reallocation, and we expect to see improvement reflected in next week's numbers.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $6,490 in ad spend against $4,803 in PPC revenue, we are in an optimization cycle focused on improving return without sacrificing category visibility or ranking momentum. We are identifying underperforming keywords and shifting budget toward terms with stronger conversion signals to drive a more efficient ROAS. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand placements is also underway to improve ad CTR beyond its current 0.64% and increase the revenue generated per dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords exceeding ACOS thresholds and reallocate that budget toward top-converting search terms to improve ROAS and bring TACoS closer to target. Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations will be tested this week to lift ad CTR from 0.64% and drive more efficient traffic. We will also review dayparting data around the Wednesday August 19 peak to identify scheduling opportunities and replicate that performance pattern through the remainder of the month.
