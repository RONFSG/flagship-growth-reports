Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush posted $16,119 in total sales for Week 33, with 627 units sold and a solid week-over-week gain of +3.1%, reflecting continued momentum heading into mid-August. Wednesday, August 12 was the peak sales day, and a CVR of 11.30% confirms that shoppers landing on the listing are converting at a strong rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 126.6% and TACoS at 55.0% reflect the current investment level in paid visibility, and both are being actively monitored as we work to improve the return on each ad dollar. ROAS of 0.79x tells us PPC revenue of $7,001 is not yet covering the $8,861 in ad spend, and we are focused on shifting that balance by tightening keyword targeting and reallocating budget toward higher-efficiency campaigns. As these optimizations take hold, we expect to see ACOS and TACoS move in a more favorable direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $8,861 in ad spend against $16,119 in total sales, we are operating at an elevated investment rate that is consistent with an active growth and visibility phase in a competitive category. We are proactively reviewing campaign structures to pull back on underperforming keywords while protecting share on high-converting terms that are driving the 11.30% CVR. These adjustments are designed to improve efficiency while sustaining the upward sales trajectory we saw with the +3.1% WoW gain.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level performance to identify and pause or reduce bids on terms with high spend and low conversion, redirecting that budget toward proven converters. On the ad creative side, we will test Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.57%, which will help lower cost-per-click over time and improve overall ROAS. We will also set clear efficiency targets for Week 34 to track progress against ACOS and TACoS benchmarks.
