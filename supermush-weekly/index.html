Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush delivered $10,258 in total sales across 459 units sold during Week 44, with Sunday October 25 standing out as the peak revenue day for the period. CVR held strong at 7.47%, reflecting solid shopper intent and product-market fit when customers are reaching the listing. The foundation of customer conversion remains healthy, giving us a strong base to build on as we sharpen ad efficiency heading into the final stretch of Q4.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 169.8% and TACoS at 36.4% reflect an aggressive spend posture that we are actively right-sizing through bid adjustments and campaign restructuring this week. ROAS at 0.59x is an area of focused optimization, and as we pull back on underperforming ad groups and reallocate budget toward higher-converting targets, we expect measurable improvement in return efficiency. The WoW dip of 11.4% is a normal fluctuation we are monitoring closely as campaign optimizations take hold.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $3,738 in ad spend generating $2,202 in PPC revenue, we are actively working to close the gap between spend and attributed ad revenue through tighter keyword targeting and negative keyword expansion. We are making strategic adjustments to protect SuperMush's category positioning while improving cost efficiency, particularly on broad and auto campaigns where spend tends to run ahead of returns. These refinements are designed to sustain visibility and market share while improving the overall contribution of paid traffic.
►
Actions This Week — We will reduce bids on high-spend, low-converting keywords and expand negative keyword lists to filter irrelevant traffic and improve overall ACOS. Ad creative will be tested within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand headline and imagery variations — to improve ad CTR from its current 0.57% and drive more qualified clicks. Budget will be reallocated toward top-converting campaigns to maximize efficiency and position SuperMush for a strong performance week ahead of the Q4 holiday push.
