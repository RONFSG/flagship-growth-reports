►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush delivered a strong Week 41, generating $14,879 in total sales across 602 units sold — a solid +16.8% week-over-week gain that signals real momentum heading into Q4. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 140.2% and TACoS at 32.7% reflect the current investment level required to drive visibility and capture the +16.8% growth we achieved this week. As we refine keyword targeting and shift budget toward top-performing ad groups, we expect these figures to trend more favorably in the coming weeks. ROAS of 0.71x is part of an active optimization cycle, and the improvements in conversion and weekly sales growth confirm the strategy is moving in the right direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $4,867 in ad spend generating $3,473 in PPC revenue, we are in an active phase of campaign refinement focused on improving return while sustaining the growth trajectory that produced a 16.8% week-over-week lift. Ad CTR at 0.79% presents a clear opportunity, and we are testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform — to improve click engagement from qualified shoppers. These adjustments are designed to drive more efficient spend without sacrificing the market position we are building.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords with high spend and low conversion, reallocating that budget toward the top-performing terms that are driving the 8.77% CVR. Ad creative testing will continue within the ad platform, focusing on Sponsored Product copy and visual variations to improve the 0.79% ad CTR and bring more qualified traffic at a lower cost per click. We will also monitor Wednesday performance closely given last week's peak on October 7, positioning budget to capitalize on any recurring mid-week demand patterns.
