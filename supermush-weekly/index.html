►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush brought in $12,479 in total sales across 481 units sold during Week 39, with a strong CVR of 6.00% demonstrating that shoppers who engage with our listings are converting at a healthy rate. Wednesday, September 23 was the peak sales day, anchoring a solid mid-week performance. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 118.7% and TACoS at 26.8% reflect an area of active focus, and campaign-level optimizations are underway to improve the ratio of spend to attributed revenue. Ad CTR of 0.92% is an opportunity we are directly addressing through ad creative testing — including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations — to improve click quality and volume. As these adjustments take hold, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend more favorably in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $3,347 in ad spend delivering a 0.84x ROAS this week, we are actively refining our keyword targeting and bid strategy to shift spend toward higher-converting placements. This includes pulling back on inefficient terms and doubling down on those driving the 6.00% CVR we are already seeing. These proactive adjustments are designed to strengthen efficiency while maintaining our competitive presence in key search positions.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level performance to pause or reduce bids on low-converting terms that are driving up ACOS, while protecting spend on placements tied to the strong 6.00% CVR. Ad creative testing across Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad formats will be prioritized to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.92% and drive more qualified traffic. We will also set daily spend pacing guardrails to keep TACoS on a downward trajectory heading into Week 40.
