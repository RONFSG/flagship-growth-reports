Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — SuperMush delivered $9,828 in total sales for Week 45, up 1.4% from $9,693 the prior week, on 481 units. PPC revenue grew 9.9% to $2,507, with the Sleep Gummies 28 Count climbing to $738 (+17.3%) and the Energy Gummies 60 Count rising to $963 (+6.3%). Sunday, Nov 1 was the peak day at $1,683.
►
Metrics to Watch — The Sleep Gummies 60 Count eased to $3,396 (-7.3%) and the Passion Gummies 10 Count to $888 (-12.8%), as sessions softened across top products, including 211 sessions (-23.8%) on the Sleep 60 Count. Conversion held up well, with the Sleep 60 Count at 55.5% CVR (+29.1%), so we are focused on rebuilding traffic to these listings and lifting organic sales of $7,321 (-1.2% WoW).
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend came down to $2,058 from $3,734 while PPC revenue grew to $2,507, doubling ROAS to 1.22x from 0.61x and bringing TACoS down to 20.9% from 38.53%. ACOS is still elevated at 82.1% on a 0.65% ad CTR and 8.81% ad CVR, so we will keep tightening spend toward converting terms.
►
Actions This Week — We will continue trimming bids on high-ACOS targets and reallocate budget to search terms that convert, with the goal of bringing ACOS below 82.1%. We will also direct more ad traffic to the Sleep Gummies 60 Count and Passion Gummies 60 Count, where CVR is 55.5% and 17.3%, and test new Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.65%.
