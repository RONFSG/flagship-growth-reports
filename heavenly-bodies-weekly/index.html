We are adjusting bids and creative elements to drive higher qualified traffic while keeping TACoS in a healthy range.

Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords with high spend and low conversion to bring ACOS closer to target while protecting efficient placements. With Q4 demand building, we will ensure budget is allocated to top-performing campaigns so we are positioned to capture the increase in category search volume.
