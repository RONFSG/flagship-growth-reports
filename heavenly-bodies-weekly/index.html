Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Heavenly Bodies brought in $9,126 in total sales across 165 units sold during Week 44, with PPC contributing $1,675 in revenue against a $685 ad spend. TACoS held at a healthy 7.5%, reflecting strong organic sales momentum supporting the overall business, and ROAS came in at 2.45x demonstrating that paid campaigns are continuing to drive meaningful returns.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 40.9% and a CVR of 3.34% are both active areas of optimization as we work to improve conversion efficiency across campaigns. The WoW dip of 11.2% reflects normal weekly variability and is not a structural concern — we are monitoring trends closely heading into the next period. With ad spend being reviewed and refined at the keyword and targeting level, we expect both ACOS and CVR to trend in a more favorable direction this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $685 in ad spend generating $1,675 in PPC revenue, the campaigns are producing a positive return while we continue refining efficiency. Ad CTR of 0.35% signals an opportunity to sharpen ad creative — we are testing Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations within the ad platform to increase click-through and drive stronger conversion downstream. These proactive adjustments are designed to protect our category position while improving the overall return on every dollar invested.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on underperforming keywords contributing to elevated ACOS and redirect that budget toward top-converting targets. Ad creative testing on Sponsored Product headlines and ad copy will be prioritized to lift ad CTR above the current 0.35% baseline. We will also monitor Tuesday performance closely, as Oct 27 was the peak day last week, to identify any day-of-week patterns worth capitalizing on in campaign scheduling.
