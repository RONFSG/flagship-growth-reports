Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Heavenly Bodies closed Week 42 with $10,361 in total sales across 188 units sold, with Thursday October 15 standing out as the peak sales day of the week. PPC contributed $2,214 in revenue against $840 in ad spend, delivering a solid 2.64x ROAS that reflects continued strength in our paid channel performance. TACoS came in at 8.1%, indicating that the majority of sales volume is being driven organically, which is a healthy sign for the brand's overall visibility and momentum.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 37.9% reflects an area of active focus as we work through campaign-level optimizations to improve keyword efficiency and bid allocation. Ad CTR at 0.38% is an ongoing opportunity we are addressing through Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative testing to improve click-through performance at the ad level. As these refinements take hold, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to trend in a more favorable direction in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $840 deployed and 2.64x ROAS, our ad spend is generating meaningful returns while we continue fine-tuning campaign structure to improve efficiency across placements. We are proactively reviewing keyword targeting and match types to concentrate spend on the highest-converting terms and reduce waste in lower-performing segments. These adjustments are designed to strengthen our cost-per-sale metrics while protecting organic rank and category positioning heading into the back half of Q4.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a bid-level review across Sponsored Product campaigns, reducing spend on keywords with high ACOS and reallocating budget toward top-converting search terms to improve overall efficiency. Ad creative variations within the ad platform will be tested to lift ad CTR from its current 0.38% baseline. We will also monitor daily performance closely around any mid-week demand signals to capitalize on high-velocity days similar to the Thursday October 15 peak.
