Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Heavenly Bodies closed Week 40 with $17,579 in total sales and 318 units sold, anchored by a strong peak on Friday, October 2. PPC drove $2,646 in revenue against $1,101 in ad spend, and a TACoS of 6.3% reflects a healthy relationship between advertising investment and total business revenue. CVR held solid at 3.99%, confirming that shoppers arriving at the listing are converting at a strong rate.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 41.6% and a CTR of 0.45% on our sponsored ads are both areas we are actively optimizing through campaign-level bid adjustments and ad creative refinements. The -5.4% WoW dip in sales is a normal week-over-week fluctuation and does not indicate a structural shift in demand. As campaign optimizations take hold and ad creative continues to be refined, we expect both efficiency and click volume to trend in the right direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With ROAS at 2.40x and $1,101 deployed this week, we are making proactive adjustments to shift budget toward the highest-converting keywords and ad placements. We are reviewing underperforming targeting segments to reallocate spend where it generates the strongest return, while maintaining visibility in key category positions. These ongoing refinements are designed to improve ACOS progressively while sustaining the sales momentum built over recent weeks.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations, including updated ad copy and imagery within the ad platform, to improve ad CTR above the current 0.45% benchmark. Bid strategies will be tightened on lower-performing keywords to bring ACOS closer to target while protecting spend on top converters. We will also monitor Friday performance closely, as October 2 established a clear peak-day pattern worth capitalizing on with strategic dayparting adjustments.
