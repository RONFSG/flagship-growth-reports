►
Weekly Highlights — Week 38 delivered $20,319 in total sales across 371 units sold, marking a strong +16.6% week-over-week increase that signals growing momentum heading into fall. ►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 38.7% and a CTR of 0.36% on ad placements are both areas we are actively optimizing through ongoing campaign refinements. We are testing ad creative variations within the ad platform — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve click-through rates and drive more qualified traffic. As these adjustments take hold alongside continued bid optimization, we expect both ACOS and ad CTR to improve in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $1,094 in ad spend generating $2,830 in PPC revenue, we are producing a 2.59x ROAS, and TACoS sits at 5.4% relative to total sales — a sign that organic performance is carrying meaningful weight alongside paid efforts. We are proactively reallocating budget toward top-performing campaigns and pulling back on lower-efficiency placements to sharpen overall spend efficiency. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position and sustain the growth trajectory we saw this week.
►
Actions This Week — We will refine keyword bids on underperforming ad groups and redirect budget toward the placements driving the strongest conversion at the lowest ACOS. Ad creative testing — including Sponsored Product ad copy and visual asset variations — will continue within the ad platform to improve the 0.36% ad CTR. We will also monitor Thursday-level demand patterns throughout the week to identify opportunities to capitalize on any recurring mid-week traffic spikes.
