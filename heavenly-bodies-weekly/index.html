Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 40.5% and TACoS at 5.9% are areas we are actively optimizing as campaigns are refined for greater efficiency. ►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad CTR of 0.43% presents a clear opportunity to improve click volume through ad creative refinements, and we are testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand headline variations and ad imagery within the ad platform to drive stronger engagement. These proactive adjustments are designed to protect market position while steadily improving the ratio of spend to revenue.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit keyword-level ACOS, pausing or reducing bids on terms exceeding target thresholds while increasing investment in top-converting search terms to improve overall campaign efficiency. Ad creative testing will continue within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns, focusing on headline copy and ad image variations to lift ad CTR above the current 0.43%. We will also monitor daily pacing closely to capitalize on any repeat peak-day momentum similar to Friday's performance and ensure budget is available through the weekend.
