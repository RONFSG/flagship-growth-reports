Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Heavenly Bodies delivered $10,042 in total sales for Week 45, up 10.0% from $9,126 the prior week, on 184 units. The Tarot Deluxe Boxset led with $8,332 (+4.8%) as sessions grew 18.5% to 2770, while the Astrology Deluxe Boxset jumped 74.8% to $1,022. Tuesday, Nov 3 was the peak day at $1,829.
►
Metrics to Watch — Tarot Boxset CVR eased to 5.5% (-10.9%) even as traffic grew, and the Tarot & Astrology 2-pack CVR moved to 8.2% (-20.4%) on 46.6% more sessions. We are watching these conversion rates closely so the added traffic turns into more orders, while building on the Astrology Boxset's CVR gain to 4.6% (+38.6%).
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $608 (down from $683) generated $2,174 in PPC revenue (+29.8%), lifting ROAS to 3.57x from 2.45x at a 28.0% ACOS. TACoS improved to 6.1% from 7.49%, and organic sales grew 5.6% to $7,868 alongside a 0.37% ad CTR and 3.70% ad CVR.
►
Actions This Week — We will scale budget on Astrology Boxset campaigns to build on its 74.8% sales lift while holding ACOS near 28.0%. We will also refine Tarot Boxset targeting to convert its growing traffic more efficiently, and test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative to lift ad CTR above 0.37%.
