These proactive adjustments position us well to sustain growth heading into the fall selling season.

Actions This Week — We will conduct a bid review across Sponsored Product campaigns, pausing low-converting keywords and increasing investment in terms driving the strongest CVR, which currently stands at an excellent 6.90%.
