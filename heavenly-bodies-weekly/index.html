These proactive adjustments are designed to protect our competitive position in the category while improving per-dollar efficiency across all active campaigns.
►
Actions This Week — We will audit underperforming keywords and shift budget toward search terms delivering the strongest conversion rates to build on the 5.35% CVR baseline. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand campaigns will be prioritized to improve ad CTR above the current 0.34% and drive more qualified traffic. Bid adjustments on top-performing placements will be implemented to sustain ROAS momentum and reduce ACOS toward our target threshold.
