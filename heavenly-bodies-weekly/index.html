Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Total sales rose to $10,768 (+27.7% WoW) on 192 units, powered by organic revenue of $9,013 (+39.5% WoW). The Tarot Deluxe Boxset led at $8,816 (+28.2%) on 162 units (+29.6%) as its conversion climbed to 8.0% (+27.2%), and the Tarot & Astrology 2-pack grew to $1,066 (+85.7%) on sessions up +136.8%. Friday, Nov 20 was the peak day at $2,179.
►
Metrics to Watch — PPC revenue eased to $1,755 (-11.0%) as ad spend was pulled back to $577 (prior $748), a deliberate efficiency move while organic sales carried the growth. The Tarot 3-pack eased to $210 (-40.0%) on sessions down -19.4%, and Astrology Boxset sessions dipped -12.8%, yet its conversion improved to 3.7% (+22.9%) and lifted sales to $675 (+7.1%). We are focused on rebuilding traffic to the Astrology Boxset and the 3-pack bundles to add volume.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — ROAS improved to 3.04x from 2.64x and TACoS fell to 5.4% from 8.87%, so advertising is now a much smaller share of total sales. ACOS came in at 32.9% with a 0.36% ad CTR and a 3.33% ad conversion rate. Spend is being concentrated on the terms that drive the Tarot Boxset's conversion gains to keep ROAS above 3.04x.
►
Actions This Week — Shift budget toward the Tarot Deluxe Boxset's top-converting search terms to build on its 8.0% conversion while holding ACOS at or below 32.9%. Add dedicated campaigns for the Tarot & Astrology 2-pack to capture its +136.8% session growth. Test new ad creative on the Astrology Boxset to lift the 0.36% ad CTR and win back sessions.
