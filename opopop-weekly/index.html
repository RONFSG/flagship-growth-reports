Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 37 delivered $40,813 in total sales across 2,143 units sold, marking a strong +19.0% week-over-week increase that signals sustained momentum heading into fall. PPC drove $18,034 in revenue at a healthy 3.51x ROAS, and a 17.42% conversion rate reflects strong purchase intent from shoppers engaging with Opopop's ads and listings. Sunday, September 6 stood out as the peak day, anchoring a week of consistent performance across the board.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR at 0.50% and ACOS at 28.5% are both areas we are actively optimizing through more targeted keyword segmentation and bid refinements to improve click quality and cost efficiency. As underperforming ad placements are trimmed and spend is concentrated toward higher-converting terms, we expect both metrics to trend favorably in the coming week. TACoS at 12.6% remains a useful north star, and continued improvements to paid efficiency will help bring that figure down as organic contribution grows.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $5,138 in ad spend generated $18,034 in PPC revenue, reflecting a 3.51x ROAS that demonstrates strong return on invested media dollars. We are proactively refining campaign structure to ensure spend is concentrated on the highest-efficiency placements, particularly as competition around branded and category terms remains active in this period. These adjustments are designed to protect our market position and profitability while continuing to scale revenue week over week.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve ad CTR above the current 0.50% baseline and drive stronger top-of-funnel engagement. Bid optimization will focus on reducing ACOS by deprioritizing high-spend, low-conversion keywords and reallocating budget toward proven performers. We will monitor daily TACoS closely to validate that efficiency gains are compounding, with a target of pushing ROAS above 3.6x by end of week.
