Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Total sales climbed to $59,364, up +29.2% WoW from $45,935, on 2384 units. The Flavor-Wrapped Microwave Popcorn Kit led the way at $21,846 (+181.9%) on 493 units (+218.1%), with conversion nearly doubling to 17.2% (+98.5%). The Silicone Popcorn Popper also doubled to $9,429 (+99.4%), and the week finished strong with Saturday, Nov 21 as the peak day at $11,692.
►
Metrics to Watch — TACoS moved up to 11.4% from 7.85% because ad spend was deliberately scaled to $6,741 (prior $3,605) to capture growing demand — and it paid off, with organic revenue still rising +9.4% to $34,062 alongside paid. The Pop Cups Super Butter 6-Pack held sessions flat at 1802 (-0.4%), yet its conversion jumped to 28.6% (+21.1%), lifting sales to $7,054 (+11.5%). We are focused on adding more traffic to Pop Cups so its strong conversion turns into more volume.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — The larger budget drove PPC revenue to $25,302 (+71.1%) with an 18.44% ad conversion rate, a 0.98% ad CTR and ACOS at 26.6%. ROAS came in at 3.75x (prior 4.10x), a modest step down that is expected when spend nearly doubles into new traffic. We are now trimming low-converting search terms and shifting budget to the best-converting SKUs to bring ROAS back toward 4.10x while keeping volume.
►
Actions This Week — Keep scaling campaigns on the Flavor-Wrapped Microwave Popcorn Kit while its conversion holds at 17.2%, and protect its top-converting search terms with stronger bids. Raise bids on the Pop Cups Super Butter 6-Pack's best keywords to grow sessions beyond 1802 while its 28.6% conversion is strong. Cut wasted spend on low-converting terms to pull TACoS back toward 7.85% and ROAS back toward 4.10x.
