►

►

Ad CTR of 0.75% presents a clear opportunity to drive incremental volume, and we are actively testing Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve click-through performance within the ad platform. These creative optimizations are expected to lower cost-per-click efficiency and expand reach without requiring additional budget.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations focused on improving ad CTR beyond the current 0.75%, targeting high-intent keyword segments where conversion rates are strongest. We will also monitor daily pacing closely following Sunday's peak to ensure spend distribution is optimized across the full week.
