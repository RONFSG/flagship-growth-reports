Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Opopop closed Week 33 with $43,296 in total sales and 2,230 units sold, marking a solid +4.0% week-over-week gain and continuing the brand's positive growth trajectory. PPC drove $23,901 in revenue against $3,764 in ad spend, delivering a strong 6.35x ROAS that reflects well-performing campaigns. Saturday August 15 was the peak sales day, capping the week on a high note and signaling strong weekend demand.
►
Metrics to Watch — Ad CTR came in at 0.74%, and as we continue refining ad creative — including Sponsored Product imagery and headline variations — we expect to see that click volume grow in the weeks ahead. ACOS at 15.7% is within an actionable range, and with ongoing bid optimizations and tighter keyword targeting, we anticipate continued improvement toward our efficiency targets. CVR at 19.99% is a genuine bright spot and confirms that shoppers who engage with the ads are converting at a healthy rate.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — TACoS at 8.7% reflects that ad spend is well-calibrated relative to total revenue, and we are continuing to fine-tune campaign allocation to protect that efficiency as we scale. We are proactively reviewing keyword-level performance to shift budget toward the highest-converting terms and reduce waste on lower-performing placements. These adjustments are designed to sustain the 6.35x ROAS while creating additional headroom for growth in coming weeks.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including updated ad copy and imagery — to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.74% baseline and drive incremental click volume. Bid adjustments will be applied across underperforming keywords to bring ACOS closer to target while protecting strong placements that are contributing to the $23,901 in PPC revenue. We will also monitor weekend pacing closely given Saturday's peak performance, ensuring campaign budgets are structured to fully capture elevated demand heading into Week 34.
