►

Conversion rate hit a strong 13.62%, confirming that shoppers landing on the listing are highly engaged and ready to buy.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 38.3% and TACoS at 20.5% are areas we are actively working to bring into tighter range as campaign structure matures and spend becomes more targeted. Ad CTR at 0.51% is an opportunity we are addressing through ad creative testing — including Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand headline and imagery variations within the ad platform — to drive higher qualified click volume. As these optimizations take hold, we expect both click efficiency and conversion economics to improve in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $7,018 in ad spend generating $18,311 in PPC revenue, the campaign is producing meaningful returns while we continue refining efficiency across placements and match types. We are proactively reallocating budget away from underperforming keywords and toward high-converting targets to strengthen ROAS beyond the current 2.61x. These adjustments are designed to protect our competitive market position while improving the overall spend-to-revenue ratio as we move deeper into the fall season.
►
Actions This Week — We will test new Sponsored Product and Sponsored Brand ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad imagery — to improve ad CTR above the current 0.51% and drive more qualified traffic. Keyword-level bid optimization will continue with a focus on reducing spend on high-ACOS terms to bring ACOS closer to target range. We will also monitor Saturday performance closely given its status as last week's peak day to ensure budget is fully capitalized on weekend demand patterns.
