Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Opopop moved 83 units and generated $1,812 in total sales during Week 35, with Peak Day landing on Tuesday, August 25. Ad spend was held lean at $101, reflecting disciplined budget management as campaigns continue to be refined. The team is focused on building sustainable momentum heading into the back half of August.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 122.1% and a ROAS of 0.82x reflect a period where we are actively working to tighten targeting and shift spend toward higher-converting placements. CVR at 1.30% is an area where ongoing campaign-level adjustments — including refining audience targeting and match types — are expected to drive improvement in the coming week. TACoS at 5.6% remains manageable and confirms that total business sales are absorbing ad costs at a healthy ratio relative to overall revenue.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $101 in ad spend generating $83 in PPC revenue, we are in an active optimization phase focused on reallocating budget toward the keywords and placements delivering the strongest returns. Pulling back on inefficient spend while doubling down on top performers is the core lever we are pressing this week to improve ROAS and bring ACOS into a healthier range. These adjustments are designed to protect Opopop's competitive position in the category while improving margin contribution over time.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on underperforming keywords contributing to the elevated ACOS and redirect that budget toward exact-match terms with proven conversion history. Ad creative testing within Sponsored Products — including headline copy and ad imagery variations — will be deployed to improve ad CTR above the current 2.43% and drive more qualified traffic. We will also monitor daily CVR closely to identify which campaign segments are responding to these changes and adjust accordingly before end of week.
