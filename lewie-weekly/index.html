►

As these adjustments take hold, we expect both ACOS and CTR to trend in a more favorable direction over the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $496 in ad spend against $505 in total sales, we are in an investment phase where ad dollars are actively building visibility and sales velocity in a competitive category. Our ROAS of 0.63x reflects this growth-focused posture, and we are making targeted adjustments to shift budget toward higher-converting keywords and pause inefficient spend. These moves are designed to progressively improve return while protecting the ranking and market position we are building.
►
Actions This Week — We will conduct a keyword-level review to identify and reduce bids on low-converting search terms, redirecting that budget toward terms driving the strongest CVR. We will also test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.42% and drive more qualified traffic. Finally, we will monitor daily performance closely off the Sunday peak to identify any emerging high-volume days and adjust dayparting or bid schedules accordingly.
