Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Lewie moved 22 units and generated $295 in total sales during Week 39, with PPC contributing $115 of that revenue. Tuesday September 22 was the peak sales day, showing that mid-week momentum is building and providing a strong targeting signal for campaign scheduling.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 334.2% and TACoS at 130.3% reflect a period of active campaign scaling, and we are focused on tightening keyword targeting and shifting budget toward the highest-converting placements. CVR of 4.76% is a genuine bright spot, confirming that shoppers who do click are converting at a healthy rate. As we continue refining bid strategies and reducing spend on lower-performing terms, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend meaningfully lower this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $384 in ad spend against $115 in PPC revenue, we are actively reallocating budget away from inefficient keywords and toward placements that are driving the 4.76% CVR we are already seeing. Ad CTR of 0.29% indicates an opportunity to improve ad creative performance through Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variation testing within the ad platform, which we are prioritizing this week. These adjustments are designed to bring ROAS up from 0.30x while protecting Lewie's visibility and category positioning.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or significantly reduce bids on keywords with high spend and zero or near-zero conversion, redirecting that budget toward terms supporting the strong 4.76% CVR. Sponsored Product ad creative will be tested with headline and copy variations to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.29%. Campaign scheduling will be adjusted to weight spend toward Tuesday and other peak-performing days based on the Week 39 sales pattern.
