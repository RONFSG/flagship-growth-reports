Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Lewie posted $600 in total sales across 46 units sold this week, with a strong week-over-week gain of +233.3% signaling meaningful momentum building in the market. Tuesday July 28 was the peak sales day, and PPC drove $405 of total revenue, showing that paid campaigns are actively contributing to growth. The team is capitalizing on this upward trajectory heading into August.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 168.8% and TACoS at 113.9% reflect the investment phase of scaling campaigns, and both are being actively monitored as we tighten targeting and shift budget toward higher-converting ad groups. CVR at 6.09% is a genuine bright spot, confirming that shoppers who engage are converting at a healthy rate. As we continue refining keyword bids and reducing spend on lower-performing placements, we expect these efficiency metrics to trend in the right direction this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — Ad spend of $683 against $600 in total sales reflects an aggressive push to build visibility and capture market share during a period of strong growth momentum, and we are actively making adjustments to close that gap. ROAS at 0.59x is the key lever we are optimizing, with a focus on reallocating budget toward campaigns and keywords delivering the strongest conversion return. Ad CTR at 0.74% presents an opportunity to improve click volume efficiency by testing Sponsored Product ad creative variations, including headline copy and ad imagery within the ad platform, to drive higher-quality traffic at lower cost.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords with high spend and zero or low conversions to bring ACOS and TACoS closer to target thresholds. Ad creative testing for Sponsored Product campaigns will be prioritized, with new headline and imagery variations launched to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.74%. We will also increase budget allocation to the top-performing ad groups that contributed most to the 6.09% CVR, reinforcing what is already working and scaling it efficiently.
