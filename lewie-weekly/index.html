►

►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 199.5% and TACoS at 88.4% reflect a period where ad spend is outpacing returns, and we are actively working through campaign refinements to bring these ratios into better balance. As targeting optimizations take hold and lower-performing keywords are deprioritized, we expect both ACOS and TACoS to trend in a more favorable direction this week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $349 in ad spend against $395 in total sales, we are in an active optimization phase where tightening campaign efficiency is the clear priority. We are reviewing keyword bids and match types to shift budget toward terms delivering the strongest return, with the goal of improving ROAS beyond the current 0.50x. These adjustments are designed to protect Lewie's market presence while steadily improving the relationship between spend and revenue over the coming weeks.
►
Actions This Week — We will pause or reduce bids on keywords contributing to high ACOS with low conversion volume, reallocating that budget toward the best-performing terms driving sales. Ad creative will be reviewed within the ad platform — including Sponsored Product ad copy and headline variations — to improve ad CTR from its current 0.62% and attract more qualified clicks. We will also monitor Thursday performance closely given its strength in Week 33, looking to capture any repeat mid-week demand patterns.
