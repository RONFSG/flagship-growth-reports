Ad Spend Efficiency — $583 in ad spend against $485 in total sales reflects an aggressive push to build visibility and category presence, and we are making deliberate adjustments to sharpen efficiency without pulling back on hard-won momentum. These proactive shifts are designed to improve ROAS and bring TACoS into a healthier range as campaign learning continues.
