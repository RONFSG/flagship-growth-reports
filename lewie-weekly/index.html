Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Lewie closed Week 42 with $185 in total sales across 14 units sold, with Saturday October 17 standing out as the peak sales day of the week. PPC contributed $85 in revenue, and the team is actively managing spend levels in response to the current post-tentpole slowdown across FSG brands. This period of recalibration is setting the foundation for a stronger, more efficient ad structure heading into the weeks ahead.
►
Metrics to Watch — ACOS at 364.5% and TACoS at 167.5% reflect the current environment where ad spend is intentionally elevated relative to a softer sales volume, and both metrics are expected to improve as the market normalizes and spend is right-sized. CVR at 1.67% shows that shoppers who do engage are converting at a reasonable rate, giving us a solid base to build on. As campaign optimizations take effect and spend is reallocated toward higher-performing placements, we expect meaningful efficiency gains in the coming week.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $310 in ad spend against $85 in PPC revenue, we are actively pulling back on underperforming spend and reallocating budget toward the placements and keywords delivering the strongest return. A ROAS of 0.27x and ad CTR of 0.31% signal clear opportunities to sharpen ad creative targeting and messaging within the Sponsored Products platform to drive more qualified clicks. These adjustments are already in motion and are designed to improve efficiency while maintaining Lewie's visibility during this transitional period.
►
Actions This Week — Pause or significantly reduce bids on keywords with high spend and zero conversions over the past 14 days to bring ACOS and TACoS in line with targets. Test new Sponsored Product ad creative variations — including headline copy and ad-level imagery — to improve ad CTR beyond the current 0.31% and attract higher-intent shoppers. Continue monitoring daily performance with a focus on replicating Saturday's peak-day conditions earlier in the week to smooth out the sales curve.
